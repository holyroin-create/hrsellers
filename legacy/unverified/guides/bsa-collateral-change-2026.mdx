Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement — the contract every seller accepted and almost no seller has read — was amended effective August 24, 2026, per this month's seller-news reporting. Two clauses matter: rights and obligations under the agreement cannot be transferred, and **future Amazon sales revenue cannot be pledged as collateral**.

## Who actually feels this

The collateral clause lands on a specific and growing group: sellers funding inventory through revenue-based financing or cash advances secured against Amazon payouts. If your facility is structured that way, the security interest underneath it now sits in tension with the platform contract. That is a conversation with your lender and, at meaningful sums, a lawyer — this week. The transfer clause matters at exit: acquisition structures routinely assign seller-account rights, and how brokers and buyers adapt is worth watching before you sign anything.

## What to do

Pull every financing document and check how it is secured. Ask lenders the direct question in writing. If you are mid-acquisition, put the clause in front of the deal attorney. And treat this as the reminder it is: the [payout timing guide](/guides/dd7-payout-reserve/) covers how much of your cash Amazon already controls on any given day — a number worth knowing before you borrow against it.
