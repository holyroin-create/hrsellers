The [holiday peak fee guide](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/) covers the window, the programs and the deadlines. This page does one thing. It runs the arithmetic per unit, so "peak fee unchanged" never gets mistaken for "unit cost unchanged."

## The formula

```
2025 peak unit cost = base fee (2025)  + peak uplift (tier)
2026 peak unit cost = [base fee (2026) + peak uplift (tier)] × 1.035
difference          = base fee change + 3.5% of (2026 base + uplift)
```

Amazon confirmed on July 7, 2026 that the per-tier uplift is unchanged from last peak and that the 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge applies on top of peak fees. The base fee rose on January 15, 2026 by an average of $0.08.

## Add the price-band check

The 2026 base fee depends on the selling price band: under $10, $10 to $50, over $50. A November promotion that takes a $52 item to $49 moves it down a band and lowers all three numbers; a price rise from $9.99 to $10.49 takes it out of Low-Price FBA rates and raises them. Run the worksheet at the price you will sell at during the window, not the list price.

## Where to get your inputs

`Seller Central > Reports > Fulfillment > Payments > Fee Preview` — export, filter to your Q4 SKUs; the fulfillment fee column already reflects 2026 base rates and, in the peak columns, the uplift.

`Seller Central > Growth > Profit Analytics` — per-product unit economics with fee lines separated, useful for checking the surcharge line against your worksheet.

**Run it on your own figures.** The [margin and break-even calculator](/tools/margin-calculator/) takes the 2026 peak cost from your worksheet and returns net margin and the maximum ACOS the product can carry in Q4. Nothing is stored and nothing leaves your browser.
