TikTok Shop's fee schedule looks like the cheapest place to sell in the United States. It is, on the line item everyone quotes, and that line item describes a minority of what selling there costs.

## The mandate that did not happen

In January 2026 TikTok told US sellers Seller Shipping was ending: existing sellers moving to TikTok-controlled logistics from February 25, hard cutoff March 31, and no independent option for anyone onboarding after February 9.

**On February 17 TikTok withdrew it.** The email to merchants said Seller Shipping remains unchanged, previously shared deadlines are not going into effect, and sellers should continue operating as usual. A TikTok spokesperson confirmed that all four options — Seller Shipping, Upgraded TikTok Shipping, Collections by TikTok and Fulfilled by TikTok — stay available. The reversal followed public pushback from beauty and enterprise brands and landed weeks after TikTok's US joint venture closed on January 22.

Articles published after February 17 that still say independent shipping ended on March 31 were either written before the reversal or never updated. Two things about the reversal deserve attention anyway. The email said "at this time" — TikTok's phrasing leaves the door open. And several accounts report that sellers who onboarded after February 9 are required to use TikTok Shop Logistics from their first order; if you are new, check your Seller Center fulfillment settings rather than assuming.

## The fee structure, and where guides still disagree

**The referral fee.** **8% for most non-food US categories since August 4, 2026**, up from 6%; select beauty and electronics sub-categories sit higher and food lower. TikTok has published no press release; the rate on your own settlement statement is the record. Calculated on item price before shipping and tax.

**Payment processing — three different answers:**

| Source | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Canopy, TikAdSuite | The referral fee is a **unified** fee combining commission and processing; no separate transaction fee for US sellers |
| Dashboardly, FeesCal | Referral **plus** 1.02%–3.78% processing — a structure that describes other TikTok Shop markets, not the US |
| Darkroom | 8% referral plus $0.30 per transaction plus 2.0% processing |

The unified account appears in two sources describing it explicitly as what trips up sellers coming from Shopify or Etsy. The blended figures appear in two others. **Check your own settlement statement**, which resolves it in one look — see the [reports guide](/guides/seller-central-reports/).

**Other charges on the Seller Center fee pages:**

- **Refund Administration Fee** — on a return, TikTok refunds the referral fee but keeps 20% of it, capped at $5 per SKU
- **Smart Promotion fee** — one source reports 3.5% where Smart Promo is enabled, described as required for full ad eligibility
- **Return shipping share** — at 20% if your Shop Performance Score is 4 or above, otherwise 50%
- **Withdrawal fees** — $0.05 flat to a bank account, 0.9% via PayPal
- **Hub Placement Fees** — from about $0.31 per unit (seller-tool reading of the FBT schedule) where you do not ship to multiple regional fulfillment centers
- **FBT Incident Fees** — for non-compliant inbound: wrong labeling, incorrect routing, quantity discrepancies

That last pair should look familiar. It is the same structure as Amazon's [inbound placement](/guides/inbound-placement-split-arithmetic/) and [defect fees](/guides/amazon-stopped-prepping/).

## The number that actually matters

**Creator commissions.** You set the rate. Ranges: near 0% to about 50%, with most sellers at 5% to 20%, and category medians of roughly 20% for beauty and supplements, 15% for fashion, 10% to 15% for home, 8% to 12% for electronics.

That is not an optional marketing expense. Reporting is consistent that TikTok's discovery-driven model **typically requires 10% to 20% in affiliate commissions to generate consistent traffic**, while Amazon delivers built-in buyer intent through search.

**Which produces the real comparison:**

| | Headline | Effective on an affiliate-driven sale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok Shop | 8% (6% before August 4, 2026) | 20%–30%+ |
| Amazon | 8%–15% | Referral plus advertising — see the [PPC guide](/guides/amazon-ppc-benchmarks/) |

One source's total platform-side cost on a $32 beauty SKU: **roughly 35% to 40% of GMV before cost of goods and ad spend.**

**The honest framing:** TikTok Shop is not cheaper than Amazon. It has a lower platform fee and a higher customer acquisition cost, and the total lands in a similar region. What differs is where the money goes and how predictable it is.

## Fulfillment

**FBT** — from about $3.58 per unit, **falling to about $2.86 at four or more units.** Another source gives $4.28 for lightweight single units; another gives pick-and-pack of $2.50 to $4.50 with storage at $0.75 to $1.50 per cubic foot.

**The basket-size effect is the actionable part.** Because per-unit fees fall with order size, bundle and basket optimization is a direct profit lever rather than a marketing preference. See the [bundling guide](/guides/bundles-and-multipacks/) — the same arithmetic, a different platform.

Free storage: 30 days in one source, 60 in another. Since june 16, 2025: FBT products automatically qualify for free shipping with no minimum order value.

**Settlement timing** is at roughly 15 days for standard sellers, 1 to 8 days for Star Shops, and up to 31 days for flagged accounts — which is a cash-cycle consideration alongside [DD+7](/guides/dd7-payout-reserve/) if you sell on both.

## Where it fits

**It works** for visually demonstrable products with impulse appeal, in categories where creators are active — beauty, supplements, fashion, home. Reporting projects $23.4 billion US GMV for 2026, described as roughly 70% year-on-year growth.

**It does not** for low average order value. On a $12 item, a $4.28 fulfillment fee is a third of revenue before anything else. One source is direct that small sellers with low-priced products are especially exposed.

**And it is a different business**, not a second listing. Amazon rewards a listing that answers a search; TikTok rewards content that creates demand. A seller who copies listings across and waits will conclude the platform does not work.

## What to do

**Confirm the shipping position in Seller Center first.** The published record contradicts itself on the most consequential operational question.

**Build the effective take-rate**, not the referral fee: referral, plus processing if separate, plus expected affiliate commission, plus allocated ad spend, as one percentage.

**Check your settlement statement** to resolve whether processing is bundled.

**Model at your actual average order value.** The fixed fulfillment component is what decides viability at low price points.

**Optimize basket size deliberately.** FBT per-unit fees fall with units per order.

**Negotiate creator commissions on volume and exclusivity** rather than running open marketplace campaigns at premium rates.

**Fix listing accuracy to reduce returns.** Reporting attributes most TikTok Shop returns to mismatched expectations — size charts, color inconsistency, and overpromising in video. Returns cost twice: the lost sale and the retained refund administration fee.

**Run it on your own figures.** The [margin and break-even calculator](/tools/margin-calculator/) takes your price, cost of goods, referral rate, fulfillment fee, returns and ad spend and returns net profit per unit, net margin and the maximum ACOS the product can carry.
