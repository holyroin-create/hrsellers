This site's editorial position is that a rate without a date is a rumour with a decimal point. This page is that position applied to a year.

Every entry links to the guide where the figures, the sources and the disagreements between sources are set out. Every 2026 date below was checked against the platform or regulator announcement in September 2026; verify anything you are about to act on.

## The timeline

### 2025 — the groundwork

**May 2, 2025** — De minimis eliminated for China and Hong Kong origin goods under Executive Order 14256. → [De minimis guide](/guides/de-minimis-suspension-2026/)

**August 29, 2025** — The $800 duty-free threshold suspended for **every** country of origin, closing the country-hopping route. The step most sellers missed, because 2025 coverage focused on China. → [De minimis guide](/guides/de-minimis-suspension-2026/)

**October 2025** — Amazon announces the 2026 US fee update, roughly three months ahead. → [Fee changes](/guides/amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026/) Also: 0-day handling becomes the practical fulfillment speed baseline for FBM sellers on Seller-Fulfilled Prime and premium shipping. → [Buy Box guide](/guides/buy-box-featured-offer/)

**September 2025** — European seller accounts migrate to the delivery-date based reserve. → [DD+7 guide](/guides/dd7-payout-reserve/)

### 2026 — the year it all landed

**January 1** — **Amazon ends US FBA prep and labeling services.** FNSKU labeling, poly-bagging and protective packaging move to the seller or a prep center at roughly $0.35 to $2.00 per unit. → [Prep guide](/guides/amazon-stopped-prepping/)

**January 15** — **US FBA fulfillment fees change.** An average $0.08 per unit, but with selling price becoming an input through three bands, and band variation from $0.05 to $0.51. Inbound defect fees rise from a $0.02–$0.07 range to $0.32–$5.72. → [Fee changes](/guides/amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026/) · [Price bands](/guides/price-tier-repricing-trap/)

**January 15** — **AWD fees rise**, with a West-region premium of about 19%. → [AWD guide](/guides/awd-vs-3pl-warehousing/)

**February 12** — **Review pooling across variations ends** for variations Amazon judges to have significant feature differences (Amazon announcement of January 7, 2026; rollout by category to May 31). → [Variations guide](/guides/variations-review-pooling/)

**March 12** — **DD+7 applies to remaining North American accounts.** Funds release seven days after delivery; order-to-bank of 14 to 27 days for FBA. → [DD+7 guide](/guides/dd7-payout-reserve/)

**March 31** — **Commingled inventory ends.** Brand Registry brand owners can use GS1 manufacturer barcodes as the tracking identifier; resellers must FNSKU every unit, with unlabeled inventory reported as classified defective. → [Barcodes guide](/guides/gs1-barcodes-fnsku/)

**February 28** — **The flat $80 / $160 / $200 postal duty option expires.** All postal shipments assessed ad valorem from March 1. → [Entry types](/guides/entry-type-11-vs-01/)

**April 17** — **A 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge** applies to FBA fulfillment fees in the US and Canada; MCF and Buy with Prime follow on May 2. Amazon's stated average: $0.17 per unit. → [Surcharge guide](/guides/fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026/)

**April 30** — All FBA-eligible dangerous goods qualify for the Partnered Carrier program (agency-reported; not yet verified against an Amazon announcement). → [Hazmat guide](/guides/hazmat-dangerous-goods/)

**June 24** — **The de minimis suspension is codified by regulation** through CBP interim final rules (19 CFR 10.151 and 145.31). → [De minimis guide](/guides/de-minimis-suspension-2026/)

**July 6** — **Seller Fulfilled Prime speed thresholds rise**: 40% one-day and 75% two-day page views for standard-size, announced May 26. → [SFP guide](/guides/seller-fulfilled-prime/)


**July 8** — **CPSC certificates must be filed electronically with CBP** via a Partner Government Agency message set, for importers of most regulated consumer products. → [Compliance guide](/guides/us-product-compliance/)

**July 24** — **Postal informal entry required** for international mail shipments — bond, 10-digit HTS code and duty before release; compliance date October 22. → [Entry types](/guides/entry-type-11-vs-01/)

**July 27** — **Amazon titles capped at 75 characters** outside media, with a new 125-character Item Highlights field; announced June 10. → [Listing optimization](/guides/listing-optimisation-a10/)

**July 30** — **FBA New Selection Program (2026)**: referral fee capped at 10% on the first 100 units and 5% on the next 100 of a new branded parent ASIN; enrollment through October 31. → [New Selection](/guides/new-asin-referral-incentive/)

**August 4** — **TikTok Shop raises the US referral fee from 6% to 8%** on most non-food categories, per trade reports. → [TikTok fee increase](/guides/tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026/)

**August 12** — The EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation applies, with expanded marketplace obligations to verify seller EPR compliance. → [Europe guide](/guides/expanding-eu-uk-compliance/)

**October 15 to January 14, 2027** — **Holiday peak fulfillment fees**, at roughly average $0.32 per unit, with the 3.5% surcharge on top. → [Peak fee guide](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/)

### Still ahead

**September 22, 2026** — CBP Entry Type 13 voluntary test begins for international mail shipments valued at $2,500 or less. → [Entry types guide](/guides/entry-type-11-vs-01/)

**July 1, 2027** — **Section 321 repealed for commercial shipments** under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of 2025. The current arrangement is a transition, not an endpoint. → [Entry types guide](/guides/entry-type-11-vs-01/)

## Where they interact

The list above reads as eleven independent events. Four pairs are not.

**January 1 and March 31.** Amazon stopped labeling inventory, then made labeling mandatory for resellers. The same sellers, the same direction, three months apart. → [Prep](/guides/amazon-stopped-prepping/) · [Barcodes](/guides/gs1-barcodes-fnsku/)

**January 15 and April 17.** The fulfillment fee changed, then a percentage was applied to it. Because price is now an input to the fee, one price change moves three numbers. → [Price bands](/guides/price-tier-repricing-trap/)

**July 1 and October 15.** The storage rate rose and the aged-inventory trigger tightened, and then the peak fulfillment fee opened — before Q4 storage does. → [Storage](/guides/fba-storage-four-layers/) · [Peak fee](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/)

**March 12 and everything upstream.** DD+7 widened the gap between spending and receiving in the year every other cost rose. → [Peak season](/guides/peak-season-cost-stack/)

## What to do with this

**Check which of these you have actually acted on.** Most sellers have adjusted for one or two and absorbed the rest without noticing.

**Rebuild unit economics once, properly**, rather than adjusting for each change as it is reported. The [margin calculator](/tools/margin-calculator/) and the [landed cost guide](/guides/landed-cost-fee-stack/) are the two inputs.

**Diarise the two ahead.** September 2026 and July 2027 are both known now.

**Treat every published rate card as dated.** The reason this page exists is that most guidance in this subject describes a moment without naming it — and after a year like this one, that makes it unusable.
