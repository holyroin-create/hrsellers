Three days. That is how long you have from today to get a Prime Big Deal Days submission in. The holiday deal calendar has dates now — and they are closer than they feel. **Prime Big Deal Days submissions close September 8.** Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday close October 20, with a reported **$50-per-deal saving for submissions made by September 5**. Ten days of discount window, then the ordinary deadline, then nothing.

## The math that comes before the date

The reporting around these deadlines carries the right warning: sellers who plan Q4 deals on last year's fee math will find deals that looked profitable running negative once peak fees activate — because this year the [peak surcharge](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/) stacks with the spring fuel-and-logistics surcharge. The full commitment math gets its own guide later in our Q4 series; the short version fits in one sentence: compute the extra units needed for the deal week to beat a normal week at your funded discount plus the deal fee plus October's fulfillment stack, and opt in only if your realistic forecast clears it.
