This page summarizes what ZonGuru publishes about its own product, prices and terms on zonguru.com, checked on November 8, 2026. We did not test the software, we do not rate it, and this site has no affiliate relationship with ZonGuru or any other vendor. ZonGuru's pricing page centers on one product, Helix, sized by how many listings it watches and how many it rewrites each month, with one-off credits sold separately. <C id="zonguru-09"/>

## What ZonGuru sells

ZonGuru describes Helix as "AI listing engineering for Amazon. Helix scores, fixes, and future-proofs your listings for how shoppers search now." <C id="zonguru-21"/>

The vendor's own FAQ separates its free and paid offers:

- **Free AI Readiness Score:** "a 0-to-100 read of how well a live listing answers" the questions Amazon's AI asks about it. It "scores the text on two layers, its meaning and its buyer Q&A coverage, and it reads nothing else." <C id="zonguru-18"/> <C id="zonguru-19"/>
- **Helix (paid):** "Helix engineers the listing and publishes it to Amazon, then keeps watching it." <C id="zonguru-20"/> The homepage says "Your managed listings are checked nightly for content changes and score drops." <C id="zonguru-23"/>

## Two units: managed listings and transformations

ZonGuru says "Two numbers size every plan": "how many of your listings Helix keeps under watch (managed listings), and how many it rebuilds each month (transformations)." <C id="zonguru-09"/> Credits are the currency for rebuilds: "One credit buys one transformation." <C id="zonguru-11"/>

## Connecting Seller Central

ZonGuru's FAQ answers whether you need to connect: "For a plan, yes. Seller Central connection is how Helix pulls, scores and publishes your catalog, and it needs an Amazon Professional selling plan." <C id="zonguru-16"/> Amazon says "A Professional selling account is $39.99/month + selling fees." <C id="zonguru-31"/> For public Selling Partner API apps, Amazon's developer documentation says "Reauthorization is required annually." <C id="zonguru-32"/> Amazon also says apps in its Selling Partner Appstore are "built on secure, Amazon-provided data pipelines." <C id="zonguru-33"/>

ZonGuru's privacy policy says that "integration with Amazon allows us access to your Amazon account information and business data," and that "ZonGuru does not sell your personal information." <C id="zonguru-29"/> <C id="zonguru-30"/>

## Responsibility for what gets published

ZonGuru's terms say: "You are solely responsible for reviewing, editing, and approving all AI Outputs before use." <C id="zonguru-27"/> For trademark and brand questions, see the [Brand Registry and trademark guide](/guides/brand-registry-trademark/). Amazon says its own Manage Your Experiments tool can be used "to A/B test product content." <C id="zonguru-34"/>

## Renewal, refunds and cancellation

- **Renewal:** the terms are with ZonGuru Holdings, Inc. and say "Your Subscription will continue indefinitely until canceled by you or terminated by us." <C id="zonguru-24"/>
- **Cancellation timing:** renewal happens "unless you cancel your account at least 10 days prior to the end of your then-current Subscription period." <C id="zonguru-25"/>
- **Refunds:** "New signups, both annual and monthly, are eligible for a full refund if the refund is requested within 7 days of the first transaction." <C id="zonguru-26"/>
- **Data after cancellation:** "Your managed-listing data, including monitoring history and score trends, is held for 60 days." <C id="zonguru-15"/>
- **Governing law:** Delaware. <C id="zonguru-28"/>
