This page summarizes what GETIDA publishes about its own FBA reimbursement service, fees and terms on getida.com, checked on November 8, 2026. We did not use or test the service, we do not rate it, and this site has no affiliate relationship with GETIDA or any other vendor. GETIDA charges a share of the FBA reimbursements it recovers, and the sections below set its pricing-page wording beside its Terms of Service and Amazon's own reimbursement reports. <C id="getida-02"/>

## What GETIDA does

GETIDA presents itself as a claims service rather than software you operate: it says "Claims management experts handle recovery on your behalf." <C id="getida-17"/> It describes the problems it looks for this way: "Common issues include FBA inventory units that got lost, damaged, disposed of, overcharged with fees." <C id="getida-15"/>

Its "How we help" page lists the claim types it files, including Lost Warehouse, Damaged Inbound, Lost Removal Order, Wrong FNSKU Returns and Lost in Transit. <C id="getida-12"/> Pick & Pack (Overcharge Fees) claims are marked "US only." <C id="getida-13"/> GETIDA says "Our recovery process first audits the past 18 months worth of Amazon FBA transactions, then continues in real time." <C id="getida-11"/>

Other features it lists:

- A "Patented Proof of Inventory Ownership document creation solution for private label brands" and "Proof of Delivery document retrieval from leading carriers." <C id="getida-16"/>
- Notifications inside Seller Central: "Getida notifications within Seller Central help us keep sellers updated on key action items." <C id="getida-18"/>
- Coverage: "Our marketplace coverage is worldwide, with support and assistance from a global team." <C id="getida-19"/>

GETIDA describes itself as "an authorized Amazon Selling Partner." <C id="getida-14"/> Its terms define the "API" as the interface "made available to You by Amazon." <C id="getida-10"/> For public Selling Partner API apps, Amazon's developer documentation says "Reauthorization is required annually." <C id="getida-26"/>

## Fees (checked November 8, 2026)

GETIDA publishes no named plans. Its pricing page says:

- "Free to join. No commitment. No monthly subscription fees." <C id="getida-01"/>
- "With our performance-based pricing starting at 20%, you pay only when we successfully recover FBA reimbursements." <C id="getida-02"/>
- A promotion: "Pay nothing for the first $400 we recover for you in FBA reimbursements." <C id="getida-03"/>

The Terms of Service state the fee in words and in figures. Section 1.10 reads: "'Fees' means the monies due from Customer to GETIDA in the amount of twenty-five percent (20%)." <C id="getida-04"/> The words say twenty-five percent and the figure in parentheses says 20%. On collection, the terms say "Fees and Additional Fees are due and payable via Payment Processing periodically." <C id="getida-05"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical)">
Inputs: GETIDA recovers $1,000 of reimbursements for a new account in its first period.

- At 20%: $1,000 × 0.20 = **$200** fee; seller keeps $800.
- At twenty-five percent: $1,000 × 0.25 = **$250** fee; seller keeps $750.
- If the "first $400" promotion applies to this account and is read as waiving the fee on the first $400 recovered, at 20%: ($1,000 − $400) × 0.20 = **$120** fee.

Both percentages are the two figures written in Section 1.10; the example only shows the arithmetic for each and does not say which one applies.
</Callout>

The terms say "Either party may terminate the Agreement, upon written notice, without cause, upon sixty (60) days' notice," and that notice must go "via email at the following email address: cancel@getida.com." <C id="getida-06"/> <C id="getida-07"/> After termination, "GETIDA may continue pursuing and/or collecting on claims made or arising prior to the effective termination date." <C id="getida-08"/> The agreement is governed by "the laws of the State of New Jersey." <C id="getida-09"/>

## Privacy

GETIDA's privacy policy names "Anything You Need, LLC d/b/a Getida" as the operator. <C id="getida-20"/> It says Getida may collect personal information when you use its services, which the policy defines to include "claims with third-party logistics providers (such as Amazon.com and its affiliates)." <C id="getida-22"/> It also states: "GETIDA DOES NOT SELL (AS SUCH TERM IS DEFINED IN THE CCPA) THE INFORMATION WE COLLECT." <C id="getida-21"/>

## Checking reimbursements with Amazon's own reports

Amazon's Selling Partner API documentation describes the FBA Reimbursements report as one that "Contains itemized details of a seller's inventory reimbursements, including the reason for the reimbursement." It is updated daily and "can only be requested." <C id="getida-23"/> <C id="getida-24"/> The Inventory Ledger detailed view tracks units "sold, returned, removed/disposed, damaged, lost, and found." <C id="getida-25"/> Amazon's detailed reimbursement policy for lost and damaged FBA inventory, including claim windows, is published inside Seller Central and should be checked there.
