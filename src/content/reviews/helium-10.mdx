This page summarizes what Helium 10 publishes about its own product on helium10.com and its help center, checked on November 8, 2026. We have not tested the software, we do not rate it, and this site has no affiliate relationship with Helium 10. If you are deciding whether a paid plan fits your Amazon business, the questions to settle are which tier covers the number of seller accounts and users you need, whether yearly billing is worth committing to, and what you give the tool access to when you connect your seller account.

## What Helium 10 says it is

Helium 10 describes its product as seller tools for Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop. <C id="helium-10-22"/> Its site groups the tools into product research (Black Box, Xray), keyword research (Cerebro, Magnet), listing optimization (Listing Builder) and daily operations (Profits, Inventory Management), with separate advertising and analytics groups. <C id="helium-10-23"/> The pricing page states that 24 or more marketplaces are supported. <C id="helium-10-14"/>

The vendor is Pixel Labs, LLC, doing business as Helium10, and its Terms & Conditions carry an effective date of 17 August 2026. <C id="helium-10-20"/>

## Renewal, cancellation and refunds

The pricing page says there are no long-term commitments required for monthly plans. <C id="helium-10-15"/> Cancellation is done from the Plans & Billing section of your account, before the next renewal period. <C id="helium-10-16"/> The Terms & Conditions say a subscription automatically renews at the subscription period frequency. <C id="helium-10-17"/>

On refunds, the terms say subscription payments are nonrefundable except as the terms expressly provide, and they give a full refund if you terminate within the first seven days of your initial purchase. <C id="helium-10-18"/> That seven-day window is the point to decide whether a yearly commitment is right for you.

## How it connects to your Amazon data

Some Helium 10 tools need access to your own seller account. Helium 10's help center lists tools such as Alerts, Profits and Follow-Up as needing at least one authorized token, <C id="helium-10-25"/> and says that without a Professional seller account you will not be able to connect a Seller Token. <C id="helium-10-24"/> Its terms require users of those features to grant access to third-party accounts such as an Amazon Seller Central account. <C id="helium-10-19"/>

On Amazon's side, authorization is the process by which a selling partner grants an application permission to call the Selling Partner API on its behalf. <C id="helium-10-26"/> Amazon's documentation states that reauthorization is required annually. <C id="helium-10-27"/> Apps listed in the Selling Partner Appstore are authorized from the app's detail page with Authorize Now, <C id="helium-10-28"/> and Amazon states it is not directly involved in the development or sale of third-party apps. <C id="helium-10-29"/>

Helium 10's Privacy Policy, effective 17 August 2026, is published at helium10.com/privacy-policy. <C id="helium-10-21"/>

## Amazon's own tools that cover similar ground

Some of what research tools do overlaps with tools inside Seller Central. Amazon's Product Opportunity Explorer groups products into niches built from customer search terms and analyzes trends in pricing, reviews and returns. <C id="helium-10-30"/> Brand Analytics is a set of Seller Central dashboards for registered brands. <C id="helium-10-31"/> Both sit inside a Professional selling account, which Amazon lists at $39.99 a month plus selling fees. <C id="helium-10-32"/> Our [Amazon marketplace overview](/marketplaces/amazon/) and [referral fee guide](/guides/amazon-referral-fees/) cover the selling costs that any software subscription is added to.
