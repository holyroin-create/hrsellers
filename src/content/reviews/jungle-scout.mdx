This page summarizes what Jungle Scout publishes about its own product on junglescout.com and its help center, checked on November 8, 2026. We have not tested the software, we do not rate it, and this site has no affiliate relationship with Jungle Scout. The decisions it helps with: which of the two product lines applies to you, which Catalyst plan's limits fit, whether to pay annually given the refund rules, and whether your marketplace is fully supported.

## Two product lines: Catalyst and Cobalt

Jungle Scout describes Catalyst as designed for smaller and scaling Amazon sellers focused on product-level insights. <C id="jungle-scout-20"/> Cobalt is described as built for established brands, agencies and data-driven teams, and it is sold as custom plans through a demo rather than at a listed price. <C id="jungle-scout-21"/> The homepage also lists Jungle Scout Cloud for high-volume data, Jungle Scout MCP and Jungle Scout Consult, a done-for-you analysis service. <C id="jungle-scout-34"/> The rest of this page covers Catalyst, because that is the line with published prices.

The vendor is JS Operating Company, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, and its Terms of Use were last updated February 18, 2026. <C id="jungle-scout-19"/>

## Guarantee, renewal and cancellation

Jungle Scout says it does not offer a free trial but offers a 7-day money-back guarantee on standard Catalyst plans. <C id="jungle-scout-12"/> Special promotions and trials are excluded from that guarantee. <C id="jungle-scout-13"/> The Terms of Use require you to contact Customer Support within 7 days of your original sign-up to use it. <C id="jungle-scout-18"/>

Outside that window, the terms say payments are nonrefundable, with no refunds or credits for partially used periods. <C id="jungle-scout-17"/> To avoid a renewal you must cancel at least one day before the next billing date. <C id="jungle-scout-16"/> Catalyst has no contractual obligations beyond the current billing cycle, and a cancelled subscription ends after the current term with no further charges. <C id="jungle-scout-14"/> Upgrades can be made at any time, with incremental charges prorated. <C id="jungle-scout-15"/>

## Which Amazon marketplaces are covered

Catalyst is compatible with Amazon marketplaces in the United States, Canada, Mexico, United Kingdom, France, Germany, Italy and Spain. <C id="jungle-scout-22"/> It has partial compatibility with the Netherlands, Turkey, India, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Japan, Australia, Singapore and Brazil. <C id="jungle-scout-23"/> Cobalt is listed as supporting 19 Amazon marketplaces. <C id="jungle-scout-24"/> If you sell mainly in a partially supported marketplace, check the vendor's supported-countries article for the features that apply there before paying.

## What it needs from your Amazon account

Jungle Scout's help center says connecting a Seller Central account unlocks Sales Analytics, Inventory Manager and Review Automation, <C id="jungle-scout-25"/> and that you need a Professional seller account to connect. <C id="jungle-scout-26"/> The browser Extension does not require connecting a Seller Central account. <C id="jungle-scout-27"/>

When you connect any app, Amazon's model is the same: authorization is the process by which a selling partner grants an application permission to call the Selling Partner API on its behalf. <C id="jungle-scout-28"/> For apps in the Selling Partner Appstore, you authorize from the app's detail page with Authorize Now, <C id="jungle-scout-29"/> and Amazon states it is not directly involved in the development or sale of third-party apps. <C id="jungle-scout-30"/> If you use Review Automation, our guide to [review manipulation risk](/guides/review-manipulation-risk/) covers Amazon's rules on asking for reviews.

## Amazon's own research tool

Amazon offers its own product research view inside Seller Central. Product Opportunity Explorer groups products into niches built from customer search terms and analyzes trends in pricing, reviews and returns. <C id="jungle-scout-31"/> It sits inside a Professional selling account, which Amazon lists at $39.99 a month plus selling fees. <C id="jungle-scout-32"/>

## How to confirm

Plan names, prices and limits change. Re-check the [Catalyst pricing page](https://www.junglescout.com/pricing/catalyst-plans/) and the main [pricing page](https://www.junglescout.com/pricing/) on the day you buy, and read the [Terms of Use](https://www.junglescout.com/termsofuse/) for current renewal and refund rules. For how third-party apps access your seller data, Amazon's [Selling Partner Appstore page](https://sell.amazon.com/tools/selling-partner-appstore) is the primary reference.
