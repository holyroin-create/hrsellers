This page summarizes what sellerboard publishes about its own product on sellerboard.com, checked on November 8, 2026. We have not tested the software, we do not rate it, and this site has no affiliate relationship with sellerboard. The decisions it helps with: which plan's order allowance fits your monthly volume, whether annual billing makes sense given the refund terms, and what you are agreeing to when the tool reads your Amazon data.

## What sellerboard says it does

sellerboard describes itself as a profit analytics tool for Amazon FBA sellers. <C id="sellerboard-01"/> Its dashboard shows sales, orders, refunds, advertising costs, estimated payout and net profit, <C id="sellerboard-02"/> and it says it factors in Amazon fees such as storage fees, inbound shipment and labeling fees. <C id="sellerboard-03"/> Our [Amazon referral fee guide](/guides/amazon-referral-fees/) explains Amazon's referral fees, and the [COGS and accrual accounting guide](/guides/accrual-accounting-cogs/) covers how product cost enters a profit figure.

Other features listed on the homepage:

- PPC KPIs for every campaign, ad group and keyword. <C id="sellerboard-04"/>
- Advance notice of estimated stock shortages. <C id="sellerboard-05"/>
- Finding FBA errors so you can request money back through Seller Support. <C id="sellerboard-06"/>
- An autoresponder for follow-up email campaigns to collect feedback and product reviews. <C id="sellerboard-07"/> Amazon's rules on review requests are covered in our [review manipulation risk guide](/guides/review-manipulation-risk/).

sellerboard says it works with all Amazon marketplaces. <C id="sellerboard-08"/> The homepage also lists Shopify, Walmart and eBay under the heading "Other platforms." <C id="sellerboard-09"/> It supports accounts with a maximum of 30,000 products. <C id="sellerboard-10"/>

## How costs get into the profit numbers

A profit figure is only as good as the costs behind it. sellerboard's help center says its Products page is where the product-level data that feeds its profit calculations is managed. <C id="sellerboard-30"/> Cost of goods is entered in an Edit COGS panel that handles costs by batch, period and marketplace, <C id="sellerboard-31"/> and a cost can be set to apply to a specific date range. <C id="sellerboard-32"/> For orders you fulfill yourself, per-order costs can be set, including by import, and formulas cover costs that are part fixed and part variable. <C id="sellerboard-33"/>

Two reporting choices are also documented. Currency conversion uses the exchange rate from the last day of the selected reporting period. <C id="sellerboard-34"/> A lifetime-value dashboard reports units and orders per average buyer, accumulated within the selected time range. <C id="sellerboard-35"/>

## Trial, renewal, cancellation and refunds

sellerboard offers a 1-month free trial with no credit card required. <C id="sellerboard-11"/> After that, the terms say the subscription renews automatically, monthly or on a yearly or half-yearly basis depending on the package. <C id="sellerboard-20"/> You can terminate at any time by notifying sellerboard through support chat or email, effective the last day of the current renewal term. <C id="sellerboard-21"/> Subscription fees are non-refundable. <C id="sellerboard-22"/>

## Who runs it and how it reaches your Amazon data

sellerboard is a brand of nextechnology GmbH, based in Stuttgart, Germany. <C id="sellerboard-19"/> Its privacy policy, at sellerboard.com/privacypolicy, says business data are used exclusively for operating the sellerboard software. <C id="sellerboard-23"/>

The sellerboard pages checked for this summary do not describe the connection process itself. Amazon's general model applies to any app: authorization is the process by which a selling partner grants an application permission to call the Selling Partner API on its behalf, <C id="sellerboard-24"/> and Amazon's documentation states that reauthorization is required annually. <C id="sellerboard-25"/> Apps in the Selling Partner Appstore are authorized from the app's detail page with Authorize Now, <C id="sellerboard-26"/> and Amazon states it is not directly involved in the development or sale of third-party apps. <C id="sellerboard-27"/>

## Amazon's own free tools

A Professional selling account, which Amazon lists at $39.99 a month plus selling fees, <C id="sellerboard-28"/> already includes some cost tools. Amazon describes its FBA revenue calculator as giving real-time comparisons to decide whether to fulfill orders yourself. <C id="sellerboard-29"/>

## How to confirm

Prices, limits and terms change. Re-check the pricing section on [sellerboard.com](https://sellerboard.com/) and the [Terms of Service](https://sellerboard.com/terms) on the day you subscribe. For how any app is authorized to read your seller data, use Amazon's [Selling Partner Appstore page](https://sell.amazon.com/tools/selling-partner-appstore).
