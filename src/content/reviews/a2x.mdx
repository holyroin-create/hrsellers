This page summarizes what A2X publishes about its own product on a2xaccounting.com and its support site, checked on November 8, 2026. We have not tested the software, we do not rate it, and this site has no affiliate relationship with A2X. If you are deciding whether to use it, the questions are which plan your monthly order count falls into, what the plan will cost after the November 14, 2026 price change, and how its entries fit the way your bookkeeper records Amazon payouts.

## What A2X says it does

A2X integrates Amazon settlement data with QuickBooks, Xero or NetSuite. <C id="a2x-16"/> It creates a summary entry covering the exact period you are paid for, <C id="a2x-13"/> and says that entry matches the payout in your bank. <C id="a2x-14"/> It can also create a COGS entry in QuickBooks Online or Xero. <C id="a2x-15"/> Our [accrual accounting and COGS guide](/guides/accrual-accounting-cogs/) explains why matching cost of goods to the period of sale matters.

The source data is Amazon's settlement report. Amazon's Selling Partner API documentation says settlement reports cannot be requested or scheduled; Amazon schedules them automatically. <C id="a2x-27"/> For Amazon US, A2X says settlement data is fetched automatically and can then be posted automatically to your accounting system. <C id="a2x-19"/>

A2X sells separate plans per sales channel; its pricing overview lists Amazon starting at US$29 a month and Walmart at US$79. <C id="a2x-28"/> This page covers the Amazon plans.

## Trial, billing, cancellation and refunds

A2X's free trial gives full access to all features and lets you post three settlements to your accounting system, <C id="a2x-11"/> with no credit card required. <C id="a2x-12"/>

The Terms of Service, from A2X Software Limited, a Canadian company, were last updated October 20th, 2026. <C id="a2x-20"/> They say Access Fees are billed for each Organization on a monthly basis, <C id="a2x-21"/> that a subscription automatically continues for another period of the same duration, <C id="a2x-22"/> and that you cancel by selecting to cancel on the website. <C id="a2x-23"/> Access Fees are non-refundable, including prepaid subscriptions, <C id="a2x-24"/> and A2X may change the fee schedule upon notice. <C id="a2x-25"/>

## Connecting Amazon and what data it collects

Each A2X account can pull settlements from any marketplace in one region and automatically connects the other marketplaces in that region. <C id="a2x-17"/> If you are not the owner of the seller account, the owner receives an email with instructions to complete the connection. <C id="a2x-18"/>

Under Amazon's model, authorization is the process by which a selling partner grants an application permission to call the Selling Partner API on its behalf, <C id="a2x-29"/> and Amazon states it is not directly involved in the development or sale of third-party apps. <C id="a2x-30"/> A2X's privacy policy, last updated October 20th, 2026, says it collects financial, sales and accounting information from sales channels and accounting platforms. <C id="a2x-26"/>

Any subscription sits on top of Amazon's own charges; Amazon lists a Professional selling account at $39.99 a month plus selling fees. <C id="a2x-31"/> For the tax reporting side of marketplace income, see our [Form 1099-K guide](/guides/form-1099-k-marketplace-sellers/).
