This page summarizes what AMZScout publishes about its own product, prices and policies on its website, checked on November 8, 2026. We did not test the software, we do not rate it, and this site has no affiliate relationship with AMZScout or any other vendor. The practical question for a seller is which of AMZScout's two separately sold products, if either, matches the research work you actually do, and what that costs per year. <C id="amzscout-07"/>

## What AMZScout sells

AMZScout splits its offer into two products that are priced independently: the **AMZScout AI Toolset**, labeled "DO IT YOURSELF", and **AMZScout MCP for Claude and ChatGPT**, labeled "WITH YOUR AI ASSISTANT". The vendor states that "they are sold separately, so you only pay for what you use." <C id="amzscout-07"/>

The AI Toolset bundles these research tools, in the vendor's words:

- **Product Database:** "Generate profitable product ideas, select the best product from a profitable niche, and check product performance." <C id="amzscout-14"/>
- **PRO AI Extension:** "Find and verify profitable products using AI, and see if they are trending upward or downward." <C id="amzscout-15"/>
- **Keyword Search:** discovering "thousands of effective keywords for your product." <C id="amzscout-16"/>
- **Keyword Tracker:** "Make sure that your product's keywords are indexed by Amazon, and track their organic search ranking." <C id="amzscout-17"/>
- **MCP Connector:** "Give Claude and ChatGPT access to real Amazon data for instant product validation and keyword research." <C id="amzscout-18"/>

The MCP product is described on its own page as a service that "connects your AI to real Amazon product data." <C id="amzscout-19"/> The page lists the data it works with as "demand, competition, pricing, product history, keywords, reviews, Buy Box, and marketplace availability." <C id="amzscout-20"/>

## Marketplaces and data accuracy

AMZScout says its tools let you "sell across 14 Amazon markets," listing the USA, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Canada, Mexico, India, Japan, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Australia and Brazil. <C id="amzscout-13"/>

On accuracy, the vendor's FAQ is direct: "AMZScout provides estimates based on real sales data and market trends," and "no tool can guarantee 100% accuracy." <C id="amzscout-22"/> <C id="amzscout-23"/> The MCP page adds that data available through the service and AI-generated outputs are "provided for informational purposes only." <C id="amzscout-21"/>

## Trial, refunds and cancellation

- **Trial:** the homepage shows "Try AMZScout for Free!", "No credit card required" and "Unlimited-time trial". It does not spell out what the trial includes, so confirm the limits at sign-up. <C id="amzscout-12"/>
- **Refunds:** the homepage advertises a "10-day money-back guarantee." <C id="amzscout-11"/>
- **Cancellation:** the FAQ says "You can cancel your subscription by contacting support@amzscout.net or using the Help window on the AMZScout website." <C id="amzscout-24"/>

Renewal and refund conditions beyond the homepage wording are set out in AMZScout's Terms of Use and are not summarized here.

## Privacy

AMZScout's privacy policy says it "does not sell, trade, or transfer User Information to third parties," and that it keeps personal data only "for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes we collected it for." <C id="amzscout-25"/> <C id="amzscout-26"/>

## Amazon's own research tools

Amazon offers a research tool of its own. Amazon says Product Opportunity Explorer "primarily categorizes products within niches" and shows data including "search terms and volume." <C id="amzscout-27"/> Amazon says you reach it from Seller Central: "In the main menu in Seller Central, select Growth, then Product Opportunity Explorer." <C id="amzscout-28"/> Amazon's page gives no separate price for the tool; the cost it names is the selling account: "A Professional selling account is $39.99/month + selling fees." <C id="amzscout-29"/> For what Amazon charges per sale, see the [Amazon referral fee guide](/guides/amazon-referral-fees/) and the [Amazon fee tables](/marketplaces/amazon/).
