This page summarizes what Aura publishes about its own repricing software, prices and terms on goaura.com and its help center, checked on November 8, 2026. We did not test Aura, we do not rate it, and this site has no affiliate relationship with Aura, its owner Vendrive, Inc., or any other vendor. Aura's plans are tiered by monthly sales, marketplaces, Hyperdrive listings, workflows and users, and Amazon offers its own free Automate Pricing tool, described at the end of this page. <C id="aura-26"/> <C id="aura-33"/>

## What Aura is

Aura describes itself as "The AI-powered Walmart and Amazon repricer." <C id="aura-14"/> Its pricing logic has three layers, as the vendor describes them:

- **Maven**, the AI engine, which Aura says "learns which prices lose you the Buy Box." <C id="aura-15"/>
- **Workflows**, which "Automate strategy changes, scheduling, and listing management." <C id="aura-16"/>
- **Hyperdrive**, which the help center says "allows Aura to submit price updates every 10 seconds on your most critical listings." Hyperdrive "is currently only available for Amazon listings." <C id="aura-17"/> <C id="aura-18"/>

Aura's help center says "Aura is a reactive repricer" and "Each time the targeted competitor changes their price, Aura will follow suit." <C id="aura-19"/> With Hyperdrive off, it says "A standard price change takes about 1.5-2 minutes on average." <C id="aura-20"/> On Amazon's side, the Selling Partner API publishes an ANY_OFFER_CHANGED notification that "is sent whenever there is a change to any of the top 20 offers, by condition (new or used)." <C id="aura-37"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical)">
Inputs: a seller sells on Amazon US and Walmart, with sales of $42,000 and $47,000 in the last two months, and wants Hyperdrive on 20 listings.

- Trailing two-month sales average: ($42,000 + $47,000) ÷ 2 = $44,500 per month, which is above Starter's "Up to $15K" and within Essential's "Up to $50K".
- Essential allows 3 marketplaces and 25 Hyperdrive listings, which covers two marketplaces and 20 listings.
- Annual cost at the published figure: $931, or $931 ÷ 12 = $77.58 per month.

Aura's exact method for "trailing 2-months" is not spelled out beyond that phrase; the averaging here is an assumption for illustration.
</Callout>

## Floors, ceilings and costs

Aura "calculates your min from your buy cost plus your target ROI" and "automatically accounts for Amazon's referral fee and, on FBA listings, the fulfilment fee." <C id="aura-21"/> Other costs, such as inbound shipping and prep, "must be entered in the Additional Cost field, or it won't be reflected in your min price." <C id="aura-22"/> At the top end, "Aura holds a listing at its max price whenever there's no fulfillable stock." <C id="aura-23"/> Because the floor depends on the fees you enter or import, check your numbers against the [Amazon referral fee guide](/guides/amazon-referral-fees/) and, for Walmart, the [WFS fee guide](/guides/walmart-wfs/).

## Connecting your Amazon account

Aura's help center says that, after you choose a region ("Marketplaces for US, Canada, and Mexico fall under North America"), "Clicking Continue will open authentication for Seller Central," where you authorize access. <C id="aura-24"/> It says "This connection allows all listings, listing data, sales metrics, and inventory to import." <C id="aura-25"/> For public Selling Partner API apps, Amazon's developer documentation says "Reauthorization is required annually." <C id="aura-38"/> Aura's privacy policy says it may collect "an Amazon account token when you register for an account on the Service." <C id="aura-32"/>

## Amazon's free alternative: Automate Pricing

Amazon says: "Our free Automate Pricing tool automatically adjusts your prices in real time," and "The Automate Pricing tool is included for free in the Professional selling plan." <C id="aura-33"/> <C id="aura-34"/> Like Aura, it uses limits: "Manage your margins by setting a minimum price and an optional maximum price." <C id="aura-35"/> You find it under "Pricing, then Automate Pricing" in the Seller Central main menu. <C id="aura-36"/> Third-party repricers sold through the Selling Partner Appstore set their own prices; in Amazon's words, "For each individual app, software partners set their own prices." <C id="aura-39"/>
