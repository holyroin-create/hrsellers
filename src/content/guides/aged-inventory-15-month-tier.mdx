If you hold FBA units that have been in storage for more than 181 days, you pay a monthly aged inventory charge on top of regular storage. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-01"/> The oldest band in Amazon's inventory report for the US store starts at 456 days, and the report estimates the charge for it separately; 456 days is about 15 months by our arithmetic, which is why this page calls it the "15-month" tier. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-19"/> The per-cubic-foot rate for each band is published inside Seller Central (look up "aged inventory surcharge" there); everything else on this page comes from Amazon's public announcements and Selling Partner API documentation. From February 1, 2027 the separate surcharge ends and age is priced into a single storage rate. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-07"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-08"/>

## What Amazon publicly says the charge is

Amazon's FBA cost overview describes aged inventory as "Charged monthly for all items stored in a fulfillment center for more than 181 days," and adds that "Maintaining sound inventory health can help you avoid this cost." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-01"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-02"/>

- **It is per item and age-based.** The test is how long each item has been stored, not how fast the SKU sells overall.
- **It is monthly.** An aged unit keeps drawing the charge every month it stays.
- **It is additional.** Regular storage is a separate charge, billed monthly on the daily average volume inventory occupies. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-17"/> How the layers combine is covered in [FBA storage fees: the four layers](/guides/fba-storage-four-layers/).

## The age bands, as Amazon's report lays them out

The field list of the Manage Inventory Health report (report type `GET_FBA_INVENTORY_PLANNING_DATA`) shows the bands the surcharge estimate is split into. For each band there is a `quantity-to-be-charged-ais-…` column (units that will be charged) and an `estimated-ais-…` column (the estimated surcharge). <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-18"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-19"/>

| Days in storage | Report columns | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 181–210 | `quantity-to-be-charged-ais-181-210-days`, `estimated-ais-181-210-days` | No store limit noted |
| 211–240 | `…-ais-211-240-days` | No store limit noted |
| 241–270 | `…-ais-241-270-days` | No store limit noted |
| 271–300 | `…-ais-271-300-days` | No store limit noted |
| 301–330 | `…-ais-301-330-days` | No store limit noted |
| 331–365 | `…-ais-331-365-days` | No store limit noted |
| 366–455 | `…-ais-366-455-days` | US, DE, UK, FR, IT, ES only |
| 456 and over | `…-ais-456-plus-days` | US, DE, UK, FR, IT, ES only |

*Source: Amazon Selling Partner API documentation, FBA report type values, checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-18"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-19"/>

Fifteen average-length months come to about 456 days (15 × 30.4 = 456), so this page treats the 456-plus band as the 15-month tier; that equivalence is our arithmetic, not Amazon's wording. The same report carries inventory-age columns for the oldest brackets — `inv-age-271-to-365-days`, `inv-age-366-to-455-days` and `inv-age-456-plus-days` — so you can see how many units are in each bracket. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-20"/>

## How the bands have moved

Amazon has adjusted bands and rates before. Its November 2022 fee announcement said Amazon would "increase the surcharges applied to inventory stored between 271-365 days" and introduce surcharges on inventory stored 180–270 days, excluding Apparel, Shoes, Bags, Jewelry and Watches. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-03"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-04"/> Those changes applied from "the April 15, 2023 aged inventory assessment date." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-05"/> Amazon's stated reason: to "help drive more efficient use of our storage and create more capacity for sellers who are using space efficiently." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-06"/>

## February 1, 2027: one age-based rate

Amazon's 2027 fee announcement says it will "remove the aged inventory surcharge and combine it with monthly storage fees into a single, age-based rate," with storage changes effective February 1, 2027. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-07"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-08"/> The stated effect: "flat or lower fees for inventory that turns in under six months, higher fees for aged inventory." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-09"/> Amazon also describes it as "a new age-based rate that will make your storage costs easier to predict." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-25"/>

To see the effect on your own SKUs, Amazon points to a new Fee Change Comparison dashboard. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-26"/> Today's Storage Fees report (`GET_FBA_STORAGE_FEE_CHARGES_DATA`) already splits each ASIN's estimate into `base_rate`, `utilization_surcharge_rate` and `estimated_monthly_storage_fee`; save a copy for January 2027 so you have a before-and-after baseline when the structure changes. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-24"/>

## Worked example (hypothetical): when a lot reaches each band

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical)">
Inputs: three lots of the same SKU, received into FBA on November 1, 2025, February 1, 2026 and August 1, 2026. Thresholds: Amazon's published 181 days and the 366-day and 456-day band starts shown in the inventory health report. Dates are simple calendar arithmetic (receipt date plus the number of days); Amazon's exact assessment day is set in Seller Central.

| Lot received | Day 181 | Day 366 (366–455 band) | Day 456 (456+ band) | Structure when it reaches day 456 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 1, 2025 | May 1, 2026 | Nov 2, 2026 | Jan 31, 2027 | Reaches day 456 one day before the change |
| Feb 1, 2026 | Aug 1, 2026 | Feb 2, 2027 | May 3, 2027 | Single age-based rate |
| Aug 1, 2026 | Jan 29, 2027 | Aug 2, 2027 | Oct 31, 2027 | Single age-based rate |

*Source: 181-day threshold from Amazon's FBA page; band starts from the SP-API report fields; February 1, 2027 from Amazon's 2027 fee announcement, checked Nov 9, 2026; dates are our arithmetic.* <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-01"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-19"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-08"/>
</Callout>

On these dates, the November 2025 lot is already in the 366–455 band and reaches 456 days on January 31, 2027, a day before the surcharge is folded into storage; its cost from February onward depends on the new age-based rate. The August 2026 lot passes 181 days three days before the change, so almost all of its aged period falls under the new rate.

Amazon's inventory management guide lists over 90 days of supply, or at least one unit aged over 90 days, among the signs of excess inventory, and warns that "aging inventory could force you to liquidate." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-13"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-14"/> For 2026, Amazon also listed "maintaining healthy inventory levels" among the ways to reach lower fees. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-16"/> For the cost comparison itself, see [remove or keep FBA inventory](/guides/removal-vs-hold-decision/).

## Which report answers which question

| Question | Report (SP-API report type) | Columns to read |
|---|---|---|
| How many units are in each age bracket now? | Manage Inventory Health (`GET_FBA_INVENTORY_PLANNING_DATA`) | `inv-age-366-to-455-days`, `inv-age-456-plus-days` |
| What will the next charge be, by band? | Manage Inventory Health | `quantity-to-be-charged-ais-…`, `estimated-ais-…` |
| What does Amazon suggest doing? | Manage Inventory Health | `recommended-action`, `recommended-removal-quantity`, `recommended-sale-duration-days` |
| Which sellable units are 365+ days old? | Recommended Removal (`GET_FBA_RECOMMENDED_REMOVAL_DATA`) | `sellable-271-365-days`, `sellable-365+-days` |
| What was I actually billed? | Long-Term Storage Fee Charges (`GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_LONGTERM_STORAGE_FEE_CHARGES_DATA`) | `surcharge-age-tier`, `rate-surcharge`, `qty-charged`, `amount-charged` |

*Source: Amazon Selling Partner API documentation, FBA report type values, checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-12"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-23"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-27"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-21"/>

The Long-Term Storage Fee Charges report "Contains the charge data for long-term storage," and Amazon says a request should span one month, so pull it month by month rather than for a whole year at once. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-11"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-22"/> The Manage Inventory Health report also gives a consolidated view of sales over 7, 30, 60 and 90 days, which you need to judge whether aged units will sell before the next band. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-12"/>
