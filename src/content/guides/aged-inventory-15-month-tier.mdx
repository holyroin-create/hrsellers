The aged-inventory escalator grew a new top step in 2026: **a 15+ month tier**, reported at **$0.35 per unit or $7.90 per cubic foot** (whichever is greater), above a 12-15 month tier at $0.30/$6.90. The message in the structure is blunter than the rates: Amazon no longer treats "over a year old" as one problem — it now prices a year and a half separately, and worse.

## Why this matters in September specifically

Aged surcharges stack on monthly storage, and monthly storage [steps up for Q4](/guides/fba-storage-four-layers/) from October. A unit crossing its 15-month birthday in November pays the new top tier on top of peak-season rates — the most expensive possible combination, billed monthly, for stock that by definition is not selling. The September discipline: pull the aged-inventory report, and force a decision on everything past 180 days while the exits are cheap — a removal or liquidation now costs less than three months of stacked winter rent on a unit that will still be there in February. The [returns and disposal math](/guides/returns-cost-and-badge/) prices the exit options.
