If you hold FBA units that have been in storage for more than 181 days, you pay a monthly aged inventory charge on top of regular storage. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-01"/> The current rates for each age band, including the 15-month tier this page is named for, are published inside Seller Central and should be checked there. What Amazon has published is the threshold, how the bands have moved, and a structural change on February 1, 2027 that ends the separate surcharge. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-07"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-08"/> The decision for slow stock is whether to sell it down, pull it out, or liquidate it before that date and before it ages into the next band.

## What Amazon publicly says the charge is

Amazon's FBA cost overview describes aged inventory as "Charged monthly for all items stored in a fulfillment center for more than 181 days," and adds that "Maintaining sound inventory health can help you avoid this cost." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-01"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-02"/>

Three things follow from that wording:

- **It is per item and age-based.** The test is how long each item has been stored, not how fast the SKU sells overall.
- **It is monthly.** An aged unit keeps drawing the charge every month it stays.
- **It is additional.** Regular storage is a separate charge, billed monthly on the space inventory occupies. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-17"/> How the layers combine is covered in [FBA storage fees: the four layers](/guides/fba-storage-four-layers/).

## How the age bands have moved

Amazon's announcements show that the surcharge is applied in age bands, tied to assessment dates, and that Amazon has adjusted bands and rates over time. Its November 2022 fee announcement, for example, said Amazon would "increase the surcharges applied to inventory stored between 271-365 days" and introduce surcharges on inventory stored 180–270 days, excluding Apparel, Shoes, Bags, Jewelry and Watches. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-03"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-04"/> Those changes applied from "the April 15, 2023 aged inventory assessment date." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-05"/>

Amazon gave its reason at the time: the changes would "help drive more efficient use of our storage and create more capacity for sellers who are using space efficiently." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-06"/>

Rates for bands above 365 days, including the 15-month tier, are in Seller Central. Check the rate, the category exclusions, and the assessment day there before you model a long hold.

## February 1, 2027: one age-based rate

Amazon's 2027 fee announcement changes the structure. It says Amazon will "remove the aged inventory surcharge and combine it with monthly storage fees into a single, age-based rate," with storage changes effective February 1, 2027. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-07"/> <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-08"/> The stated effect: "flat or lower fees for inventory that turns in under six months, higher fees for aged inventory." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-09"/>

So age keeps costing money after February 1, 2027 — Amazon says aged inventory will pay higher fees — but the cost moves into the storage rate itself rather than a separate surcharge line. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-09"/>

</Callout>

Two points come out of the table. The November 2025 lot is already past a year and will hit the 15-month mark exactly when the surcharge is replaced, so its cost from then on depends on the new age-based rate, not the old surcharge band. And the August 2026 lot crosses 181 days only three days before the structure changes, so on these dates what matters for it is the age-based rate from February.

## Reports that show the charge

Two Selling Partner API report types hold the data. The Long-Term Storage Fee Charges report "Contains the charge data for long-term storage." <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-11"/> The Manage Inventory Health report "Provides a consolidated view across your sales (7, 30, 60, and 90 days), shipment statuses" and more, which is the starting point for the review above. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-12"/>

## How to confirm the current age bands

- Amazon's FBA cost overview at sell.amazon.com/fulfillment-by-amazon, for the 181-day definition. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-01"/>
- Amazon's 2027 fee announcement on sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com, for the February 1, 2027 change. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-08"/>
- Seller Central's aged inventory surcharge page, for the current bands (including 15 months and over), rates, exclusions and assessment day.
- The Long-Term Storage Fee Charges report for what you were actually billed. <C id="aged-inventory-15-month-tier-11"/>

This page describes Amazon's published rules and is not a review of any specific charge; a charge you believe is wrong has to be raised with Amazon through your seller account.
