If you send shoppers to your Amazon listings from your own ads, emails or social posts, the Brand Referral Bonus gives you back an average of 10% of the qualifying sales as a credit against future referral fees. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-01"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-02"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-10"/> To qualify you need a Professional plan in Amazon's US store, a brand enrolled in Brand Registry, and traffic tagged with Amazon Attribution. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-13"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-14"/> It is not cash, it does not apply to Amazon's own ads, and the full amount generally lands after a two-month wait. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-11"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-09"/>

How the referral fee itself is calculated is covered in the [referral fee guide](/guides/amazon-referral-fees/); this page covers only the bonus that offsets it.

  "Marketing that runs outside Amazon: social media, search and display ads, email"

*Source: Amazon, Brand Referral Bonus post on sell.amazon.com and Brand Registry page, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="brand-referral-bonus-13"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-28"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-19"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-12"/>

Amazon Ads describes the program the same way: a credit for US seller brand owners worth an average of 10% of qualifying sales. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-25"/> Amazon's Brand Registry page describes it as a way to earn credits from non-Amazon marketing. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-26"/> If you have no trademark yet, see the [trademark and Brand Registry guide](/guides/brand-registry-trademark/). Sellers who are not brand owners, or have no registered trademark, might be able to join Amazon Associates instead, which offers up to 10% in commissions on qualifying purchases. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-18"/>

## How much you earn

The rate depends on several factors, including sales price and product category. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-03"/> Amazon publishes two examples:

| Category | Estimated bonus rate |
|---|---|
| Clothing | 11% |
| Furniture, portion of total sales price up to $200 | 10% |
| Furniture, portion above $200 | 7% |

*Source: Amazon, Brand Referral Bonus post on sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="brand-referral-bonus-04"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-05"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-06"/>

Shipping and gift wrap charges can move the amount, <C id="brand-referral-bonus-07"/> and cancellations or returns can reduce it. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-08"/> Amazon's public post gives only these two category examples; check your own category's rate in Seller Central before you budget.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): a furniture brand's email campaign">
Inputs: a brand sends an email campaign tagged with Amazon Attribution; it drives 20 qualifying sales of a $300 chair; no returns; Amazon's estimated furniture rates apply.

- First $200 of each sale: 10% × $200 = $20 <C id="brand-referral-bonus-05"/>
- Remaining $100: 7% × $100 = $7 <C id="brand-referral-bonus-06"/>
- Bonus per sale: $27; for 20 sales: $540 in credits
- Those credits offset referral fees on later sales, not the email platform's bill. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-10"/>

If the same sales came from a Sponsored Products ad instead, the bonus would be $0, because Amazon advertising does not qualify. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-11"/>
</Callout>

Amazon's own illustration uses a $100 backpack with a 14% referral fee: <C id="brand-referral-bonus-16"/> a 7% credit earned on one sale offsets the fee on a later sale, raising net profit on that sale from $86 to $93. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-17"/>

Amazon Attribution is free <C id="brand-referral-bonus-21"/> and available to professional sellers enrolled in Brand Registry. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-22"/> Its reports use a 14-day attribution window. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-23"/> On August 5, 2022, Amazon Ads announced that Brand Referral Bonus data is also available in Amazon Attribution performance reports in the Ad console and through the Amazon Ads API. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-24"/>

## Cash flow: what the bonus does and doesn't do

Because the bonus arrives as credits that offset referral fees on future sales, <C id="brand-referral-bonus-10"/> it only has value if you keep selling on Amazon after the campaign. A brand that runs one outside campaign and then pauses sales has credits but no fees to apply them to.

The timing matters too. The full amount is generally credited after a two-month waiting period, <C id="brand-referral-bonus-09"/> and cancellations or returns can affect the amount. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-08"/> The bonus arrives after the campaign has run, as credits against later referral fees rather than money for the campaign itself.

The bonus and the tracking also run on different clocks. Attribution reports use a 14-day attribution window, <C id="brand-referral-bonus-23"/> while the bonus itself settles over about two months. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-09"/>

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What Amazon's pages say |
|---|---|
| Expecting a bonus on Sponsored Products sales | Amazon advertising does not qualify |
| Using one Attribution tag across several ads | Each tag should be unique, one per ad |
| Assuming 10% on every product | 10% is an average; the rate varies by price and category |
| Booking the bonus as cash in the month of sale | It is a credit against future referral fees, generally in full after two months |
| Ignoring returns | Cancellations and returns can reduce the bonus |
| Enrolling without a trademark filing | Brand Registry needs a pending or registered trademark |

## Don't confuse it with the new-brand offer

Amazon's Brand Registry page also advertises a separate offer, headed "Get 10% back on branded sales": 10% back on the first $50,000 in branded sales, then 5% back through the first year until $1,000,000. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-27"/> The Brand Registry page does not list conditions for that offer. Separately again, Amazon's pricing page says a new-to-Amazon brand can get over $50,000 in automatic referral-fee waivers for its first year. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-29"/>

## How to confirm it for your brand

Re-read Amazon's Brand Referral Bonus post on sell.amazon.com for the rules and examples, the Amazon Attribution page on advertising.amazon.com for tagging and the 14-day window, and the Brand Registry page for the new-brand offer. <C id="brand-referral-bonus-01"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-23"/> <C id="brand-referral-bonus-27"/> Then open the program in Seller Central to see your own category rates before tagging a campaign.
