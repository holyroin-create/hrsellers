This site now runs to around ninety guides, and most of them end with something to check on a schedule. Collected, those instructions turn out to be a small routine rather than a large one.

## Daily — two minutes

**Account Health dashboard.** Order Defect Rate, late shipment rate, cancellation rate, policy notifications. Reporting describes ODR notifications as arriving *after* you cross 1%, which makes this the only early warning that exists. → [Order defect rate](/guides/order-defect-rate/)

That is the entire daily list.

## Weekly — fifteen minutes

**Feedback Manager** for new negative entries, including any eligible for removal. → [ODR](/guides/order-defect-rate/)

**A-to-z claims** — respond immediately rather than letting them auto-grant.

**Capacity and IPI**, particularly in the run-up to Q4. → [IPI and restock limits](/guides/ipi-restock-limits/)

**Days of cover per SKU** against the 28-day floor. → [Forecasting](/guides/demand-forecasting-reorder/)

**Advertising search terms** — mine converting keywords, cut the rest. → [PPC benchmarks](/guides/amazon-ppc-benchmarks/)

## Monthly — about an hour

**Inventory age report.** The 150-day review, ahead of the reported 180-day aged-inventory trigger. → [Removal decision](/guides/removal-vs-hold-decision/)

**Storage fee report.** What you were actually charged, per ASIN. → [Storage](/guides/fba-storage-four-layers/)

**SKU Economics Report** for the returns processing fee — deducted between the 7th and 15th of the third month after shipment, which is why it needs looking for. → [Returns](/guides/returns-cost-and-badge/)

**Return rate per ASIN** and reason codes. → [Returns](/guides/returns-cost-and-badge/)

**Brand Analytics**, if enrolled. Free, and rarely opened. → [Brand tools](/guides/aplus-content-brand-tools/)

**Stranded inventory** — resolve within 48 hours as a standing habit. → [Listing disappeared](/guides/listing-disappeared/)

## Quarterly

**Rebuild landed cost per unit.** Duty rates, freight, user fees. → [Landed cost](/guides/landed-cost-fee-stack/) · [Tariff stack](/guides/tariff-stack-2026/)

**Fee Preview on your top twenty SKUs.** Since price bands became an input, published averages do not describe you. → [Price bands](/guides/price-tier-repricing-trap/)

**Settlement reports** for order-to-cash timing and effective currency conversion rate. → [DD+7](/guides/dd7-payout-reserve/) · [Currency](/guides/currency-conversion-payouts/)

**Actual lead times against assumed ones**, per supplier and freight mode. → [Forecasting](/guides/demand-forecasting-reorder/)

**Automated message templates** against current policy. → [Buyer-seller messaging](/guides/buyer-seller-messaging/)

## The fixed dates

These cannot be caught up later.

| Date | What |
| --- | --- |
| **~5 Aug / 5 Sep** | Early-bird deal submission deadlines for the autumn events → [Deals](/guides/deal-and-coupon-fees/) |
| **30 September** | Last day to fix a Q4 inventory position at off-peak rates → [Peak season](/guides/peak-season-cost-stack/) |
| **1 October** | Q4 storage rates begin; user-fee inflation adjustments take effect → [Storage](/guides/fba-storage-four-layers/) · [Landed cost](/guides/landed-cost-fee-stack/) |
| **15 October** | Peak fulfilment fees begin → [Peak fee](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/) |
| **14 January** | Peak fulfilment fees end — relevant to January clearance timing |
| **January** | Amazon's recent pattern for annual US fee changes → [Fee changes](/guides/amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026/) |

**The two most consequential are 30 September and 15 October**, and they are two weeks apart. Everything you want fixed before peak has to be fixed before the first; everything you want fulfilled at standard rates has to ship before the second.

## The windows that expire quietly

No notification, no reminder, and each one runs on its own clock.

**180 days from liquidation, per entry** — the customs protest window for recovering overpaid duty. → [Prior disclosure and protests](/guides/prior-disclosure-protests/)

**90 days** — the appeal window on a deactivated account, and the reported fund settlement period. → [Suspension](/guides/account-suspension-appeal/)

**~72 hours** — to produce a compliance certificate on request. Retrieval time, not testing time. → [US compliance](/guides/us-product-compliance/)

**60 days** — your lowest price in this window caps your next deal price. → [Deals](/guides/deal-and-coupon-fees/)

**14 days** — the attribution window before judging an external campaign. → [Attribution](/guides/brand-referral-bonus/)

**30 days** — the buyer-seller messaging window.

## Annually

**Classification review against the current tariff schedule.** Reporting notes the 2026 edition was published on 31 December 2025 with Revision 4 by 25 February — a code correct two years ago may not be correct now. → [Tariff engineering](/guides/tariff-engineering-design/)

**Supplier re-verification.** Registry status, court records, ownership. Verification is not a one-time event. → [Supplier verification](/guides/supplier-verification/)

**GS1 renewal**, if you hold a prefix. A lapse causes listing problems with no obvious cause. → [Barcodes](/guides/gs1-barcodes-fnsku/)

**Trademark renewal.** Brand Registry access depends on it. → [Brand Registry](/guides/brand-registry-trademark/)

**Insurance renewal and limits review.** → [Insurance](/guides/seller-insurance-coi/)

**Customs bond adequacy** against current duty spend — the amount scales with duty, and duty rose. → [Customs bonds](/guides/customs-bonds/)

## The two ahead

**22 September 2026** — CBP Entry Type 13 voluntary test for mail shipments begins.

**1 July 2027** — Section 321 repealed for commercial shipments by statute.

→ [Section 321 repeal](/guides/section-321-repeal-2027/)

## What to do

**Put the daily check in a calendar**, not in your intentions. Two minutes, and it is the only early warning for the metric that suspends accounts by itself.

**Block one hour a month** for the monthly list. It is where the 150-day inventory review and the delayed returns fee both live.

**Diarise 30 September and 15 October now.**

**Set a reminder for any 180-day protest deadline** the moment you learn an entry may be affected. Nothing else will remind you.

**Do the annual reviews in a quiet month** — reporting on the [SFP guide](/guides/seller-fulfilled-prime/) names January to early June and August to mid-October as the low-pressure windows, and the same logic applies to any review that needs attention.
