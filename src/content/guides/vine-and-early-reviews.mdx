Amazon Vine is Amazon's program for collecting reviews on your products, even before launch: reviewers called Vine Voices request enrolled units free of charge and post honest, unbiased reviews. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-01"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-02"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-03"/> In the US the fee is $0 for up to 2 units, $75 for 3 to 10 and $200 for 11 to 30, per parent product. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-17"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-18"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-19"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-20"/> The real decisions are how many units to offer and when to enroll, because the inventory has to be in Amazon's network before reviewers can claim it.

What the law says about getting reviews outside Vine is covered in [the fake review rule guide](/guides/review-manipulation-risk/), not on this page.

## Who the reviewers are

Vine Voices are chosen for the insightfulness of the reviews they have already published in Amazon's store. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-04"/> Amazon matches your product with Voices who have purchase history and expertise in your category. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-05"/> Amazon fulfills all Vine products through FBA so that the Voices' contact information is not disclosed. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-30"/> Amazon describes the reviews as honest and unbiased opinions, <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-03"/> so enrolling buys access to reviewers, not a particular rating.

*Source: Amazon, Amazon Vine page on sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-08"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-06"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-07"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-09"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-10"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-12"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-11"/>

The fewer-than-30-reviews ceiling makes Vine a launch tool: once a listing has 30 reviews it can no longer enroll. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-09"/> Bundles are excluded outright, as are adult and digital products. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-11"/>

Vine runs in the US, Canada, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Australia and Japan stores. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-29"/>

## What it costs and when you are charged

| Units offered per parent product | US fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 2 | $0 |
| 3–10 | $75 |
| 11–30 | $200 |

*Source: Amazon, Amazon Vine page on sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-17"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-18"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-19"/>

The fee is per parent product, and child variations add nothing. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-20"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-21"/> Billing is deferred: you are not charged until 30 days after enrollment and after your first review is published. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-22"/> If no Vine review arrives within 90 days of enrollment, you are not charged at all. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-23"/> New sellers who enroll a brand in Brand Registry receive a $200 Vine credit. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-28"/>

The enrollment fee is not the full cost: Voices receive the units free of charge, <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-02"/> so the cost of the units you give away comes on top. Amazon's Vine page does not state how FBA fees apply to Vine units; check your Seller Central fee reports after the first claims.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): 10 units or 11?">
Inputs: one parent product in three colors (child variations), landed cost $8 per unit, US store.

- 10 units: $75 fee + 10 × $8 = $155, or $15.50 per unit offered. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-18"/>
- 11 units: $200 fee + 11 × $8 = $288, or about $26.18 per unit offered. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-19"/>
- 30 units: $200 fee + 30 × $8 = $440, or about $14.67 per unit offered.
- The three colors cost nothing extra, because the fee is per parent product. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-21"/>

In this example, 11 units has the highest cost per unit offered, and 30 units the lowest. Any FBA fees on the units are excluded.
</Callout>

## Timing: enroll before you launch

An unlaunched product can enroll as soon as it has a customer-ready FBA listing. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-13"/> Amazon recommends enrolling, with inventory available in its network, at least three weeks before your planned launch date. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-14"/>

Work backward from launch day. If your launch is fixed, the three-week lead time applies to inventory being available, not to the shipment leaving your supplier, so the inbound transit to Amazon's warehouses sits before that window. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-14"/>

Stopping is a one-way step: it does not guarantee that reviews of already-claimed units stay unpublished, and the product cannot be enrolled again. So check the listing before you enroll. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-26"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-27"/>

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Amazon's rule |
|---|---|
| Enrolling a bundle listing | Bundled products are not eligible |
| Enrolling a merchant-fulfilled listing | The listing must use FBA |
| Enrolling before inventory arrives | Units must reach Amazon fulfillment centers to ship to Voices |
| Waiting until reviews pile up | Products need fewer than 30 reviews to enroll |
| Paying for each color or size separately | The fee is per parent product; child products cost nothing extra |
| Planning to contact reviewers | Amazon fulfills Vine through FBA so Voices' contact information is not disclosed |

## What Amazon says Vine does, and the wider review climate

Amazon states, citing its internal data, that Vine can boost sales by an average of 30%. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-32"/> Treat that as Amazon's average across products, not a projection for yours.

Amazon also reports that it proactively blocked hundreds of millions of suspected fake reviews in 2025 <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-33"/> and that its legal actions that year shut down more than 100 websites attempting to facilitate fake reviews and scams targeting its store. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-34"/>

<Callout type="note" title="Scope">
This page covers Amazon's published Vine terms for sellers. Whether a separate review-generation practice is lawful is a question for the FTC's rule and, if needed, a lawyer; Amazon's own review policies are published in Seller Central and should be checked there.
</Callout>

## How to confirm it before you enroll

Re-check the fee table, the 30-review ceiling and the billing rules on Amazon's Vine page on sell.amazon.com, since fees can change. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-17"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-09"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-22"/> Then confirm in Seller Central, under Advertising, that your product shows as eligible before you ship units for it. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-16"/>
