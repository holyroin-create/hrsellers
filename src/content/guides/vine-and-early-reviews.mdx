Amazon Vine is Amazon's program for collecting reviews on your products, even before launch: reviewers called Vine Voices request enrolled units free of charge and post honest, unbiased reviews. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-01"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-02"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-03"/> In the US the fee is $0 for up to 2 units, $75 for 3 to 10 and $200 for 11 to 30, per parent product. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-17"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-18"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-19"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-20"/> The real decisions are how many units to offer and when to enroll, because the inventory has to be in Amazon's network before reviewers can claim it.

What the law says about getting reviews outside Vine is covered in [the fake review rule guide](/guides/review-manipulation-risk/), not on this page.

## Who the reviewers are

Vine Voices are chosen for the insightfulness of the reviews they have already published in Amazon's store. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-04"/> Amazon matches your product with Voices who have purchase history and expertise in your category. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-05"/> Amazon fulfills all Vine products through FBA so that the Voices' contact information is not disclosed. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-30"/> Amazon describes the reviews as honest and unbiased opinions, <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-03"/> so enrolling buys access to reviewers, not a particular rating.

*Source: Amazon, Amazon Vine page on sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-08"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-06"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-07"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-09"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-10"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-12"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-35"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-11"/>

The fewer-than-30-reviews ceiling makes Vine a launch tool: once a listing has 30 reviews it can no longer enroll. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-09"/> Bundles are excluded outright, as are adult and digital products. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-11"/>

Vine runs in the US, Canada, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Australia and Japan stores. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-29"/> The Vine dashboard in Seller Central shows products Amazon recommends enrolling and their potential sales lift. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-37"/>

## What it costs and when you are charged

| Units offered per parent product | US fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 2 | $0 |
| 3–10 | $75 |
| 11–30 | $200 |

*Source: Amazon, Amazon Vine page on sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-17"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-18"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-19"/>

Selling in more than one store? The same tiers are priced in local currency:

| Store | 3–10 units | 11–30 units |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | CAD 100 | CAD 240 |
| UK | GBP 60 | GBP 140 |
| European Union | EUR 70 | EUR 170 |
| Japan | JPY 10,000 | JPY 22,000 |

*Source: Amazon, Amazon Vine page on sell.amazon.com (fee footnotes), checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-39"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-40"/>

The fee is per parent product, and child variations add nothing. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-20"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-21"/> Billing is deferred: you are not charged until 30 days after enrollment and after your first review is published. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-22"/> If no Vine review arrives within 90 days of enrollment, you are not charged at all. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-23"/> New sellers who enroll a brand in Brand Registry receive a $200 Vine credit. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-28"/>

The enrollment fee is not the full cost: Voices receive the units free of charge, <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-02"/> so the cost of the units you give away comes on top. Amazon's Vine page does not state how FBA fees apply to Vine units; check your Seller Central fee reports after the first claims.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): 10 units or 11?">
Inputs: one parent product in three colors (child variations), landed cost $8 per unit, US store.

- 10 units: $75 fee + 10 × $8 = $155, or $15.50 per unit offered. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-18"/>
- 11 units: $200 fee + 11 × $8 = $288, or about $26.18 per unit offered. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-19"/>
- 30 units: $200 fee + 30 × $8 = $440, or about $14.67 per unit offered.
- The three colors cost nothing extra, because the fee is per parent product. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-21"/>

In this example, 11 units has the highest cost per unit offered, and 30 units the lowest. Any FBA fees on the units are excluded.
</Callout>

## Timing: enroll before you launch

An unlaunched product can enroll as soon as it has a customer-ready FBA listing <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-13"/> and a launch date set in the future. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-36"/> Amazon recommends enrolling, with inventory available in its network, at least three weeks before your planned launch date. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-14"/>

Work backward from launch day. If your launch is fixed, the three-week lead time applies to inventory being available, not to the shipment leaving your supplier, so the inbound transit to Amazon's warehouses sits before that window. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-14"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): one launch on the calendar">
Inputs: planned launch Monday, March 29, 2027; 20 units offered in the US; first Vine review published April 20.

- Enroll and have the units received at Amazon by Monday, March 8 at the latest: three weeks (21 days) before launch. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-14"/>
- Fee: 20 units falls in the 11–30 tier, so $200 for the parent product. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-19"/>
- Billing: not before 30 days after enrollment (April 7) and not before the first review (April 20), so the charge comes after April 20. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-22"/>
- No-review cutoff: if no Vine review had arrived within 90 days of March 8 (by June 6), there would be no charge. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-23"/>
</Callout>

## Changing your mind: cancel versus stop

  {if:"You canceled before any claims", then:"You can re-enroll the product later", claim:"vine-and-early-reviews-38"},
  {if:"One or more units have been claimed", then:"You can only stop enrollment, which prevents further claims", claim:"vine-and-early-reviews-25"},
  {if:"You stopped enrollment", then:"The product cannot be re-enrolled in Vine", claim:"vine-and-early-reviews-26"},

The difference matters: canceling before any claim keeps re-enrollment open, <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-38"/> while stopping after a claim is one-way. It does not guarantee that reviews of already-claimed units stay unpublished, and the product cannot be enrolled again, so check the listing before you enroll. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-26"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-27"/>

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Amazon's rule |
|---|---|
| Enrolling a bundle listing | Bundled products are not eligible |
| Enrolling a merchant-fulfilled listing | The listing must use FBA |
| Enrolling before inventory arrives | Units must reach Amazon fulfillment centers to ship to Voices |
| Waiting until reviews pile up | Products need fewer than 30 reviews to enroll |
| Paying for each color or size separately | The fee is per parent product; child products cost nothing extra |
| Planning to contact reviewers | Amazon fulfills Vine through FBA so Voices' contact information is not disclosed |

## Vine and the federal review rule

The FTC's rule on consumer reviews is codified at 16 CFR Part 465. Its definition of purchasing a consumer review includes providing something of value such as money, products, discounts or coupons in exchange for a review. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-41"/> Three of its provisions, set next to Amazon's description of Vine:

| Provision | What it prohibits | How Amazon describes Vine |
|---|---|---|
| § 465.4 | Compensation or incentives conditioned, expressly or by implication, on reviews expressing a particular sentiment | Voices share "honest, unbiased opinions" |
| § 465.5(a) | Officer or manager reviews without a clear and conspicuous disclosure of the relationship | Amazon selects and matches the Voices |
| § 465.5(c) | Officers or managers soliciting or demanding reviews from relatives, employees or agents, in stated conditions | Sellers do not receive Voices' contact information |

*Source: 16 CFR Part 465 (govinfo, CFR 2025 edition) and Amazon's Vine page, checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-42"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-44"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-43"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-03"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-05"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-30"/>

The table sets the rule's text next to Amazon's description of the program; it is not a finding that any particular practice complies. The [fake review rule guide](/guides/review-manipulation-risk/) covers these sections in full.

## Reading the reviews once they arrive

Sellers using software connected through the Selling Partner API can follow what reviews say, not only how many there are. The Customer Feedback API returns an item's ten most positive and ten most negative review topics, <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-46"/> each of which can carry a starRatingImpact, the topic's positive or negative effect on the ASIN's star rating. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-47"/> It also returns positive and negative review trends for the past six months. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-45"/>

## What Amazon says Vine does, and the wider review climate

Amazon states, citing its internal data, that Vine can boost sales by an average of 30%. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-32"/> Treat that as Amazon's average across products, not a projection for yours.

Amazon also reports that it proactively blocked hundreds of millions of suspected fake reviews in 2025 <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-33"/> and that its legal actions that year shut down more than 100 websites attempting to facilitate fake reviews and scams targeting its store. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-34"/>

<Callout type="note" title="Scope">
This page covers Amazon's published Vine terms for sellers. Whether a separate review-generation practice is lawful is a question for the FTC's rule and, if needed, a lawyer; Amazon's own review policies are published in Seller Central and should be checked there.
</Callout>

## How to confirm it before you enroll

Re-check the fee table (including the footnoted fees for other stores), the 30-review ceiling and the billing rules on Amazon's Vine page on sell.amazon.com, since fees can change. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-17"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-09"/> <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-22"/> Then confirm in Seller Central, under Advertising, that your product shows as eligible before you ship units for it. <C id="vine-and-early-reviews-16"/>
