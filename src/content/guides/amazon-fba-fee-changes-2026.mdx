Amazon's 2026 fee year had three moments that matter for your margin: the annual update that took effect January 15, 2026, an FBA fuel and logistics surcharge added in April 2026, and the 2027 update announced November 5, 2026, which changes storage fees from February 1, 2027. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-02"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-10"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-12"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-14"/> The practical question is which of your products are exposed to each change before the January 15 and February 1 dates.

## The January 15, 2026 update in one paragraph

Amazon published its 2026 update to US referral and FBA fees on October 15, 2025. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-01"/> Unless otherwise noted, all changes took effect January 15, 2026. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-02"/> The headline: "In 2026, FBA fees will increase by an average of $0.08 per unit sold, or less than 0.5% of an average item's selling price." <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-03"/> Amazon added that there would be no new FBA fee types in 2026 and that sellers had at least 90 days before any increase took effect. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-04"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-05"/> It also noted the increase came "on top of no increase in US Referral and FBA fees in 2025." <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-06"/>

The word that matters is *average*. Amazon says FBA costs depend on the products you sell and the services you use, and that fulfillment costs are based on the weights and dimensions of the product. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-19"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-20"/> Amazon also described "more granularity in our fee structure so we can offer lower fees where our costs are lower." <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-22"/> An average of $0.08 across all units tells you little about one SKU; your exact rate has to be checked in Amazon's fee summary inside Seller Central.

## What Amazon told sellers to do about it

The 2026 announcement named three ways to reach lower fees: updating product packaging, selecting lower cost inbound shipment options, and maintaining healthy inventory levels. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-07"/> Each maps to a fee line:

- **Packaging** changes the weight and dimensions that set the fulfillment fee. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-20"/>
- **Inbound shipment options** set what you pay for inbound placement; see [the inbound placement split arithmetic](/guides/inbound-placement-split-arithmetic/).
- **Inventory levels**: see [FBA storage fee layers](/guides/fba-storage-four-layers/) and [the low-inventory-level fee](/guides/low-inventory-level-fee/).

For measurement, Amazon said the Revenue Calculator and the Fee and Economics Preview report were updated with the 2026 rates, and pointed to a Profit Analytics dashboard that shows how fee changes affect each product. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-08"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-09"/> Amazon's public pricing pages also say you can get an estimate of upcoming fee changes for both seller-fulfilled and FBA products. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-21"/>

## April 2026: the fuel and logistics surcharge

Although the 2026 update said there would be no new FBA fee types for the year, Amazon's 2027 announcement says costs, especially fuel and line-haul trucking, "have remained elevated since we introduced the surcharge in April 2026." <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-10"/> In the 2027 announcement Amazon said the FBA fuel and logistics surcharge remains unchanged and that it will keep reviewing it as conditions change. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-10"/> The announcement does not state the surcharge's rate; check it in Seller Central before you model 2026 or 2027 unit costs.

## The 2027 update, announced November 5, 2026

Amazon said it shared the 2027 changes so sellers have more than two months to plan before most of them take effect. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-12"/>

**Fulfillment fees.** "On average, FBA fulfillment fees will increase by $0.19 per unit sold, approximately 0.8% of the average item's selling price." <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-11"/> Amazon says fees for some products will increase and others will decrease. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-13"/> Most changes take effect January 15, 2027. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-12"/>

**Storage.** Effective February 1, 2027, Amazon will eliminate the storage utilization surcharge, and it will remove the aged inventory surcharge and combine it with monthly storage fees into a single, age-based rate. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-14"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-15"/>

**Low-inventory-level fee.** Amazon says the fee is assessed today using trailing 30- and 90-day sales. Effective February 1 (the sentence gives no year), it will apply only when inventory falls below 28 days of supply, based on actual shipments in the four weeks before and after the assessment date. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-16"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-23"/>

**Other lines.** The announcement lists further updates for small and large standard-size items, items priced above $50, "Buy X, Get Y" promotions, Multichannel Fulfillment and Amazon Warehousing and Distribution, with the detail in the fee summary inside Seller Central. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-17"/>

## Worked example (hypothetical): what an "average" means for your account

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical)">
Inputs: a seller ships 3,000 FBA units a month. Apply Amazon's published averages only — $0.08 per unit for 2026 and $0.19 per unit for 2027 — as if every unit moved by exactly the average. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-03"/> <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-11"/>

- 2026: 3,000 × $0.08 = $240 more per month, or $2,880 a year.
- 2027: 3,000 × $0.19 = $570 more per month on top of the 2026 level, or $6,840 a year.
- Combined change versus 2025 rates: $810 a month, or $9,720 a year.
</Callout>

This is a sizing exercise, not a forecast. Because Amazon says some products' fees rise and others fall, an individual catalog can move more or less than the average, or down. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-13"/> Replace the averages with each SKU's actual old and new fee from Seller Central before you reprice.

## How to confirm the fees on your own products

Amazon's public announcements give dates and averages; the fee tables that settle your exact cost are inside Seller Central. Re-check these sources:

- Amazon's 2026 announcement on sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com ("Update to U.S. Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon fees for 2026"). <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-02"/>
- Amazon's 2027 announcement, published November 5, 2026, on the same site. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-12"/>
- The fee estimate tools Amazon describes at sell.amazon.com/pricing/estimate, including the estimate of upcoming fee changes. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-21"/>
- In Seller Central: the 2026 and 2027 US referral and FBA fee summary pages, the Fee and Economics Preview report and the Revenue Calculator. <C id="amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026-08"/>

This page summarizes Amazon's published announcements; it does not resolve a charge you believe is wrong. A question about a charge on a specific shipment or SKU has to be raised with Amazon through your seller account.
