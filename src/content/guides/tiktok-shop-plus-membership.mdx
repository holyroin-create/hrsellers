Sellers asking what TikTok Shop Plus means for them should know the starting point: we could not find any public TikTok seller page, policy or terms document that describes Shop Plus, its price or its benefits, so this page does not describe them. What TikTok does publish is how its referral fee treats discounts that TikTok funds, and how it tells sellers about fee changes. Those two documented rules are what a seller can rely on today; check Seller Center for any program-specific terms.

## What TikTok publishes about platform-funded discounts

TikTok's US seller portal lists "TikTok Shop-funded discounts and campaigns" among its incentives for sellers <C id="tiktok-shop-plus-membership-01"/>. On the fee side, the referral fee is the rate multiplied by customer payment plus platform discount minus tax <C id="tiktok-shop-plus-membership-02"/>. In TikTok's own example, a $5 platform discount is added back to a $102 customer payment, and $2 of tax is removed, for a 6% fee of $6.30 <C id="tiktok-shop-plus-membership-03"/>.

The practical reading: when a discount is recorded as a platform discount, your referral fee is charged as if the buyer had also paid that discount. Whether any membership discount is recorded as a platform discount is not stated on a public page.

Inputs: $40 item, $4 discount funded by TikTok and recorded as a platform discount, no tax, 6% rate.

Buyer pays $36. Fee base: $36 + $4 − $0 = $40. Referral fee: $2.40 — the same as with no discount. <C id="tiktok-shop-plus-membership-02"/>

## How you would hear about a seller-side change

The Seller Terms of Service allow TikTok to amend the referral fee at its discretion <C id="tiktok-shop-plus-membership-04"/> and say sellers are informed by email or through Seller Center <C id="tiktok-shop-plus-membership-05"/>. If a membership program ever changes what sellers pay, that is the channel to watch.
