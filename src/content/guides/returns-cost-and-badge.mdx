Amazon charges sellers for returns in two published ways. For FBA orders on Amazon, the returns processing fee is "charged on orders when Amazon provides a customer with free return shipping." <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-01"/> For Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) orders from other channels, Amazon publishes a flat return fee by size tier and weight — $4.70 for a large standard item over 1 lb to 2 lb. <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-10"/> <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-14"/> The FBA fee amounts, and Amazon's rules for return-related labels and badges shown on product pages, are published inside Seller Central and should be checked there. This page covers what is public: when the FBA fee applies, how its scope has changed, the MCF return fee table, the waivers, and the reports you can use to track it.

## When the FBA returns processing fee applies

FBA's fulfillment service already includes returns handling. Amazon describes fulfillment costs as covering picking, packing and shipping, "as well as handling customer service and returns." <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-02"/> The returns processing fee is a separate charge, and its trigger is specific: Amazon gives the customer free return shipping. <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-01"/> The fee is tied to how the return is offered to the shopper, not simply to whether a unit comes back.

Its scope has widened over time:

The 2023 announcement described the expansion this way: Amazon would "expand the returns processing fee to apply to high return-rate products in all categories, excluding apparel and shoes." <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-04"/> It added that the fee would "only apply to products that have the highest return rates relative to other products in their category," starting June 1, 2024. <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-05"/> The benchmark is your category, not an absolute rate. The public announcement does not give the thresholds.

## MCF return fees by size

For MCF, Amazon publishes the full table. "Amazon charges return processing fees for items returned through MCF," and the fees are "based on the product's size tier and weight and include return logistics transportation, regrading, and restocking." <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-10"/> <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-11"/>

| Size tier and weight | Return fee per unit |
|---|---|
| Small standard, up to 4 oz | $3.22 |
| Small standard, over 12 oz to 16 oz | $3.55 |
| Large standard, over 1 lb to 2 lb | $4.70 |
| Large standard, over 3 lb to 20 lb | $5.60 + $0.40 per lb above 3 lb |
| Small bulky, up to 30 lb | $8.65 + $0.40 per lb above 2 lb |

## What a return rate costs per unit sold

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): spreading the return fee over sales">
Inputs: 100 units shipped through MCF; return fees from Amazon's published MCF table; return-related refunds and resale value left out. Large standard, 1–2 lb, 8 returns: 8 × $4.70 = $37.60, or $0.38 per unit sold. Same item, 15 returns: 15 × $4.70 = $70.50, or $0.71 per unit sold. Small standard, up to 4 oz, 8 returns: 8 × $3.22 = $25.76, or $0.26 per unit sold.
</Callout>

The example covers only the return fee, not the value of the returned unit. To see how a return fee adds to peak-season storage and fulfillment charges on a single unit, see our [peak season cost stack](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/).

## Waivers for new products

The FBA New Selection program covers returns for new listings. Amazon says it will "waive return processing costs for up to 200 units of each parent ASIN." <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-06"/> Returned items "have to be received at a fulfillment center within 120 days of the first date the inventory was received." <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-07"/> Eligibility requires parent ASINs that have never been received at an Amazon fulfillment center, and an Inventory Performance Index (IPI) of 300 or higher. <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-09"/> <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-08"/>

## What happens to returned units

Amazon says "the vast majority of returns are resold as new or used, returned to selling partners, liquidated, or donated." <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-17"/> Items resold by Amazon are graded into one of four conditions: Like New, Very Good, Good or Acceptable. <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-18"/> For your own FBA stock, you can have Amazon return, dispose of or liquidate inventory in a fulfillment center for a per-item charge. <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-22"/>

## How to confirm the returns charges on your account

Start with Amazon's FBA page at sell.amazon.com/fulfillment-by-amazon, which describes when the returns processing fee applies, and the MCF rate card at supplychain.amazon.com/docs/rate-card for MCF return fees. <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-01"/> <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-10"/> Match the fees on your settlement against the FBA Returns Report for the same period. <C id="returns-cost-and-badge-19"/> This page summarizes Amazon's published terms. A disputed returns charge or reimbursement is settled through a Seller Central support case.
