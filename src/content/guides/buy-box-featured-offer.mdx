When several sellers offer the same product, Amazon selects one offer for the Add to Cart button and calls it the Featured Offer. The levers Amazon names are concrete: keep the offer in stock, deliver fast, and price it competitively, using the expected price Amazon computes as a guide, without dropping below what your margin can carry. This page sets out what Amazon publishes about each of those levers and how to read the signals that tell you which one failed.

## Featured Offer, Offer Display, Buy Box: the terms

Amazon calls the prominent area at the top of a product detail page the Offer Display, sometimes called the Buy Box. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-01"/> It contains one or more Featured Offers. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-02"/> The Featured Offer is the offer selected when a shopper chooses Add to Cart. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-03"/>

"One or more" is literal. Amazon's offers data says there can be up to two Buy Box winners per ASIN at any time, one eligible for Prime and one not. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-13"/> Featured offers can also be broken out by customer membership, Prime and non-Prime shoppers. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-23"/> So "who has the Buy Box" can have more than one answer, depending on who is looking.

## What Amazon says decides eligibility

Amazon's developer documentation puts it in one sentence: to be Featured Offer eligible, you must provide great prices, availability, and delivery speed. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-04"/> Amazon's seller pages expand each element.

### FBA or self-fulfilled?

FBA products are eligible for free, two-day shipping through Prime, <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-28"/> and Amazon handles customer service and returns for those orders. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-29"/> Amazon also says fulfilling orders yourself can be just as effective at increasing your chances of becoming the Featured Offer. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-07"/> For FBA fee levels, see [Amazon referral fees](/guides/amazon-referral-fees/) and Amazon's pricing pages.

## Price: both too high and too low

Amazon lists a price that is too high as a reason an offer may not be featured <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-09"/> — and a price that is too low as well. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-10"/> It can also happen that no available offer is eligible at all. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-11"/>

The ceiling is set partly by prices outside Amazon. An offer becomes ineligible when its total price (price plus shipping) is higher than the Competitive External Price, if one is available, or higher than recent prices. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-22"/> Amazon may also use a product's list price when deciding whether to feature an offer. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-24"/>

The external benchmark has a name in Amazon's pricing data: CompetitivePriceThreshold, based on competitive prices from other retailers, excluding other Amazon sellers. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-37"/> The offer may be ineligible if your price plus shipping, minus any Amazon Points, is greater than it. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-38"/> The PRICING_HEALTH notification schema includes these reference prices:

| Reference price in a Pricing Health alert | Amazon's definition |
|---|---|
| competitivePriceThreshold | The competitive price threshold from external competitors of Amazon <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-45"/> |
| retailOfferPrice | The 14-day maximum of the retail offer price <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-46"/> |
| msrpPrice | The manufacturer suggested retail price for the ASIN <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-47"/> |
| averageSellingPrice | The average selling price of the item <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-48"/> |

*Source: Amazon Selling Partner API models, PRICING_HEALTH notification schema, checked Nov 9, 2026.*

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): shipping counts toward the ceiling">
Inputs: a self-fulfilled offer at $24.99 plus $4.99 shipping, no Amazon Points, and a competitive price threshold of $27.50. The total Amazon compares is $24.99 + $4.99 = $29.98, which is $2.48 over the threshold, so the offer may be ineligible even though the item price alone is below it. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-38"/> Repricing to $26.99 with free shipping gives a total of $26.99, under the threshold, and collects $2.99 less per order ($29.98 − $26.99). Whether that clears your margin is the same calculation as in the expected-price example below.
</Callout>

## Reading Amazon's signals

Amazon gives sellers three ways to see where an offer stands.

**Manage All Inventory.** The Price and shipping costs column shows whether your item is the Featured Offer. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-27"/>

**Pricing Health.** This view lists your offers that are not currently eligible to become the Featured Offer. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-26"/> The same event is available to software as a PRICING_HEALTH notification, sent when an offer is disqualified. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-31"/> In the offers data, a flag shows whether the seller is eligible to win the Buy Box. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-14"/>

**Featured offer expected price (FOEP).** Amazon computes the listing price at or below which a seller can expect to become the featured offer, before applicable promotions. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-15"/> It is defined as an item price, <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-30"/> and it is not a guarantee, because competing offers might change. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-16"/> Software can request it for up to 40 SKUs at once. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-18"/>

## The offer fields that explain a lost Featured Offer

Amazon's offers data for an ASIN carries the fields below. Comparing them for your own offer and the current winner can point to the lever that failed.

| Field | What Amazon says it is | What to compare |
|---|---|---|
| IsBuyBoxWinner | True when the offer is currently in the Buy Box <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-35"/> | Whether yours or a rival's is true (up to two can be) |
| BuyBoxEligibleOffers | Count of Buy Box-eligible offers by condition and fulfillment channel <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-36"/> | How many offers you are actually competing with |
| LandedPrice | ListingPrice + Shipping − Points <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-40"/> | Your landed price vs. the winner's, not item price alone |
| SuggestedLowerPricePlusShipping | Based on historical selling prices, recent Buy Box-eligible prices and customer input <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-39"/> | Whether you are above what has recently won |
| ShippingTime | Minimum and maximum hours until the item likely ships <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-41"/> | Your handling time vs. the winner's |
| SellerPositiveFeedbackRating | Positive feedback percentage over the past 365 days <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-42"/> | Service history, one of the non-price requirements |
| glanceViewWeightPercentage | A segment's share of the ASIN's total glance views <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-43"/> | Whether you win the segments most shoppers see |

*Source: Amazon Selling Partner API models, Product Pricing API v0 and 2022-05-01, checked Nov 9, 2026.* The competitive summary lists up to 20 lowest priced offers, <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-44"/> so on crowded listings you may not see every competitor.

An OFFER_NOT_FOUND result points to the offer itself rather than its price. Check listing status and issues with the steps in [why an Amazon listing disappears](/guides/listing-disappeared/).

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): is the expected price worth matching?">
Inputs: your item price is $27.99, the FOEP is $25.49, and your costs before Amazon's referral fee total $15.00. Matching the FOEP cuts revenue per unit by $2.50 ($27.99 − $25.49). If the referral fee is 15% (assumed rate for this example), it falls from $4.20 to $3.82, so the margin before other costs goes from $8.79 ($27.99 − $4.20 − $15.00) to $6.67 ($25.49 − $3.82 − $15.00). Amazon does not guarantee the featured offer at that price, <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-16"/> so the question is whether the extra units at $6.67 beat fewer units at $8.79.
</Callout>

## Tracking your Featured Offer share over time

A single check shows who holds the Featured Offer now; a trend shows whether a change worked. The Sales and Traffic report (GET_SALES_AND_TRAFFIC_REPORT) aggregates by day, week or month, and per ASIN at parent, child or SKU level. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-33"/> Its buyBoxPercentage field is the percentage of page views where the buy box appeared for customers to add your product to their cart. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-32"/> A separate buyBoxPercentageB2B field covers page views by Amazon Business customers and is populated only when the seller is a B2B seller. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-34"/> Pulling it by SKU and by week before and after a price, stock or shipping change shows whether your share moved.

## Automating price changes

Amazon's Automate Pricing tool is included free in the Professional selling plan. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-25"/> A repricing rule only moves the price; it cannot fix stock, delivery or service gaps, which Amazon treats as separate requirements. <C id="buy-box-featured-offer-12"/>
