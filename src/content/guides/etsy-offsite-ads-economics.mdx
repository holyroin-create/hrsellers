Etsy's Offsite Ads fee is an advertising charge on specific orders, not on every sale. Etsy advertises listings on external sites and charges a fee only if that advertising directly results in a sale <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-02" />: an order placed from your shop within 30 days of a buyer clicking an Offsite Ad <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-03" />. The standard fee is 15% of that order <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-04" />, falling to 12% for shops with $10,000 or more in sales over the prior 365 days <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-05" />, and it never exceeds $100 on a single order <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-01" />.

On a $25 item with free shipping, that is $3.75 at 15% or $3.00 at 12%, added to the transaction, processing and listing fees the order already pays. Whether you can avoid it depends on one number: your shop's trailing 365-day sales.

Two details follow from Etsy's wording. First, the window is tied to your shop, not only to the advertised listing: Etsy counts orders from your shop within 30 days of the click <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-03" />. Second, Offsite Ads and Etsy Ads do not stack on the same order: if the buyer's last click before ordering was an Etsy Ad, only the Etsy Ads fee applies <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-06" />.

## What the fee is charged on

Etsy calculates the fee as a percentage of the total order amount, which is the displayed price for each listing plus the amount you charge for shipping and gift wrapping <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-07" />. For sellers in the US, the fee does not apply to sales tax <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-08" />.

So free shipping and charged shipping are treated differently. Worked example (hypothetical): a $20.00 item with $5.00 shipping and a $25.00 item with free shipping both have a $25.00 total order amount, and both pay $3.75 at 15%.

## The two rates and the cap

| Condition | Rate | |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (shop under $10,000 in sales over the prior 365 days) | 15% of attributed order | <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-04" /> |
| Shop at $10,000 or more over the prior 365 days | 12% of attributed order | <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-05" /> |
| Cap on any single attributed order | $100 | <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-01" /> |

*Source: Etsy, Fees & Payments Policy, Offsite Ads section, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-04" />

The cap means the percentage stops growing above a certain order size. At 15%, $100 is reached at an order total of $666.67 ($100 divided by 0.15). At 12%, it is reached at $833.33 ($100 divided by 0.12).

## The full fee stack on an attributed order

An attributed order still pays Etsy's ordinary fees: the 6.5% transaction fee <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-09" />, payment processing of 3% + $0.25 for a US bank account <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-10" />, and the $0.20 listing fee <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-11" />.

"All other Etsy fees" is $0.20 listing, 6.5% transaction and 3% + $0.25 processing. Below the cap, an attributed order costs roughly 25% of price in total Etsy fees at these settings, and more on cheap items because of the fixed $0.45. Above $666.67 the Offsite Ads part stops growing, so the share falls.

Since only some orders are attributed, the average cost per order depends on what share of your orders come through Offsite Ads. Worked example (hypothetical): if 10% of $25 orders are attributed at 15%, the Offsite Ads cost averages $0.375 per order across all orders; if 25% are attributed, it averages about $0.94.

## Opt-out conditions as Etsy states them

Etsy's policy sets out the opt-out in three parts:

1. **Under the threshold, opting out is allowed.** A shop under $10,000 USD in sales over the prior 365 days pays 15% unless it opts out, and the opt-out lasts for the duration the shop stays under that threshold <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-13" />.
2. **The threshold ends the opt-out.** The 15% fee and the opt-out apply until or unless the shop at any time meets the $10,000 USD threshold <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-14" />. A shop that reaches it is required to participate and is charged 12% <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-05" />.
3. **The 12% rate stays for the life of the shop.** Etsy states the shop will still only be charged a 12% advertising fee for the lifetime of the shop <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-15" />.

The trigger is "at any time" <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-14" />, measured against sales over the prior 365 days <C id="etsy-offsite-ads-economics-05" />, so a strong season can move a shop across it. Worked example (hypothetical): a shop averaging $700 a month in sales is at $8,400 over 365 days; a holiday quarter adding $1,700 above its usual sales would take it to $10,100 and end the option to opt out.
