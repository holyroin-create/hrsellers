Congress repealed the de minimis privilege for commercial shipments in **Section 70531 of Public Law 119-21**, and the repeal takes effect on **July 1, 2027**. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-01"/> <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-07"/> CBP describes it as terminating the de minimis exemption on that date. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-02"/> For sellers, the day-to-day effect is smaller than it sounds. Executive Order 14388 already keeps de minimis unavailable regardless of value, origin, transport mode or entry method, so the change is mostly to the legal basis. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-17"/> The parts still open are the entry rules CBP is writing for the period after the repeal.

## What Section 70531 actually says

Section 70531 is titled "Modifications to de minimis entry privilege for commercial shipments." <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-01"/> It has two parts that work on different clocks.

  {label:"What it does", values:["Adds a penalty for misusing de minimis", "Repeals the commercial shipment exception"], claim:"section-321-repeal-2027-08"},
  {label:"Amount or change", values:["Up to $5,000 first violation; up to $10,000 each later one", "Strikes text from section 321(a)(2) of the Tariff Act"], claim:"section-321-repeal-2027-03"},
  {label:"Takes effect", values:["30 days after enactment", "July 1, 2027"], claim:"section-321-repeal-2027-04"}
]} />

**Subsection (a), the penalty.** It adds a civil penalty of up to $5,000 for a first violation and up to $10,000 for each later violation. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-03"/> It took effect 30 days after enactment. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-04"/> CBP says the law was enacted on July 4, 2025. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-02"/> Worked example (our arithmetic): 30 days after July 4, 2025 is August 3, 2025.

**Subsection (b), the repeal.** Its heading is "Repeal of Commercial Shipment Exception." <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-08"/> It amends section 321(a)(2) by striking everything from "of this Act, or" through "subdivision (2); and." <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-05"/> It also repeals the penalty subsection that subsection (a) added. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-06"/> All of subsection (b) takes effect July 1, 2027. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-07"/>

So the penalty is a bridge. It covers the period before the repeal, and it ends on the same day the privilege it polices disappears. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-06"/> <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-07"/>

CBP has said how the two layers fit together. Once the statutory repeal is effective, "the effects of the de minimis suspension belong to the statutory change and not this rule." <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-09"/> In other words, on July 1, 2027 the legal basis moves from CBP's suspension rule to the statute.

Informal entry itself does not depend on Section 321. It is authorized for shipments valued at $2,500 or less under 19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A). <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-18"/> So informal entry does not end when the de minimis privilege does. The FY2027 fee for an automated informal entry not prepared by CBP is $2.77. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-21"/>

## CBP's proposal for the post-repeal entry rules

CBP's October 8, 2026 proposed rule is the clearest sign of what low-value clearance will look like. Its main proposals:

| Proposal | Detail |
|---|---|
| New entry type 13 | "Informal Mail Entry" for mail shipments valued at $2,500 or less |
| Bond for type 11 and 13 filers | All must obtain a basic importation and entry bond |
| Bond breach minimum | New minimum of $1,000 for each breach of a listed bond condition |
| Deliver-to party on type 11 | Required when it differs from the ultimate consignee |
| Comment deadline | December 7, 2026 |

*Source: Federal Register, CBP proposed rule "Low-Value Shipments" (2026-20650), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-11"/> <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-12"/> <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-13"/> <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-14"/> <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-15"/>

The bond proposal is the part most sellers should notice. Today, a small seller may never have held a customs bond. Under the proposal, anyone filing entry type 11 or 13 would need one. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-12"/> The scale explains why CBP is focused on this. It reports 24,427,685 postal entries and 52,450,418 entry type 11 entries from September 1, 2025 through May 31, 2026. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-16"/>

## Foreign sellers: a separate deadline first

A separate CBP rule arrives before the repeal. CBP's foreign importer of record rule takes effect January 5, 2027. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-19"/> Under it, foreign importers of record are prohibited from making informal entry for consumption (where a CBP Form 3461 or 7501 must be filed) unless the foreign importer is a CTPAT validated participant or uses a CTPAT validated and licensed customs broker. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-20"/> If you are a non-US seller who clears low-value parcels in your own name, that date matters to you before July 1, 2027 does.

## Common misreadings of the repeal

| Misreading | What the text says |
|---|---|
| "De minimis is legal until July 1, 2027" | Executive Order 14388 already makes it unavailable regardless of value, origin, mode or entry method <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-17"/> |
| "The $5,000 penalty is permanent" | Subsection (b) repeals the penalty subsection effective July 1, 2027 <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-06"/> |
| "Informal entry ends with de minimis" | Informal entry rests on 19 U.S.C. 1498(a)(1)(A), for shipments of $2,500 or less <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-18"/> |
| "Entry type 13 is already required" | It is a CBP proposal; comments close December 7, 2026 <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-15"/> |
| "The bond rule is final" | CBP has only proposed bonds for all type 11 and 13 filers <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-12"/> |

The last two rows are worth repeating to your team. A proposed rule can change before it is final, and CBP asked for comments on exactly these points. If the bond requirement or the $1,000 breach minimum would change your costs, the comment window is the time to say so. <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-13"/> <C id="section-321-repeal-2027-15"/>
