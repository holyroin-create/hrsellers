Amazon applies a fuel and logistics surcharge on both of its fulfillment services. For Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF), the rate card states that "a 3.5% fuel and logistics-related surcharge will be applied to MCF fulfillment fees in the US." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-01"/> For FBA orders on Amazon, Amazon's 2027 fee announcement confirms the surcharge exists: it was introduced in April 2026 and "remains unchanged" for 2027. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-22"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-21"/> The FBA rate and the fees it applies to are stated inside Seller Central (search "fuel and logistics surcharge" there); of the public Amazon pages cited here, only the MCF rate card states a percentage. Put the surcharge into every fulfillment-fee line of your cost model, then check it against Amazon's fee reports for your SKUs.

## What Amazon says the surcharge is for

Amazon's MCF rate card explains the reasoning in three steps. First: "Elevated fuel and logistics costs have increased operating expenses across the industry." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-02"/> Second, Amazon says it had absorbed those increases until then. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-03"/> Third, the 3.5% applies "to help offset a portion of these costs, consistent with other major carriers." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-04"/>

## The FBA side: what the 2027 announcement confirms

Amazon's 2027 announcement adds three facts for FBA sellers. The surcharge stays as it is: "The FBA fuel and logistics surcharge remains unchanged." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-21"/> Amazon dates it and gives the reason it stays: costs, "especially fuel and line-haul trucking, have remained elevated since we introduced the surcharge in April 2026." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-22"/> Amazon also says "we'll keep reviewing the surcharge as conditions change." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-23"/>

The regular fee schedule also moves in 2027: Amazon says FBA fulfillment fees will rise by an average of $0.19 per unit sold, about 0.8% of the average item's selling price, with most changes effective January 15, 2027. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-24"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-25"/> To see the effect on your own products, Amazon points to its new Fee Change Comparison dashboard. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-32"/>

## Which MCF fees the 3.5% applies to

Amazon's wording names **fulfillment fees** as the base. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-01"/> MCF fulfillment fees themselves depend on product size, shipping weight, units per order and delivery speed. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-07"/> Storage, labeling, inbound and removal fees are a separate group that, for MCF, "will continue to be the same as Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA)." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-06"/> The published surcharge sentence does not name them.

### Other MCF surcharges on the same order

The fuel and logistics surcharge is one of several add-ons on the MCF rate card. For the others, Amazon states the amount and, in one case, the order of calculation.

| MCF add-on | Amount on the rate card | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel and logistics surcharge | 3.5% of fulfillment fees | US MCF orders |
| Remote area surcharge | 100% (standard-size); 200% (large bulky and extra-large) | Orders to Alaska, Hawaii, Guam, Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands |
| Lithium battery fee | $0.11 per unit, added to the base fulfillment fee | Lithium batteries and items containing them |
| Amazon Logistics block surcharge | 5%, waived for MCF orders, including Walmart, through January 14, 2027 | When you block Amazon Logistics as carrier |

*Source: Amazon Supply Chain, MCF rate card, checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-01"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-26"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-27"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-19"/>

The card says the Amazon Logistics block surcharge and the remote-area surcharge are calculated after any lithium battery fee is added to the base fulfillment fee. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-18"/> It gives no such ordering rule for the 3.5%, so for battery items and remote addresses, check on an invoice which base the surcharge was applied to.

## What the card leaves open on peak rates

The same card carries tables headed "Peak fulfillment fees," effective October 15, 2026 to January 14, 2027, and does not say whether the listed peak rates already include the 3.5% or whether the 3.5% is added to them. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-05"/> Compare a peak-period MCF invoice with the table to see which way your charges were built. The peak rates themselves are covered in our guide to the [holiday peak fulfillment fee](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/).

The surcharge scales with the fee: heavier and faster shipments carry larger fees, so they absorb more of the 3.5% in dollar terms. On the same assumption, a large standard 1–2 lb order to Hawaii would add both the 100% remote surcharge and the 3.5% — check the invoice to see which was calculated first. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-26"/>

## Amazon has done this before

In a November 17, 2022 message, Amazon wrote that "earlier this year, we introduced a Fuel & Inflation Surcharge for our FBA fees." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-08"/> The same message said Amazon would "remove the separate Fuel & Inflation Surcharge" and raise FBA outbound fee rates by $0.22 on average. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-09"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-11"/> Those changes took effect January 17, 2023, except where noted. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-10"/>

## Where to check the surcharge on your own SKUs

Start with Amazon Supply Chain's MCF rate card at supplychain.amazon.com/docs/rate-card and the 2026 card at supplychain.amazon.com/docs/2026-peak-card for the 3.5% statement, the peak dates and the add-ons. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-01"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-05"/> For the FBA side, read Amazon's 2027 announcement on sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com, then your own data:

- **Fee Preview report.** In Seller Central under Reports, then Fulfillment, then Fee Preview. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-15"/> Through the Selling Partner API it is the FBA Fee Preview Report, with estimated selling and fulfillment fees for FBA inventory with active offers, updated at least once every 72 hours. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-16"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-17"/> For US SKUs, compare `expected-fulfillment-fee-per-unit` with `expected-future-fulfillment-fee-per-unit` and `estimated-fee-total`; the report can be requested only once per day per seller. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-29"/> <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-30"/>
- **Product Fees API.** Amazon says an estimate by seller SKU gives "accurate fees based on real item measurements, but only after they've sent their items to Amazon." <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-31"/>
- **Fee Change Comparison dashboard.** For the 2027 changes at account and product level. <C id="fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026-32"/>

Then compare those estimates with the fees on a recent settlement. This page summarizes Amazon's published statements; if a charge on your account doesn't match them, raise it in a Seller Central support case.
