Amazon Renewed is not a condition you pick in a drop-down; it is an approval. You must already sell repaired or refurbished products, prove it with itemized invoices for at least $50,000 of qualifying refurbished purchases in the previous 90 days (180 days for Home, Tools, Kitchen, or Lawn & Garden products), and show sample units in Excellent cosmetic condition before Amazon lets you list under the Renewed label. <C id="amazon-renewed-03"/> <C id="amazon-renewed-08"/> <C id="amazon-renewed-11"/> If you cannot meet that bar yet, the realistic route is the ordinary Used condition, which Amazon says remains open for pre-owned goods that meet standard listing requirements. <C id="amazon-renewed-21"/>

## What the program covers

Amazon describes Renewed as a way to "repair or refurbish pre-owned electronics, mobile phones, computers, and other products." <C id="amazon-renewed-01"/> Eligible products include phones, cameras, headphones, kitchen appliances, power tools, personal computers, tablets, televisions and video game consoles. <C id="amazon-renewed-02"/> The common thread is that "products must contain electrical or mechanical components that can be replaced or upgraded." <C id="amazon-renewed-02"/> A product with nothing to repair or replace is a poor fit regardless of its condition.

Amazon also frames the program in environmental terms: "Refurbished devices help reduce e-waste." <C id="amazon-renewed-23"/>

## Which seller types can qualify

The application asks you to declare one seller type. Amazon defines six:

| Seller type | Amazon's definition (abridged) |
|---|---|
| Distributor or reseller | Buys from manufacturers or other suppliers, then sells to retailers, businesses or end-consumers |
| Manufacturer or brand owner | Designs, produces and owns the IP rights to the product |
| Brand-authorized refurbisher | Certified by the brand; follows the brand's refurbishment requirements |
| Refurbisher | Sources, inspects, tests and cosmetically restores used products without opening the device |
| Repairer | Has processes set up to repair used or damaged products |
| ITAD provider | Provides ITAD services and refurbishes sets of used parts or products |

*Source: Amazon, Amazon Renewed program page, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="amazon-renewed-09"/>

The type you choose shapes the evidence you need. Manufacturers and brands add "Trademark documentation or proof of enrollment in Amazon Brand Registry." <C id="amazon-renewed-10"/> For how Brand Registry works, see the [Brand Registry guide](/guides/brand-registry-trademark/).

**Invoices.** The core test is "Itemized invoices showing a minimum total value of $50,000 in qualifying refurbished purchases" ($2.5 million for Apple-branded items) in the previous 90 days, or 180 days for products in the Home, Tools, Kitchen, or Lawn & Garden categories. <C id="amazon-renewed-08"/> They "must be itemized with brand and model information for the purchased goods that can be validated online." <C id="amazon-renewed-06"/> Amazon rejects "Documents like retail receipts, order confirmation, or manifests," and invoices issued before a formal transaction, such as sales quotes or pro formas. <C id="amazon-renewed-07"/>

**Images.** Sample images "should show a sample product from all sides," and "If the product has a screen, include a picture with the screen on." The images should also show the product packaging and accessories showing the brand. <C id="amazon-renewed-05"/> They must show "Renewed's cosmetic grade of Excellent condition." <C id="amazon-renewed-11"/> "Mockups or digitally altered images of the product or product packaging will not be accepted as valid proof." <C id="amazon-renewed-13"/>

**Timing.** "We typically provide application review decisions within 10 days of submission." <C id="amazon-renewed-12"/> To check status after 10 days, Amazon points you to Selling Partner Support through a Contact us case. <C id="amazon-renewed-12"/>

## Listing and pricing rules after approval

Three rules are easy to break once you start listing:

- **Title.** "Include “(Renewed)” at the end of the product title." <C id="amazon-renewed-16"/>
- **No warranty talk.** "Don't mention warranties in your title, descriptions, or bullet points." <C id="amazon-renewed-17"/>
- **Brand field.** "Don't use your seller name as the brand or manufacturer, unless your product is a private label." <C id="amazon-renewed-24"/>

On price, "you must list Renewed products at a 5% or more discount than their new product counterpart." <C id="amazon-renewed-15"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical)">
Inputs: a tablet whose new counterpart sells for $329.00. The 5% rule sets a ceiling of $329.00 × 0.95 = $312.55 for your Renewed offer. If your unit cost after refurbishment is $230.00, the most you can keep before fees and shipping is $312.55 − $230.00 = $82.55. If the new price drops to $299.00, the ceiling falls to $284.05 and that room shrinks to $54.05, so reprice whenever the new offer moves.
</Callout>

## Fees

Amazon states: "For a limited time, Renewed products in eligible categories receive a reduced referral fee—no action required." <C id="amazon-renewed-22"/> The public page does not publish the reduced rate, so check the current figure in Seller Central rather than assuming it in your pricing. The Renewed program page lists the Professional plan at $39.99 per month plus selling fees. <C id="amazon-renewed-25"/> Standard category rates are in the [referral fee guide](/guides/amazon-referral-fees/).

## Quality obligations and the guarantee

Before sale, products "must be inspected, repaired, cleaned, tested, and repackaged (if applicable)." <C id="amazon-renewed-19"/> As a Renewed seller, "your company backs all of your refurbished products with the Amazon Renewed Guarantee," and your company is responsible for claims under it. <C id="amazon-renewed-20"/> The guarantee's period and claim terms are set out in Amazon's policy documents, not on the public program page, so read them before you price returns into your margin.

Amazon may buy your products as test buys. Those units come back "through the regular returns process" and "will not affect any performance metrics." <C id="amazon-renewed-26"/> Falling short has a defined consequence: "Failure to meet quality requirements may lead to the deactivation of Renewed selling privileges," and reinstatement "may require the submission of a remediation plan of action." <C id="amazon-renewed-27"/> If that happens, the [account suspension appeal guide](/guides/account-suspension-appeal/) covers how plans of action are structured.

## Common mistakes that stall an application or a listing

| Mistake | What Amazon's page says instead |
|---|---|
| Sending marketplace order confirmations as purchase proof | Receipts, order confirmations and manifests are not accepted |
| Invoices with only "assorted laptops" lines | Lines need brand and model that can be validated online |
| Stock photos or edited renders of the sample | Mockups or digitally altered images are rejected |
| "90-day warranty" in the bullet points | Warranties must not be mentioned in title, description or bullets |
| Your store name in the brand field | Only allowed when the product is your own private label |
| Matching the new price during a sale on the new item | Renewed must stay at least 5% below the new counterpart |
