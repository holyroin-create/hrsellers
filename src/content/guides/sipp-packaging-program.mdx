If your product already arrives in a sturdy box or a thick mailer, Amazon's Ships in Product Packaging (SIPP) program can lower your FBA fulfillment fees, and for bulky items it now also avoids a packaging fee that started on January 15, 2026. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-05"/> <C id="sipp-packaging-program-16"/> The decision turns on three things: whether your packaging meets the size and structure rules, whether you can self-test or need a lab, and whether the per-unit discount outweighs any redesign cost.

## What SIPP is

SIPP lets Fulfillment by Amazon sellers ship products in their own custom-brand packaging. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-01"/> Enrolled FBA orders are delivered in that packaging without any Amazon-added material. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-02"/> Amazon rebranded the program to Ships in Product Packaging, and its materials may still use the older names Frustration-Free Packaging (FFP) and Ships in Own Container (SIOC). <C id="sipp-packaging-program-03"/> Amazon's Buy with Prime documentation states that SIPP and SIOC follow the same packaging certification standards. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-41"/>

Amazon reports 28 million unique products certified for SIPP globally <C id="sipp-packaging-program-37"/> and says 11% of packages globally shipped without additional Amazon packaging in 2025. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-38"/>

SIPP is available to sellers who use FBA and sell into seven marketplaces: the United States, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Spain and the United Kingdom. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-09"/>

The minimum size applies to product and packaging regardless of packaging structure. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-04"/> Extra-large items are excluded because they already ship in product packaging and do not receive SIPP discounts. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-32"/>

## Flexible or rigid: the two packaging types

Amazon recognizes two structures:

| Type | What Amazon requires |
|---|---|
| Flexible SIPP | Flexible exterior such as plastic or paper bags; material at least 2 millimeters thick |
| Rigid SIPP | Rigid, six-sided packaging such as boxes |

*Source: Amazon Packaging, "FBA Sellers," checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="sipp-packaging-program-18"/> <C id="sipp-packaging-program-19"/>

Flexible packaging has one extra test step that checks the material strength of the packaging. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-26"/> If you are choosing between a mailer and a box for a new product, see the [packaging design guide](/guides/packaging-design-fba/) for the label and size decisions that come before certification.

### Grouping rules in detail

Testing one product can cover a group, which keeps lab costs down. The primary product is the heaviest and largest in the group; once it is certified, secondary ASINs can be certified without their own test report. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-36"/> Each secondary product must stay within these limits:

- Up to 25% smaller than the primary in a single dimension or in total volume. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-20"/>
- Up to 25% lighter than the primary. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-21"/>
- Identical packaging type, structure and material. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-23"/>
- The same number of units in a pack. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-22"/>

The pack-quantity rule matters if you sell singles and multipacks of the same item: a 1-pack and a 3-pack fall into different groups. The [bundles and multipacks guide](/guides/bundles-and-multipacks/) covers the labeling side of that choice.

### What fails a test

An item does not qualify if, after testing, it shows visible damage or breakage, loss of function or utility, or a compromised seal or tape. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-25"/>

## What it costs and what it saves

Amazon charges no fee to enroll, but updating packaging to meet the requirements can cost money, <C id="sipp-packaging-program-06"/> <C id="sipp-packaging-program-07"/> and products that cannot be self-tested must go through an ISTA 6 lab test instead. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-08"/>

On the savings side, Amazon offers lower FBA fulfillment fees for enrolled products. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-05"/> The discount rate depends on the product's size and weight as used to charge FBA fulfillment fees. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-12"/> Amazon's packaging site points FBA sellers to fee-reduction charts by size tier and shipping weight to calculate savings. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-42"/> For orders fulfilled through Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) and Buy with Prime, Amazon's Buy with Prime documentation lists these per-unit discounts:

| Size tier | Shipping weight | SIPP discount |
|---|---|---|
| Small standard | 4 oz or less / 4–8 oz / 8–12 oz / 12–16 oz | $0.04 / $0.05 / $0.06 / $0.07 |
| Large standard | 4 oz or less / 4–8 oz / 8–12 oz / 12–16 oz | $0.04 / $0.04 / $0.07 / $0.08 |
| Large standard | 1–2 lb / 2–3 lb / 3–20 lb | $0.11 / $0.14 / $0.23 |
| Small and large bulky | 0–50 lb | $1.32 |

*Source: Buy with Prime by Amazon, "Ships in Product Packaging," checked Nov 8, 2026. Shipping weight is the greater of unit weight or dimensional weight.* <C id="sipp-packaging-program-28"/> <C id="sipp-packaging-program-29"/> <C id="sipp-packaging-program-30"/> <C id="sipp-packaging-program-31"/>

All SIPP-certified FBA products are enrolled automatically for MCF and Buy with Prime, <C id="sipp-packaging-program-35"/> but shipments of items that require an overbox do not receive the discounts. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-34"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): is a $600 lab test worth it?">
Inputs: a fragile 2.5 lb large-standard product that must go to an ISTA-6 lab; an assumed lab quote of $600; an assumed 3,000 MCF units a year; the published $0.14 MCF discount for the 2–3 lb tier. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-08"/> <C id="sipp-packaging-program-29"/>

- Yearly MCF discount: 3,000 × $0.14 = $420.
- Months to recover the lab cost: $600 ÷ ($420 ÷ 12) ≈ 17 months.

Now the same seller certifies a 30 lb small-bulky item, also assumed at 3,000 MCF units: 3,000 × $1.32 = $3,960 a year. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-30"/> The fixed testing cost is the same, so bulky items recover it far faster. Add your FBA discount from Amazon's chart before deciding.
</Callout>

## The 2026 bulky packaging fee

New packaging fees took effect for small bulky and large bulky products in the US starting January 15, 2026. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-16"/> Products already enrolled in SIPP remain unaffected. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-17"/> For bulky items, then, certification is no longer just a discount; it is how you stay outside a fee.

## What changes for customers and inbound

Customers see a checkout message that the item can ship in its own packaging, <C id="sipp-packaging-program-43"/> and they can choose Amazon packaging at no additional cost. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-10"/> Sellers still receive incentives for ASINs they have enrolled and certified. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-11"/>

Every unit of a SIPP-certified product you send in needs an exterior scannable label. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-14"/> SIPP is not part of the Climate Pledge Friendly program. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-15"/>

## If you are a vendor rather than a seller

For vendors, products with packaging larger than 18 × 14 × 8 inches or heavier than 20 lb must be certified unless they belong to an excluded category, <C id="sipp-packaging-program-39"/> and eligible products that are not certified incur a chargeback of $1.80 to $4.40 per unit. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-40"/>

Read the FAQ and "FBA Sellers" pages on amazon-packaging.com for the certification rules, and the "Ships in Product Packaging" page in the Buy with Prime knowledge center for the MCF discount table. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-01"/> <C id="sipp-packaging-program-28"/> Then open the Eligible Products tab of the SIPP portal in Seller Central to see which of your ASINs qualify. <C id="sipp-packaging-program-27"/>
