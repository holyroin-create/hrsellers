Amazon's holiday peak fulfillment fee is a higher per-unit fulfillment rate that applies for one season each year. On Amazon's own published terms, "from October 15 to January 14: a per unit holiday peak fulfillment fee will be applied to all" US Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) orders. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-01"/> For 2026–27 the card's peak tables are effective October 15, 2026 to January 14, 2027. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-03"/> The FBA peak fee for orders placed on Amazon is announced inside Seller Central, which is not publicly readable, so check the amounts there. This page covers the published MCF rates and how they behave.

## How the peak fee works

Three rules from Amazon's MCF fee policy and pricing page frame the fee:

- **It is per unit.** The fee is charged per unit, on all US MCF orders during the window. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-01"/>
- **It changes every year.** Amazon says peak fees "vary from year to year and are published annually." <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-02"/>
- **A 3.5% surcharge sits alongside it.** The 2026 card says "a 3.5% fuel and logistics-related surcharge will be applied to MCF fulfillment fees in the US," but does not say whether the listed peak rates already include it. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-04"/>

The 2026 card publishes its peak fee tables for the October 15, 2026 to January 14, 2027 period by size tier, weight and order size. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-03"/> <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-07"/> The dated sequence of every seasonal change — peak storage months, waivers and end dates — is on our 2026 peak surcharge schedule.

## 2026–27 peak rates at standard speed

Standard speed is defined as "3 business days, click-to-delivery." <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-05"/> The card heads its four price columns "1," "2," "3" and "4+" without a label; Amazon's pricing page says MCF fees are based on units per order, among other factors, which is how they are read here. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-16"/>

| Size tier and weight | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small standard, up to 4 oz | $7.53 | $5.17 | $4.29 | $3.83 |
| Small standard, over 12 oz to 16 oz | $8.88 | $6.12 | $5.10 | $4.60 |
| Large standard, up to 4 oz | $7.80 | $5.36 | $4.64 | $4.24 |
| Large standard, over 1 lb to 2 lb | $10.97 | $7.52 | $6.23 | $5.58 |
| Large standard, over 2 lb to 3 lb | $12.16 | $8.54 | $7.20 | $6.36 |

*Source: Amazon Supply Chain, 2026 MCF rate card, "Peak fulfillment fees - Standard" (effective October 15, 2026–January 14, 2027), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-07"/> <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-08"/> <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-09"/> <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-10"/> <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-11"/>

Heavier items switch to a base-plus-weight formula. A one-unit large standard item over 3 lb to 20 lb costs $12.29 plus $0.70 per lb above 3 lb. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-12"/> A one-unit small bulky item up to 30 lb costs $19.12 plus $0.75 per lb above 2 lb. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-13"/>

Expedited means "2 business days, click-to-delivery." <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-06"/> The premium over standard is largest on single-unit orders.

For a single small item the expedited premium is $3.36 per unit; in an order of four or more it shrinks to $0.32. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-14"/> If you offer expedited shipping on another channel, price that option for the peak window separately.

Amazon says sellers can "save up to 50% on per-unit fulfillment fees when you ship multi-unit orders." <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-16"/> The peak card shows how that works.

Bundles and "buy more" promotions that raise units per order lower your peak fulfillment cost per unit, as long as the units ship together in one MCF order. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-10"/> <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-16"/>

## Fees that sit beside the peak rate

Two other charges appear on the same card:

- **The 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge** on MCF fulfillment fees in the US; the card does not say whether listed peak rates include it. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-04"/> More in our [fuel and logistics surcharge guide](/guides/fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026/).
- **The lithium battery charge**: items with lithium batteries add $0.11 per unit to the base fulfillment fee. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-21"/>

Storage is separate and has its own peak months. To see how fulfillment, storage, surcharges and returns add up on a single unit, use our peak season cost stack. One option avoids seasonal pricing upstream: Amazon describes AWD as having "no additional costs for the holiday season." <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-22"/>

## FBA orders on Amazon

For orders placed on Amazon, FBA fulfillment costs are based on the product's weights and dimensions. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-17"/> Amazon's public 2026 fee notice covered the January change — an average increase of $0.08 per unit sold — and promised at least 90 days before any fee increase takes effect. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-18"/> <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-19"/> The FBA peak amounts themselves are in Seller Central.

## How to confirm the peak fee for your products

For MCF, open the rate card at supplychain.amazon.com/docs/rate-card (linked from the MCF pricing page) or the 2026 card at supplychain.amazon.com/docs/2026-peak-card, and find your size tier, weight band, order size and speed in the peak tables. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-03"/> For FBA, open Seller Central and go to Reports, then Fulfillment, then Fee Preview. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-20"/> Through the Selling Partner API, the US Fee Preview Report includes an expected-future-fulfillment-fee-per-unit column. <C id="holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee-23"/> This page reflects Amazon's published rate cards. A disputed peak charge on your account is settled through a Seller Central support case, not by these tables.
