For most US categories, TikTok Shop charges a **6% referral fee per order** <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-01"/>, and TikTok's referral fee article gives the rate as 5% to 6% depending on the category <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-02"/>. What decides your actual bill is the base the percentage is applied to — customer payment plus any platform discount, minus tax <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-03"/> — and what happens to the fee when an order is refunded. This page works through both from TikTok's own policy pages. For how to open a shop, payouts and reserves, see the [TikTok Shop marketplace overview](/marketplaces/tiktok-shop/); for what changed in 2026, see TikTok Shop fee changes in 2026.

## The rate: 6% for most categories, 5% for a few

TikTok raised the referral fee to 6% per order on all qualified transactions starting April 1, 2024 <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-01"/>. Its seller portal still describes the cost of selling in one line: no setup fee, and a 6% commission on each sale <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-18"/>.

The category table is where the exceptions live. Mainstream categories — beauty, womenswear, phones and electronics, food and beverages and the rest — are listed at 6% <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-11"/>. The lower rates are narrow:

| Category group | Rate | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Most categories (e.g. Beauty & Personal Care, Womenswear, Phones & Electronics, Food & Beverages) | 6% | No note <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-11"/> |
| Jewelry: Diamond, Gold, Jade, Platinum/Carat Gold, Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald | 5% | Lowered from 6% effective October 31, 2024 <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-07"/> <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-08"/> |
| Collectibles (e.g. Trading Cards & Accessories, Sports Collectibles) | 6% | Any portion of the sale over $10K: 3% <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-09"/> |
| Pre-Owned (e.g. Watches, Bags, Refurbished Phones & Electronics) | 5% | Any portion of the sale over $10K: 3% <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-10"/> |

*Source: TikTok Shop Academy, "TikTok Shop Referral Fees in 2024 by Category" (page dated 05/14/2026, applies to the United States), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-09"/>

Other jewelry sub-categories, such as silver or pearl, are not on the 5% list, so check the exact Level 2 category your product is filed under before you assume the lower rate <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-08"/>.

If you joined on a new-seller promotional fee plan, TikTok's referral fee article says sellers enrolled in promotional plans continue on them until the promotional period ends <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-19"/>.

## The base: what the percentage is charged on

TikTok gives the formula directly: referral fee rate × (customer payment + platform discount − tax) <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-03"/>. It calls this base "Buyer Paid + Platform Discount − Tax" <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-04"/>. The Seller Terms of Service describe the same thing in contract language: the fee is calculated on the total amount a buyer pays for each purchase from you <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-17"/>.

Three consequences follow from that formula:

1. **Tax is removed.** Sales tax the buyer pays is not part of the base <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-03"/>.
2. **Platform discounts are added back.** If TikTok funds a discount, the fee is charged as though the buyer had paid that amount. TikTok's own example: a $102 customer payment, a $5 platform discount and $2 of tax give 6% × $105 = $6.30 <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-05"/>.
3. **Only the platform discount is added back.** The formula has no term for a discount you fund yourself <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-03"/>; if such a discount lowers customer payment, the base is lower too. Confirm this against your own settlement detail.

TikTok's article also lists shipping and tax fees as the exceptions to the charges the referral fee covers <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-06"/>. Read that alongside the formula above rather than in place of it, and check your order-level settlement detail to see how shipping on your own orders was treated.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): who funds the coupon matters">
Inputs: a $50 item, free shipping, $3.60 sales tax, 6% rate, a $5 coupon; assumption: a seller-funded coupon reduces customer payment and is not recorded as a platform discount.

**Seller-funded coupon.** Customer payment $48.60 ($45 + $3.60 tax), platform discount $0. Base: $48.60 + $0 − $3.60 = $45.00. Fee: **$2.70**.

**TikTok-funded coupon.** Customer payment $48.60, platform discount $5. Base: $48.60 + $5 − $3.60 = $50.00. Fee: **$3.00**.

The buyer pays the same in both cases; only the funding source changes the fee. <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-03"/> <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-05"/>
</Callout>

## Refunds: the Refund Administration Fee

When an order is refunded, TikTok refunds the referral fee to you minus a **Refund Administration Fee of 20% of the referral fee** <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-12"/>. Since rules updated on May 15, 2025 <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-14"/>, that fee is capped at **$5 per SKU type for each refund** <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-13"/>.

Two situations carry no Refund Administration Fee:

- the buyer asks for a refund before the order ships and the order meets TikTok's auto-canceling criteria <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-16"/>;
- the order is a "Buy now refund later" creator sample <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-15"/>.

## Worked example (hypothetical): four orders

Inputs: no platform discount, tax excluded from the figures below, full refund where stated, one SKU type per order, Refund Administration Fee taken as 20% of the full referral fee.

| Order | Referral fee | If fully refunded: fee kept by TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| $30 skincare item at 6% | $1.80 | 20% × $1.80 = $0.36 |
| $400 gold ring at 5% | $20.00 | 20% × $20 = $4.00 |
| $600 phone accessory bundle at 6% | $36.00 | 20% × $36 = $7.20, capped at $5.00 |
| $15,000 trading card (Collectibles, 6% / 3% over $10K) | $600 + $150 = $750.00 | Capped at $5.00 |

*Rates from TikTok Shop Academy referral fee pages, checked Nov 8, 2026; arithmetic is ours.* <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-07"/> <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-09"/> <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-12"/> <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-13"/>

Two patterns stand out. First, the $5 cap means the refund cost is proportional only on cheaper items: above a $25 referral fee (a roughly $417 order at 6%), the amount kept per refunded SKU type stops growing. Second, on high-value collectibles the 3% tier roughly halves the marginal rate, so the effective rate on the $15,000 card is 5%, not 6%.

## Common mistakes when estimating TikTok Shop fees

| Mistake | What TikTok's pages say |
|---|---|
| Calculating 6% on the price after a TikTok-funded coupon | Platform discounts are added back to the base <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-03"/> |
| Including sales tax in the base | Tax is subtracted <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-04"/> |
| Assuming all jewelry pays 5% | Only five listed sub-categories do <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-08"/> |
| Assuming a refund returns the whole fee | 20% of the fee is kept, capped at $5 per SKU type <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-12"/> <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-13"/> |
| Charging the refund fee on every cancellation | Qualifying pre-shipment buyer cancellations are exempt <C id="tiktok-shop-fees-16"/> |
