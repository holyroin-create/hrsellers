On a US import entry, you pay four things on top of each other: the **general duty rate** from the tariff schedule, any **additional duties in force** for your product and origin, the **merchandise processing fee (MPF)**, and, for ocean cargo, the **harbor maintenance fee (HMF)**. For entries on or after October 1, 2026, the MPF is 0.3464% of value, with a floor of $34.58 and a cap of $670.86. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-01"/> <C id="tariff-stack-2026-02"/> <C id="tariff-stack-2026-03"/> <C id="tariff-stack-2026-04"/> The HMF is 0.125% of value for cargo unloaded from a commercial vessel at a covered port. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-06"/> The hard part is the middle layer, because the set of additional duties changed several times in 2026.

## The four layers on one entry

| Layer | What sets it | How it is charged |
|---|---|---|
| General (MFN) duty | The HTSUS line for your product | Usually a percentage of entered value |
| Additional duties | Presidential proclamations, executive orders, USTR notices | Added on top unless the legal text caps or excludes it |
| Merchandise processing fee | 19 CFR 24.23; limits adjusted yearly | 0.3464% with a $34.58 minimum and $670.86 maximum (formal entry) |
| Harbor maintenance fee | 19 CFR 24.24 | 0.125% of value, vessel cargo at covered ports |

*Source: Federal Register (CBP Dec. 26-14) and eCFR Title 19 Part 24, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="tariff-stack-2026-01"/> <C id="tariff-stack-2026-03"/> <C id="tariff-stack-2026-06"/>

The importer pays the HMF by adding it to any normal duty, tax or fee payable at the time of formal entry processing. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-07"/>

## What changed in the additional-duty layer in 2026

Four moves between February and July reshaped the stack. Read them in order, because each one depends on the one before.

**IEEPA duties are gone, but other duties stayed.** Executive Order 14389, signed February 20, 2026, ended the IEEPA ad valorem duties. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-08"/> The same order says it does not affect other duties, including Section 232 and Section 301 duties. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-09"/>

**The Section 122 surcharge had a built-in end date.** Proclamation 11012 imposed a 10% surcharge under section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-10"/> It applied from 12:01 a.m. EST on February 24, 2026, for 150 days, through 12:01 a.m. EDT on July 24, 2026, unless it was expressly suspended, modified or terminated earlier, or extended by an Act of Congress. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-11"/> If you have entries from that window, the surcharge belongs in their stack.

**Forced-labor Section 301 tariffs took over on July 24.** USTR's notice applies to goods entered for consumption on or after 12:01 a.m. eastern time on July 24, 2026. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-13"/> For investigated economies that are not otherwise listed, the rate is 12.5%. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-14"/> Products of China get the 12.5% rate too, except as the notice's annexes provide. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-16"/>

**China's 2018 Section 301 tariffs continue.** USTR decided that both 2018 China actions did not end at their four-year anniversaries in 2026 and remain in effect. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-17"/> For Chinese-origin goods, check both the 2018 lists and the 2026 forced-labor action.

## When a duty does not simply add on top

"Stacking" is the default, but some legal texts limit it. Check for these three patterns before you add anything up.

The EU and Taiwan pattern changes the arithmetic. Worked example (hypothetical): if an EU-origin item has a 4% MFN rate, the MFN rate is below 10%, so the combined total is 10% and the forced-labor Section 301 tariff adds 6 percentage points, not 10. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-15"/> Also note that since April 6, 2026, Section 232 metals duties apply to the full customs value of the product, not just the value of the metal in it. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-18"/>

## Worked example (hypothetical): one ocean entry

The inputs below are made up to show the math. Swap in your own HTSUS rate and the additional duties that actually apply to your product and origin.

- Entered value: $20,000, arriving by ocean at a covered port, filed as a formal entry
- General (MFN) rate: 5% (hypothetical)
- Additional duty: 12.5% (hypothetical, as if the goods fall under the forced-labor Section 301 rate with no exemption) <C id="tariff-stack-2026-14"/>
- MPF: 0.3464% with a $34.58 minimum and $670.86 maximum <C id="tariff-stack-2026-01"/> <C id="tariff-stack-2026-02"/> <C id="tariff-stack-2026-03"/>
- HMF: 0.125% <C id="tariff-stack-2026-06"/>

| Line | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| General duty | $20,000 × 5% | $1,000.00 |
| Additional duty | $20,000 × 12.5% | $2,500.00 |
| MPF | $20,000 × 0.3464% = $69.28 (between $34.58 and $670.86) | $69.28 |
| HMF | $20,000 × 0.125% | $25.00 |
| **Total** | | **$3,594.28** |

*Source: rates from Federal Register (CBP Dec. 26-14; USTR notice 2026-15181) and eCFR 19 CFR 24.24, checked Nov 8, 2026. Arithmetic is ours.* <C id="tariff-stack-2026-14"/>

That total comes to about 17.97% of entered value. The two fees are small next to the duties. The additional duty is the line that swings the result.

**Same goods, smaller shipment.** Worked example (hypothetical): a $1,500 air shipment filed as a formal entry. The percentage MPF would be $5.20, so the $34.58 minimum applies instead. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-02"/> The HMF rule in 19 CFR 24.24 covers cargo loaded on or unloaded from a commercial vessel, and this air shipment involves no vessel. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-06"/> Informal entry procedures are allowed for shipments valued at $2,500 or less. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-20"/> If the shipment qualifies and is filed as an automated informal entry, the fee is a flat $2.77. <C id="tariff-stack-2026-05"/>

## Common stacking mistakes

| Mistake | Why it is wrong |
|---|---|
| Still adding IEEPA duties to new entries | Executive Order 14389 says they are no longer to be collected <C id="tariff-stack-2026-08"/> |
| Adding the 10% Section 122 surcharge to entries after July 24, 2026 | The proclamation set it to run through 12:01 a.m. EDT on July 24, 2026 unless ended earlier or extended by Congress <C id="tariff-stack-2026-11"/> |
| Adding the surcharge on top of Section 232 goods | The proclamation excluded Section 232 tariffs from the add-on <C id="tariff-stack-2026-12"/> |
| Adding a flat 10% Section 301 duty to EU goods | Below a 10% MFN rate, MFN plus Section 301 totals 10%; at 10% or more, the Section 301 tariff is zero <C id="tariff-stack-2026-15"/> |
| Calculating metals duty on metal content only | Since April 6, 2026, it applies to full customs value <C id="tariff-stack-2026-18"/> |
| Charging HMF on air or truck freight | The fee covers cargo loaded on or unloaded from a commercial vessel <C id="tariff-stack-2026-06"/> |
