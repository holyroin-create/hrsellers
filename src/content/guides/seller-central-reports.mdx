Every guide on this site carries a version of the same instruction: the published figure is somebody else's account, so get yours. This page collects where.

## The nine

### 1. Selling Economics / Fee Preview

**Settles:** what Amazon actually charges you per unit, at SKU level, on current rates.

This is the single most important report for anyone modelling margin, because 2026 made the published averages nearly useless. The [$0.08 average](/guides/amazon-fba-fee-changes-2026/) hides a spread of $0.05 to $0.51 by band, and [price is now an input to the fee](/guides/price-tier-repricing-trap/).

**Use it for:** break-even ACOS, repricing floors, product research, any deal submission.

### 2. Monthly Storage Fee and Inventory Age reports

**Settles:** what you actually paid to store things, and how old everything is.

Storage is [four separate charges](/guides/fba-storage-four-layers/) on different bases, and the published rates for all four disagree. This report is what you were charged.

**The inventory age report is where the [150-day review](/guides/removal-vs-hold-decision/) starts.** Reported aged-inventory triggers at 180 days mean a monthly habit, not a project.

### 3. SKU Economics Report

**Settles:** the returns processing fee, which is deducted between the 7th and 15th of the **third month following shipment**.

That delay is why sellers do not connect the charge to the product that caused it. See the [returns guide](/guides/returns-cost-and-badge/).

### 4. Returns and Recovery Dashboard

**Settles:** return rate per ASIN, and the reason codes behind it.

Reason codes separate the two different problems — an expectation gap needs listing work, a defect needs supplier work. **Aggregate return rate tells you nothing**; a 28% catalogue average can contain a hero SKU at 15% and a problem SKU at 55%.

### 5. Account Health dashboard

**Settles:** ODR, late shipment rate, cancellation rate, valid tracking rate and your Account Health Rating.

**Check daily.** Reporting describes notifications as arriving *after* you cross the 1% ODR threshold rather than while trending toward it — see the [ODR guide](/guides/order-defect-rate/). It is the only early warning that exists, and it takes two minutes.

### 6. Settlement reports

**Settles:** the money. Actual proceeds, actual fees, actual timing.

Three uses across this site: measuring your [real order-to-cash gap](/guides/dd7-payout-reserve/) under DD+7, measuring your [effective currency conversion rate](/guides/currency-conversion-payouts/) against mid-market, and building [accrual accounts](/guides/accrual-accounting-cogs/).

### 7. Inventory location report

**Settles:** which states your stock has been stored in.

Almost nobody opens this, and it is your [nexus footprint](/guides/sales-tax-nexus/). FBA inventory is reported to create physical nexus regardless of revenue, and you did not choose the locations.

### 8. Capacity and IPI dashboard

**Settles:** how much you are allowed to store, and whether individual ASINs are throttled.

Two constraints, not one — account capacity in cubic feet and [ASIN-level restock limits](/guides/ipi-restock-limits/). Check before planning Q4, not during it.

### 9. Advertising search term reports

**Settles:** which keywords convert, at what cost per acquisition.

The useful direction is [paid discovers, organic keeps](/guides/listing-optimisation-a10/): mine the report for converting terms and build them into the title and backend field. And calculate cost per acquisition rather than CPC — the cheaper click is frequently the more expensive sale.

## The two you should also have

**Brand Analytics**, if you are in [Brand Registry](/guides/aplus-content-brand-tools/). High-volume terms with low click share are keyword problems; high click share with low conversion share are listing problems. Free, and rarely opened.

**Business Reports**, for Featured Offer percentage — your [Buy Box share](/guides/buy-box-featured-offer/), which is a percentage rather than a binary win.

## The routine

**Daily, two minutes:** Account Health.

**Weekly, fifteen minutes:** Feedback Manager for new negatives, capacity and IPI, advertising search terms, and any A-to-z claims — reporting describes not letting them auto-grant.

**Monthly, an hour:** inventory age (the 150-day review), storage fee report, SKU Economics for the returns fee landing from three months ago, and returns by ASIN.

**Quarterly:** settlement reports for the cash-timing and currency checks, and a fee preview pass on your top twenty SKUs.

**Situational:** inventory location report before any tax conversation; CBP Form 7501 from your forwarder before any [landed cost](/guides/landed-cost-fee-stack/) work.

## Why this page exists

Across roughly fifty guides on this site, a pattern held: **published sources disagreed on almost every figure that mattered.** Storage rates, ODR thresholds, IPI thresholds, currency conversion costs, reimbursement impact, deal fees, AWD rates, title character limits.

In most cases the disagreement resolved into something mundane — a dated change, a regional variation, a publisher with something to sell. But resolving it took work, and the resolution was always the same: **the primary source, or your own account.**

These nine reports are your own account. They are not comprehensive and they are not interesting. They are the only figures in this subject that describe your business rather than an average of somebody else's.
