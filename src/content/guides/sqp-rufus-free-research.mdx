Our [Rufus guide](/guides/rufus-listing-optimization/) made the argument that the cheapest AI-era wins are structural. This is the companion piece: where the raw material for those edits already sits, free, inside accounts most sellers already have.

## Source one: the questions Rufus asks about you

Open your own listing as a shopper and read the question prompts Rufus surfaces beneath it. Reported guidance treats that list as direct research: those are the questions the assistant considers unresolved and answerable about your product. The work: for each prompt, check whether your bullets, A+ content or Q&A answer it **explicitly** — not implied, stated. Every unanswered prompt is a hole the AI fills from a competitor.

## Source two: Search Query Performance

Inside Brand Analytics, the Search Query Performance dashboard shows the actual query phrasings behind your impressions and purchases — including the long, conditional, problem-shaped phrasings that intent-based matching is described as rewarding. Read it for **nouns and constraints** — "for small apartments", "with dogs", "under-cabinet" — and carry those exact phrases into backend attribute fields and A+ copy, per the structural playbook in the Rufus guide. Brand-registered sellers are also reported to find partial Rufus attribution data in Brand Analytics: incomplete, but a free directional read on whether the AI layer is sending you anyone.

## The loop, monthly

Fifteen minutes: read the prompts, scan SQP for new phrasings, ship the edit list, note the partial attribution trend. Only when that loop stops producing edits does paid tooling earn a conversation — the same order-of-spend discipline as everywhere else on this site: free and structural first, [conversion fundamentals](/guides/listing-optimisation-a10/) always, subscriptions last.
