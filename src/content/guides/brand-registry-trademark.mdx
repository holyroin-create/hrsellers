You do not need a federal trademark to sell, but you need at least a pending one to join Amazon Brand Registry, and the US filing fee starts at $350 per class. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-32"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-11"/> As of October 1, 2026, the USPTO reported an average of 10.4 months from filing to registration or abandonment. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-29"/> Describing your goods with ID Manual entries avoids the $200-per-class free-form surcharge. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-13"/>

## What a trademark is, and what registration adds

A trademark can be any word, phrase, symbol, design, or combination of these that identifies your goods. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-01"/> You become a trademark owner as soon as you start using it with your products, <C id="brand-registry-trademark-02"/> and the USPTO is explicit that you are not required to register. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-03"/>

Registration is what turns limited, local rights into something a seller can enforce across marketplaces. It creates nationwide rights <C id="brand-registry-trademark-04"/> and adds these benefits:

| Benefit of federal registration | Why a seller cares |
|---|---|
| Legal presumption of ownership and right to use | Stronger footing against copycat listings |
| Stopping imports of goods with an infringing mark | Once recorded with CBP, it can stop those imports |
| Basis for filing in foreign countries | Starting point for expanding to other marketplaces abroad |
| Right to sue in federal court | Formal enforcement route |

*Source: USPTO, "Why register your trademark," checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="brand-registry-trademark-07"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-08"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-09"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-10"/>

You can put TM on goods before you file anything. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-05"/> The ® symbol is used only once the mark is registered. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-06"/>

## Filing basis: already selling, or about to

Most sellers choose between filing on use in commerce (you already sell under the mark) and intent to use (you plan to). The intent-to-use route adds steps, because the USPTO issues a notice of allowance only for applications filed on that basis, <C id="brand-registry-trademark-20"/> and you must then file a statement of use or an extension request. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-25"/>

## What it costs

Fees below come from the fiscal year 2025 fee rule, effective January 18, 2025. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-19"/>

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Base application, per class (Section 1 or 44) | $350 |
| Insufficient information surcharge, per class | $100 |
| Free-form text instead of the ID Manual, per class | $200 |
| Each additional 1,000 characters of free-form text, per affected class | $200 |
| Amendment to allege use or statement of use, per class | $150 |
| Extension of time to file statement of use, per class | $125 |
| Five-year Section 8 declaration, per class | $325 |
| 10-year renewal with five-year declaration, per class | $650 |

*Source: USPTO, Trademark fee information, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="brand-registry-trademark-11"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-12"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-13"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-14"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-15"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-16"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-17"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-18"/>

The surcharges are where sellers overpay. Picking your goods descriptions from the USPTO's ID Manual avoids the $200 free-form charge, <C id="brand-registry-trademark-13"/> and a complete application avoids the $100 insufficient-information charge. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-12"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): a two-class intent-to-use filing">
Inputs: a kitchenware seller files one mark in two classes on an intent-to-use basis, using ID Manual descriptions, and needs one extension before launch.

- Filing: 2 × $350 = $700 <C id="brand-registry-trademark-11"/>
- One extension: 2 × $125 = $250 <C id="brand-registry-trademark-16"/>
- Statement of use: 2 × $150 = $300 <C id="brand-registry-trademark-15"/>
- Total USPTO fees: $1,250. Writing the descriptions in free-form text instead would add 2 × $200 = $400. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-13"/>
</Callout>

## Do you need an attorney?

It depends on where you are based. Foreign-domiciled applicants must be represented at the USPTO by an attorney licensed in the United States. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-30"/> Applicants domiciled in the United States or its territories are not required to hire one. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-31"/> For a seller whose company is set up abroad but sells on US marketplaces, domicile is the deciding factor, not where the inventory sits.

## Getting into Amazon Brand Registry

Brand Registry has four practical requirements:

<Checklist title="Brand Registry readiness" items={[
  "A pending or registered trademark for your brand name or logo",
  "Issued by the trademark office of a country with an Amazon store (for the US store, the USPTO qualifies)",
  "A word mark, or a design mark that contains words, letters or numbers",
  "A logo with your brand name permanently affixed to the products or packaging"
]} />

Amazon requires a pending or registered trademark for your brand name or logo, <C id="brand-registry-trademark-32"/> issued by the designated trademark office of a country with a corresponding Amazon store. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-33"/> The mark must be a text-based word mark or an image-based design mark with words, letters, or numbers, <C id="brand-registry-trademark-34"/> and your brand name must be permanently affixed to the products or packaging. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-35"/> Sellers without a trademark can use Amazon's IP Accelerator, which connects them with Amazon-vetted legal service providers. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-36"/>

A pure logo with no letters will not meet the mark-type requirement, so if your logo is a symbol alone, file a word mark for the brand name as well. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-34"/>

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What it costs or risks |
|---|---|
| Writing goods descriptions from scratch | $200 per class free-form surcharge, plus $200 per extra 1,000 characters |
| Missing an office action deadline | The response is due within three months of the issue date |
| Using ® while the application is pending | ® is for registered marks; use TM until then |
| Forgetting the statement of use | Due within six months of the notice of allowance, or an extension request |
| Letting the registration lapse | Maintenance filings are due between years five and six, and nine and ten |

*Source: USPTO trademark basics and fee pages, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="brand-registry-trademark-13"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-14"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-22"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-06"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-25"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-26"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-27"/>

Budget for the long run, too. Keeping a two-class registration alive costs 2 × $325 = $650 for the five-year declaration, then 2 × $650 = $1,300 for the combined renewal and declaration — a hypothetical calculation from the published per-class fees. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-17"/> <C id="brand-registry-trademark-18"/> Put both windows in your calendar on the day the registration issues; the dates run from the registration date, not the filing date. <C id="brand-registry-trademark-26"/>
