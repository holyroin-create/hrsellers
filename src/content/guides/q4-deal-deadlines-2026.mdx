The fixed dates for the rest of Q4 2026 are Black Friday on November 27 and Cyber Monday on November 30, as published by Amazon. <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-04"/> <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-05"/> The submission cut-offs for Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals are published inside Seller Central and should be checked there. What you can schedule from public sources are the lead times attached to each tool — deals, coupons, price discounts, Brand Store event pages and ad budgets — counted back from those two dates.

## Lead times by tool

Each discount tool has a published duration or eligibility rule that fixes how early you must act.

| Tool | Published rule | What it means for Nov 27–30 |
|---|---|---|
| Lightning Deal | Runs 4 to 12 hours, as Amazon determines | Covers part of one day; the time slot is Amazon's choice |
| Best Deal | Runs a select number of days | Can span the Black Friday–Cyber Monday stretch if scheduled for it |
| Coupon | Expires after 90 days; Amazon suggests 2–3 months | Start date sets the expiry; one started now runs into February |
| Price discount | Up to 30 days, limited units, submitted for validation | A run of up to 30 days has to start in the last days of October or later to reach Nov 30; check the end date when you create it |

*Source: Amazon, Sell on Amazon blog, seller promotions guide, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-14"/> <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-17"/> <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-18"/> <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-16"/>

Eligibility is the other gate, and it cannot be fixed at short notice: deals need a Professional plan and an overall rating of at least four stars, and coupons need at least 3.5 stars. <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-15"/> <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-21"/> If your rating is below 3.5 stars, neither deals nor coupons are open to you; the guide states no rating threshold for price discounts. Costs for each tool are covered in [Amazon deal and coupon fees](/guides/deal-and-coupon-fees/).

## Brand Store and ad messaging rules

Amazon Ads publishes timing rules for event messaging that apply to Brand Stores:

- Sales events such as Black Friday or Cyber Monday "should start no more than seven days prior to the event," unless the event is considered to last longer, such as Cyber Week. <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-07"/>
- Event messaging should be removed or updated within 24 hours of the event date. <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-08"/>
- Submit the Brand Store at least 48 hours ahead of its scheduled start date. <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-09"/>

It also sets accuracy rules: references to Black Friday or Cyber Monday should not mislead customers about how long the deal lasts, and all deals must be live and accurate on the dates they run. <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-11"/> <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-12"/>

## Stock and ad budgets on the peak days

Amazon Ads tells advertisers to make sure products with Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals "are in stock leading up to and during the event dates," and notes that the lead-up "is just as important as the event itself." <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-10"/> <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-13"/> FBA inbound cut-offs for the holiday are set in Seller Central; build your shipping plan from those, not from the event dates alone.

For advertising, the key mechanical point is that Sponsored Products daily budgets "are not paced throughout the day," so a small budget can be spent within minutes when traffic is high. <C id="q4-deal-deadlines-2026-20"/> Review budgets for November 27 and 30 in advance rather than after campaigns stop serving. Campaign set-up is covered in [Sponsored Products campaign structure](/guides/ppc-campaign-structure-launch/).
