If you finance inventory against your Amazon sales — with a lender that takes a claim on your Amazon payouts, or a revenue-based funder that is repaid from them — the current version of Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement (BSA) is the document to read before you sign or renew. The BSA and any change notices are read inside Seller Central, so this page cannot quote them and does not describe or confirm any 2026 change. Read the current text and any change notice there, then use the publicly documented facts below to compare the financing routes open to you.

## What is publicly documented about financing against Amazon sales

Amazon has its own financing program. Amazon Lending, "through its third-party financing providers, offers business financing," and the first step is to "check for financing invitations" in Seller Central. <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-01"/> <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-07"/> Amazon says "providers take multiple factors into consideration when determining financing eligibility" and that applying "will not impact your personal credit." <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-18"/> <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-06"/>

The line of credit, in Amazon's words, is "a revolving credit line," and "interest accrues at a fixed rate on the drawn balance." <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-04"/> <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-05"/>

Of these, the merchant cash advance is the one Amazon ties to future sales, and Amazon's description is specific: it requires "no personal collateral." <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-03"/> When Amazon launched the Parafin-provided advance on November 1, 2022, it said there was "no collateral required—unlike a traditional loan," and that "payments are only required during periods that a seller has made sales." <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-11"/> <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-12"/> In Amazon's October 31, 2025 financing guide, the mechanism is that "Parafin debits a set percentage of gross sales from your linked bank account." <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-09"/> Repayment, in that description, is collected from your linked bank account. Amazon also says it gets "explicit consent before sharing any data" with providers. <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-10"/>

## How your Amazon money reaches you

Any financing secured on Amazon sales depends on how Amazon pays out. Amazon's seller FAQ says "you must provide a valid bank account as the deposit method for your selling account," that "it can take up to five business days for the money to appear in your bank account after Amazon initiates a payment," and that Amazon "cannot make payments to a credit card or online payment system." <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-13"/> <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-14"/> <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-15"/>

Not all sales are paid out at once. The Deferred Transaction Report, a "point-in-time report that contains a list of deferred transactions," lists deferred transactions and can cover up to 10 years of data. <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-16"/> A lender sizing a facility on your Amazon revenue may ask to see both settlements and deferred amounts; the [Seller Central reports guide](/guides/seller-central-reports/) explains where each sits.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): what a sales-linked repayment does to cash">
Inputs: a seller has $40,000 of monthly gross sales and takes a cash advance repaid at 10% of gross sales. Each month 0.10 × $40,000 = $4,000 is debited from the linked bank account. If sales fall to $25,000, the debit falls to $2,500; in a month with no sales, Amazon's description of the Parafin advance says no payment is required. <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-12"/> A fixed-payment loan of $4,000 a month would not adjust. The rate and fee here are invented; real offers set their own.
</Callout>

## How to confirm the agreement wording

Open the Business Solutions Agreement and any change notice in Seller Central and save a dated copy of the version in force when you sign financing. For Amazon's own financing terms, check the Amazon Lending page at sell.amazon.com/programs/amazon-lending, and for payout timing, the Selling with Amazon FAQ at sell.amazon.com/learn/faq. <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-01"/> <C id="bsa-collateral-change-2026-14"/>
