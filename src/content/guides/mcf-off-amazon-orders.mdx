If you sell anywhere besides Amazon and your stock already lives in FBA, MCF is the path of least resistance: one inventory pool, no second warehouse relationship, and reported on-time delivery of about 97% on two speed tiers — standard at 3 business days, expedited at 2. This guide is the fee side of the [second-channel decision](/guides/off-amazon-diversification/); whether to have a second channel at all is that guide's question.

## How the pricing works

MCF pricing is reported as per-unit and all-inclusive of pick, pack and ship, with four variables: **size tier, shipping weight, units per order, and delivery speed.** No monthly subscription, no setup fee — you pay per unit fulfilled.

Two structural discounts are reported:

| Programme | Reported terms |
| --- | --- |
| Multi-unit orders | Up to 50% off per-unit fees on multi-unit orders |
| MCF Preferred Pricing (2026) | Up to 15% off for eligible sellers, capped at 100,000 MCF units or 12 months from enrolment, whichever comes first |

The multi-unit discount is not a footnote. It means MCF's economics look very different for a store whose average order contains three units than for one shipping singles — and as the next section shows, the January increase drew exactly that line.

## What changed in January, and again in May

Amazon raised MCF rates on **15 January 2026**. The reported shape of the increase:

- Most **single-unit** orders up **$0.35–$0.41 per unit** — characterised in reporting as four to five times the $0.08 average base increase FBA itself took, with the overall MCF average put around $0.30 per unit.
- **No increase** for orders of 3 or more units in the small and large standard-size tiers.

Then from **2 May 2026**, Amazon applied a **3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge** to US MCF fulfilment fees — its stated framing being that elevated costs had been absorbed until then, and that like other carriers it recovers a portion through temporary surcharges. This is the same mechanism, at the same rate, as the FBA-side surcharge our [fuel and logistics guide](/guides/fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026/) covers.

And for Q4: **holiday peak fulfilment fees run 15 October 2026 to 14 January 2027**, per Amazon, with the same per-unit increases as the prior year — reported at **$0.35 per unit for small standard, scaling to $1.00 for extra-large** — and the 3.5% surcharge applied on top. The FBA side of that story is in our [holiday peak guide](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/); if MCF is part of your Q4 plan, both stacks apply to you at once.

## The referral-fee wrinkle that flatters MCF

One line makes MCF look better than a raw rate comparison suggests: **MCF orders carry no referral fee.** The sale happened on your Shopify store; Amazon only fulfilled it. Reporting frames this as saving the 8–15% an equivalent marketplace sale would surrender — which partially offsets the same reporting's finding that comparable MCF fulfilment runs **30–50% above FBA rates**, and often above dedicated 3PL rates, particularly past 3 lb.

A published head-to-head makes the ambiguity concrete: one 2026 comparison puts a sample item at **$5.70 MCF fulfilment + 3.5% surcharge ≈ $5.90**, against **$7.00 at a 3PL** ($2.75 pick/pack + $4.25 shipping). MCF wins that row — and the same source immediately argues the row is not the whole story, which is the next section.

## What MCF will not do

The constraints are where multi-channel brands reportedly hit the wall:

- **Your box is a plain box.** Unbranded packaging is the default at no extra cost — plain materials, no Amazon branding, but no *your* branding either. No custom boxes, tissue, inserts or thank-you cards. For DTC brands whose unboxing drives repeat purchase, reporting calls this a dealbreaker.
- **Unbranded has size limits.** Per Amazon, items over **56 inches on the longest side or 49.9 lb** are not supported by the unbranded option.
- **Returns and customer service stay with you.** MCF fulfils; it does not absorb the aftermath. Your returns economics are still your own — the same arithmetic as our [returns cost guide](/guides/returns-cost-and-badge/).
- **Channel rules apply.** eBay use is reported as workable with generic packaging and a non-Amazon last-mile carrier. **Walmart** has allowed MCF since May 2025 with three reported conditions: unbranded packaging, Amazon Logistics blocked as carrier, and specified shipping windows — with Walmart's 5% surcharge reported waived through 2027.

## What to do

**Price your actual basket, not a sample unit.** The January increase and the multi-unit discount together mean the answer flips with units per order: a singles-heavy store took the full increase; a 3+ unit basket in standard tiers took none and gets up to half off. Run your real order profile through Amazon's MCF calculator — noting Amazon's own caveat that it is a guide only.

**Add the surcharges before comparing to a 3PL.** The honest MCF number is the rate card **plus 3.5%**, and in Q4 plus the peak fee too. Reporting suggests dedicated 3PLs pull ahead for heavy items, specialty SKUs, and volumes past roughly 1,000–2,500 orders a month — and unlock branded packaging besides.

**Decide what the plain box costs you.** For a marketplace-led seller adding a side channel, nothing. For a brand whose Shopify store is the main act, the unboxing moment may be worth more than the fee delta.
