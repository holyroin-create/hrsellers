Multichannel Fulfillment (MCF) lets Amazon ship orders you take on your own website, another marketplace or a social shop, using the stock you already hold in FBA. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-01"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-03"/> The decision is mostly about cost per order and channel rules: MCF is priced per unit by size, weight, units per order and speed, it currently runs on a peak rate card until January 14, 2027, and some marketplaces — Walmart among them — require settings you must turn on. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-14"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-15"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-08"/>

## How MCF works

MCF and FBA "draw from a shared inventory pool," so an FBA seller's stock can serve both Amazon customers and orders from other channels. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-04"/> Inventory has to be in Amazon fulfillment centers before you can place an MCF order. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-05"/> The service is open to businesses whether or not they sell on Amazon, and Amazon says that when you sign up for FBA, you're ready to use MCF. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-02"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-13"/>

Two delivery speeds are published for the US: Standard, delivering in 3 business days, and Expedited, in 2 business days. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-11"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-12"/> Orders ship in unbranded packaging by default at no additional cost, though that feature does not cover items longer than 56 inches on the longest side. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-06"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-07"/>

## What Amazon does not handle

The fulfillment fee buys pick, pack and ship — not the customer relationship. Amazon's MCF FAQ says providing customer service for MCF orders is your responsibility, and so is handling customer refunds. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-31"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-32"/> For returns you can use your own solution or have customers send items back to an Amazon fulfillment center, and Amazon charges return processing fees for items returned through MCF. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-33"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-30"/>

## Channel rules: Walmart and other marketplaces

Marketplaces have their own policies on third-party fulfillment. For Walmart Marketplace, Amazon's FAQ says MCF is compliant if you select unbranded boxes and choose alternate carriers by blocking Amazon Logistics. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-08"/> Blocking Amazon Logistics normally carries a 5% surcharge, but the rate card says that fee is waived for MCF orders, including Walmart, through January 14, 2027. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-10"/> For any other marketplace or social store, Amazon advises you to check the channel's guidelines for 3PL requirements. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-09"/> If you also sell through Walmart's own fulfillment program, compare against [WFS fees](/guides/walmart-wfs/).

## Current fees: the peak rate card

Fulfillment fees are based on product size, shipping weight, units per order and delivery speed. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-14"/> The rate card currently published is effective October 15, 2026 to January 14, 2027. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-15"/> The rate card heads its columns only "1 2 3 4+". Amazon's pricing page lists units per order as a fee factor and describes multi-unit savings as up to 50% off "per-unit fulfillment fees," so the columns are read here as units in the order and each figure as a fee per unit. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-14"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-26"/>

| Size tier, weight | 1 unit | 2 units | 3 units | 4+ units |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small standard, ≤ 4 oz | $7.53 | $5.17 | $4.29 | $3.83 |
| Small standard, > 12 to 16 oz | $8.88 | $6.12 | $5.10 | $4.60 |
| Large standard, > 1 to 2 lb | $10.97 | $7.52 | $6.23 | $5.58 |
| Large standard, > 3 to 20 lb | $12.29 + $0.70/lb above 3 lb | $8.67 + $0.65/lb | $7.33 + $0.63/lb | $6.49 + $0.63/lb |

*Source: Amazon Supply Chain Services, MCF rate card, Standard speed (3 business days), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-16"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-17"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-18"/> <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-19"/>

Expedited costs more at one unit and narrows quickly with more units: a small-standard item of 4 oz or less is $10.89 for one unit and $4.15 at four or more. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-20"/>

The remote area surcharge is calculated after any lithium battery surcharge or peak holiday fees are added. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-23"/> Storage and labeling are not part of the MCF fee: the rate card says all non-fulfillment fees "will continue to be the same as Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA)." <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-29"/>

## Worked example: one order, three destinations

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical)">
Inputs: a 1.5 lb large-standard product with no batteries, Standard speed, ordered on your website during the current peak period. Rates from the table above; the 3.5% surcharge applied last.

- 1 unit to Ohio: $10.97 × 1.035 ≈ $11.35
- 2 units to Ohio: 2 × $7.52 = $15.04; × 1.035 ≈ $15.57 (≈ $7.78 per unit)
- 1 unit to Hawaii: $10.97 + 100% remote surcharge = $21.94 before the 3.5% surcharge (the page does not state the order in which those two are applied)

If your site charges $6.95 for shipping, the single-unit Ohio order costs you about $4.40 more than you collect; a two-unit bundle at the same shipping charge costs about $8.62 more. Run your own prices through the same steps.
</Callout>

The same order on the October 15–January 14 card is a peak price; check the rate card again for rates from January 15, 2027.

## Prime delivery on your own site

Amazon's MCF site also offers a Prime delivery option. Amazon says there are no additional fees to enable it, and fulfillment rates are at parity with MCF Standard at launch; storage fees and surcharges still apply. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-34"/> It is currently available to businesses selling in the US and shipping to US addresses. <C id="mcf-off-amazon-orders-35"/>

## How to confirm MCF fees and rules

Check the current rate card at supplychain.amazon.com/docs/rate-card (it shows the effective dates at the top of each table), the surcharges and discounts at supplychain.amazon.com/mcf/pricing, and channel and returns rules at supplychain.amazon.com/mcf/faqs. Confirm your eligibility for Preferred Pricing and the New Seller Incentive with Amazon before counting on either discount. A marketplace's own policy — not Amazon's FAQ — is the final word on whether you may fulfill its orders through MCF.
