On TikTok's public US pages, the standard referral fee is still **6%**: the seller portal says TikTok Shop charges a 6% commission on each sale <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-07"/>, and the category table dated 05/14/2026 lists mainstream categories at 6% <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-06"/>. The public pages cited here do not announce a 2026 increase to that rate. TikTok announces referral fee amendments by email or through Seller Center <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-02"/>, so if a change applies to your shop, check the notice in your account. This page sets out what the public record does show for 2026 and how to verify your own rate. The fee formula and category exceptions are explained in the [TikTok Shop fees guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-fees/).

Only seller LIVE Auction orders counted; regular LIVE sales and other non-Auction business did not <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-12"/>. Participation was opt-in, with the option to opt out at any time <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-14"/>, and TikTok reserved the right to adjust the rates, dates and eligibility <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-15"/>. TikTok's US seller portal separately advertises referral fee reductions of up to 4% for LIVE auction sellers <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-16"/>.

The end of a reduction matters as much as its start. Under the Seller Terms, a referral fee reduction terminates automatically within the fixed period TikTok states <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-03"/>. When a reduced rate ends, auction orders return to the standard rate <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-13"/> — which looks like an increase in your settlement data even though the standard rate itself has not moved.

## How TikTok changes the referral fee

The Seller Terms of Service, effective October 15, 2026 <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-04"/>, give TikTok the right to amend the referral fee from time to time at its sole discretion <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-01"/>, and say sellers will be told by email or through Seller Center <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-02"/>. TikTok's referral fee article adds that TikTok will use reasonable efforts to notify sellers, such as by email or account notifications, 30 days before changes to the article take effect <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-05"/>.

## Check your own effective rate

You can confirm what you are paying with arithmetic on your own orders: using the fee base explained in the [fees guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-fees/), divide the fee charged on an order by that base to get the rate actually applied.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): reading the rate off one order">
Inputs from a settlement line: customer payment $64.80, platform discount $0, tax $4.80, referral fee charged $3.60.

Base: $64.80 + $0 − $4.80 = $60.00. Rate applied: $3.60 ÷ $60.00 = 6%. If the same calculation on later orders in the same category returns a different figure, find the Seller Center notice that explains it. <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-07"/>
</Callout>

Not every change in a payout total is a fee change. Under TikTok's Dynamic Settlement and Reserve Policy, settlement tiers and reserve levels are re-evaluated on the 1st of every month <C id="tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026-17"/>; the [TikTok Shop marketplace page](/marketplaces/tiktok-shop/) covers that policy.
