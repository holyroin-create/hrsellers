The peak fulfillment schedule for the 2026 holiday window is reported at an **average of $0.32 per unit — the same average as last year.** Sellers will be tempted to file it under "no change." That filing would be wrong, and the reason is one word: stacking.

## Same number, bigger bill

This spring Amazon introduced a **3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge** (dated on our [fee pages](/guides/fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026/)); reporting on the peak schedule confirms the two now apply together. A $12 fulfilment fee that picks up $0.32 of peak also carries roughly $0.42 of fuel line — about $0.74 of 2026-only cost on a unit whose 2025 Q4 math assumed half that. Multiply by size tier and by your catalogue, and "unchanged average" quietly becomes the most expensive unchanged average you have shipped through. The [peak guide](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/) has the per-tier figures; the [margin calculator](/tools/margin-calculator/) turns them into an absorb/raise/exit decision per SKU before mid-October does it for you.
