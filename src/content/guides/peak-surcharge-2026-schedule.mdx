Amazon's published 2026–27 peak charges follow two overlapping schedules. On Amazon's Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) rate card, storage switches to higher rates for the October, November and December billing months. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-04"/> The card's peak fulfillment fee tables are effective October 15, 2026 to January 14, 2027. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-06"/> This page is the calendar; the fee amounts are explained in our guides to the [holiday peak fulfillment fee](/guides/holiday-peak-fulfilment-fee/) and the [peak season cost stack](/guides/peak-season-cost-stack/). Amazon announces FBA-specific peak fulfillment amounts for orders placed on Amazon inside Seller Central, so check those there.

Two notes on the dates. Storage is charged "based on calendar month," so the October rate covers the whole of October, not just the days after October 15. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-05"/> And the peak fulfillment window crosses the new year, so the first two weeks of January still carry peak fulfillment rates even after storage has dropped back. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-06"/>

The 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge is not described as a seasonal charge. The 2026 card states that "a 3.5% fuel and logistics-related surcharge will be applied to MCF fulfillment fees in the US." <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-19"/> It gives no start date and does not say whether the listed peak rates already include it, so the table leaves it out; <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-12"/> check your invoices to see how it was applied. Details are in our [fuel and logistics surcharge guide](/guides/fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge-2026/).

## The storage step-up in numbers

The October–December storage rate is a multiple of the rest of the year, and the size of the jump depends on size tier.

Dangerous goods follow the same calendar at higher rates: $0.99 rising to $3.63 per cubic foot for large standard. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-04"/>

## Charges tied to inventory age, not the calendar

Two surcharges in Amazon's fee policy are triggered by inventory age and volume, not by the calendar:

- **Aged inventory surcharge**: applies to inventory stored in fulfillment centers longer than 181 days. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-09"/>
- **Storage utilization surcharge**: applies if storage utilization surpasses 22 weeks of cover, with inventory aged 0 to 30 days excluded. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-10"/> <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-11"/>

## Why next year's schedule may differ

Amazon says holiday peak fulfillment fees "vary from year to year and are published annually." <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-14"/> The October–December storage season has been in place longer: Amazon's November 2022 fee notice already referred to "Peak storage fees (October to December)." <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-15"/> For notice periods, the 2026 fee announcement was published October 15, 2025 and promised at least 90 days before any fee increase takes effect. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-01"/> <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-03"/> One upstream option sits outside the seasonal calendar: Amazon describes AWD pricing as having "no additional costs for the holiday season." <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-13"/>

## How to confirm the dates for your account

Check the MCF rate card at supplychain.amazon.com/docs/rate-card (linked from supplychain.amazon.com/mcf/pricing) for the current peak window and surcharge, and the MCF fees policy for the aged inventory and storage utilization thresholds. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-06"/> <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-09"/> To see which month and rate Amazon applied to each ASIN, request the FBA Storage Fees Report, which contains estimated monthly storage fees per ASIN and includes month_of_charge, storage_rate and utilization_surcharge_rate fields. <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-17"/> <C id="peak-surcharge-2026-schedule-18"/> FBA peak fulfillment dates and amounts for Amazon orders are in Seller Central's fee notices. This calendar is built from Amazon's published pages. If a charge falls in a different month than shown, take it up in a Seller Central support case.
