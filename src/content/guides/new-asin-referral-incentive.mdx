The first version of this article said Amazon was discounting referral fees on the first $25,000 of revenue per new branded ASIN. That number came from a news roundup, and it was wrong. Amazon runs two programs that get blended together in secondhand summaries, and the one that changed on July 30 is unit-based, not dollar-based. Here is what it actually does, and what the other program does.

## Two programs, one confusion

| | FBA New Selection Program (2026) | New Seller Incentives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | Any Professional seller launching a new-to-FBA branded parent ASIN | Sellers in their first year, brand enrolled in Brand Registry within six months |
| Benefit | Referral fee capped at 10% on units 1–100 and 5% on units 101–200; 120 days free storage, free returns and liquidations on the first 200 units | 10% back on the first $50,000 of eligible branded sales, then 5% on the next $950,000; plus ad, coupon, Vine and inbound shipping credits |
| Basis | Per parent ASIN, by units | Per account, by dollars |
| Applied | At the point of sale as a fee credit | Monthly, as credits against next month's referral fees |
| Window | Introductory offer through October 31, 2026 | One year from first buyable ASIN, or until the cap |

The $25,000 figure does not appear in either. If you read it somewhere, the writer added two programs together and divided by nothing.

## What the 2026 New Selection Program pays

Take a $30 product in a 15% referral category.

| Units | Standard referral fee | Capped fee | Saving per unit | Saving on the band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–100 | $4.50 (15%) | $3.00 (10%) | $1.50 | $150 |
| 101–200 | $4.50 (15%) | $1.50 (5%) | $3.00 | $300 |
| 201+ | $4.50 | $4.50 | — | — |

$450 per launch on referral alone. Then the waivers: 120 days of monthly storage on 200 units (small money off-peak, real money if the 120 days cross October), free return processing on those units, and free liquidation if the launch fails — which is the waiver most sellers never price, and the one that makes a failed launch cheaper to exit.

Categories with referral rates already at or under 10% get less from the first band and the full benefit from the second. An 8% category saves nothing on units 1–100 and 3 points on 101–200.

## What qualifies

- A **new-to-FBA parent ASIN** — the first time that parent has FBA inventory received. Existing ASINs moved from FBM do not qualify.
- **Branded**: the ASIN carries a brand enrolled in Brand Registry, or you are the brand owner.
- **Professional selling plan.**
- **First inventory received on or after July 30, 2026** to get the 2026 terms. Inventory received July 29 is on the old rebate structure.
- Enrollment confirmed before **October 31, 2026** for ASINs listed after that date; sellers already in the old program were migrated on July 30.

`Seller Central > Growth > Explore Programs > FBA New Selection` shows enrollment status and the ASIN eligibility list. `Seller Central > Reports > Fulfillment > Payments > Fee Preview` shows the capped referral fee against qualifying units once they start selling; check that the credit is actually appearing on the second settlement after launch.

## Where this changes launch math

The credit lands in the first 200 units, which is the launch window where you are also spending most on advertising. On the $30 example, $450 of fee relief is roughly a week of launch PPC. Treat it as ad budget you did not have to fund, not as margin.

The liquidation waiver changes the failure case. Under the [launch sequence guide](/guides/launch-sequence/) a failed launch normally ends in removal fees or liquidation at a fraction of cost. Free liquidation on the first 200 units means the downside on a small test order is the inventory itself, not the inventory plus the exit.

The storage waiver has a date trap. 120 days from a September 15 receipt runs to January 13 — through the whole $2.40 peak storage period. 120 days from a June 1 receipt runs out on September 29, the day before rates triple. Time launches so the waiver covers Q4 if you can.

## What to do this month

- **List every launch planned before October 31 and confirm enrollment** on the program page. Automatic migration is not the same as verified coverage.
- **Move any launch sitting on the fence to before October 31.** The introductory terms are the ones worth having.
- **Price the first 200 units at the capped fee** in your launch model, and the units after that at the full rate. A launch model built on 10% all the way through will be wrong from unit 201.
- **Stop quoting $25,000.** If your launch spreadsheet has that number in it, it came from the wrong program.
