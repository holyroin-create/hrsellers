While the fee news this season has mostly run one direction, the brand-incentive side moved the other way: the 2026 program is reported to reduce referral fees on the **first $25,000 of revenue per new branded ASIN, for 365 days** — with existing participants **auto-migrated on 30 July**. Free money is rare on this platform; unclaimed free money is common. This is about making sure you are not in the second group.

## What it is worth, and what to check

The value scales with launch cadence: at a 15% category rate, each percentage point of discount on a fully-earned $25,000 is $250 per ASIN. A brand launching regularly compounds that into four figures a year — money that arrives only if the launches are enrolled and the crediting actually lands. The checklist: confirm your Brand Registry status, find the program's terms for your category in Seller Central, and — the step everyone skips — **reconcile the discount on your settlement** once a discounted ASIN starts selling. Incentives that are not audited have a way of underdelivering quietly; the same audit discipline applies to the account-level incentive stack — settlement beats assumption every time.
