If you own a trademark, copyright or patent and see an infringing listing, Amazon gives you two filing routes: Report a Violation if your brand is enrolled in Brand Registry, and the Report Infringement form in Seller Central if it is not. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-34"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-35"/> For a US utility patent, Amazon also runs a neutral evaluation, APEX, in which each side deposits $5,000 (Amazon's Brand Registry page, checked Nov 9, 2026). <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-41"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-39"/> This page covers both sides of a complaint: what the filer needs, and what the seller on the receiving end can see in Amazon's own account data.

The trademark itself, and how to get one, is covered in the [trademark and Brand Registry guide](/guides/brand-registry-trademark/).

## Which right are you relying on?

A complaint is only as good as the right behind it, and Amazon's own definitions are short: copyrights protect original works of authorship, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-30"/> trademarks protect words, symbols, designs or combinations used to identify a company, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-31"/> and patents protect inventions. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-32"/>

| Right | What the official source says | What it means for an Amazon report |
|---|---|---|
| Trademark | Registration is not required; use ® once the mark is registered | Brand Registry still needs a pending or registered mark |
| Utility patent | For a new or improved and useful process, machine, article of manufacture or composition of matter | The only patent type APEX evaluates |
| Design patent | For a new, original and ornamental design for an article of manufacture | Not an APEX case; use the standard reporting route |
| Copyright | Protects original works of authorship | Report as copyright, not trademark |

*Source: USPTO trademark and patent basics pages; Amazon, sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-61"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-62"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-59"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-60"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-41"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-30"/>

To enroll, Amazon requires a pending or registered trademark for the brand name or logo, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-48"/> issued by the designated government trademark office of a country with a corresponding Amazon store, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-49"/> and the mark must be a text-based word mark or an image-based mark with words, letters or numbers. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-50"/> If you have no trademark yet, Amazon's IP Accelerator can connect you with Amazon-vetted legal service providers; <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-52"/> Amazon says it has helped more than 33,000 brands obtain new trademark protection. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-53"/>

## Filing and tracking in Report a Violation

Report a Violation covers IP infringement, store policy violations and regulatory compliance concerns. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-36"/> Since Amazon's July 31, 2026 update it is a single guided experience for IP reports, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-12"/> one landing page consolidates all violation reporting, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-38"/> and structured fields have replaced free-text forms. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-15"/> Access still requires Brand Registry enrollment. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-14"/>

After filing, a Submission History tab tracks every report's status and outcome in real time. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-13"/> The Amazon pages checked for this guide on Nov 9, 2026 do not list the individual form fields, a review time or how to withdraw a report you filed; check Brand Registry help for those.

## What the seller on the other side sees

A seller can look in two places in Amazon's own data. At account level, Amazon's Seller Performance Report schema tracks receivedIntellectualPropertyComplaints and suspectedIntellectualPropertyViolations as separate metrics, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-54"/> each with a reporting date range, a defects count, a status and a target. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-55"/> At listing level, the Listings Items API records enforcement actions: LISTING_SUPPRESSED takes down a listing's buyability, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-56"/> and CATALOG_ITEM_REMOVED means the catalog item is inactive and all offers against it in that store are non-buyable. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-57"/> Amazon's LISTINGS_ITEM_ISSUES_CHANGE notification reports issues that make a listing inactive or search-suppressed. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-58"/>

How these complaints feed the overall account rating is covered in the [account suspension appeal guide](/guides/account-suspension-appeal/). The detailed process for a reported seller to respond to a rights owner is published in Seller Central Help, not on Amazon's public sites.

## Patent disputes: APEX

For US brands with a utility patent, Brand Registry offers a neutral evaluation process within Report a Violation. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-41"/> The evaluators are attorneys skilled in patent analysis who decide whether an asserted utility patent is infringed. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-42"/>

| APEX term | What Amazon publishes |
|---|---|
| Deposit | $5,000 from each party, paid to the neutral evaluator (checked Nov 9, 2026) |
| Refund | The winning participant gets its $5,000 back |
| Amazon's role | Follows the evaluator's decision; does not handle the deposit or keep any of the fee |
| Notice | Sellers with listings for the reported products are notified and can take part and defend their claim |
| Outcome | Infringing products are removed; if the seller wins, Amazon takes no action |
| Afterward | A winning brand can use the evaluation ID to report future infringement of that patent |
| Speed | Decided in around 30 days, versus 2+ years to reach trial |

*Source: Amazon, Brand Registry page on sell.amazon.com and 2025 Trustworthy Shopping Experience Report, checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-39"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-40"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-19"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-21"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-20"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-46"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-44"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-45"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-47"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-22"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): what APEX can cost each side">
Inputs: a patent owner reports one seller under APEX, and the seller takes part.

- Deposits paid to the evaluator: 2 × $5,000 = $10,000. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-39"/>
- The side the evaluator finds correct gets its $5,000 back, so APEX is free for it. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-40"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-43"/>
- Amazon's page describes a refund only for the winner's deposit, and says Amazon does not handle the deposit or keep any part of the evaluator's fee. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-21"/>
- If the patent owner wins, the reported products are removed and the evaluation ID can be reused for later reports on the same patent. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-44"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-47"/>

For either side, the stake is one $5,000 deposit against a decision Amazon says comes in around 30 days. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-22"/>
</Callout>

## Tools that reduce the need to file

Amazon reports that its proactive controls blocked more than 99.9% of suspected infringing listings before brands had to report them, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-23"/> and that valid notices of infringement feed its machine learning systems. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-24"/> Project Zero gives brands the ability to block and remove listings immediately. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-51"/> For counterfeits, Amazon says it seized and disposed of more than 15 million counterfeit products worldwide in 2025, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-27"/> and that its Counterfeit Crimes Unit has pursued more than 32,000 bad actors since 2020. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-26"/> Any Brand Registry user of an enrolled brand can also report store policy violations through the same tool. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-28"/>

<Callout type="warning" title="Scope">
An Amazon complaint is a marketplace process, not a court ruling on infringement. Whether a listing actually infringes your mark, copyright or patent is a legal question; for US utility patents on Amazon, APEX puts it to a neutral evaluator, and anything beyond that belongs with a patent or trademark attorney.
</Callout>
