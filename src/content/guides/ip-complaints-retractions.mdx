If you own a trademark, copyright or patent and see an infringing listing, Amazon gives you two filing routes: Report a Violation if your brand is in Brand Registry, and the Public Notice Form if it is not. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-02"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-03"/> Amazon calls the Brand Registry protections more robust than the public form. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-04"/> A report you filed can also be retracted from its complaint details. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-10"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-11"/>

This page is written for the party filing. The trademark itself, and how to get one, is covered in the [trademark and Brand Registry guide](/guides/brand-registry-trademark/).

For agents, Amazon's Brand Registry page says an authorized agent should have the trademark owner enroll the brand and then be added as an additional Brand Registry account user. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-33"/>

Report a Violation covers suspected copyright, trademark and patent infringement. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-01"/> Since Amazon's July 31, 2026 update, it is a single guided experience for IP reports, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-12"/> structured fields have replaced free-text forms, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-15"/> and access still requires Brand Registry enrollment. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-14"/>

*Source: Amazon, \"How to report an IP violation to Amazon\" on sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-05"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-06"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-07"/>

Amazon's post defines the three rights: copyrights protect original works of authorship; <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-30"/> trademarks protect words, symbols and designs that identify a company; <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-31"/> patents protect inventions. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-32"/> Amazon's advice is to be specific about which one applies. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-06"/>

After submission, Amazon reviews the complaint. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-08"/> Amazon's post does not give a review time or say what action is taken on the reported listing.

## Common filing mistakes

| Mistake | What to do instead |
|---|---|
| Filing everything as "trademark" | Match the report to the type of right: copyright for original works, trademark for brand identifiers, patent for inventions |
| Reporting one listing at a time | Report a Violation searches up to 50 ASINs or URLs at once |
| Leaving out the store | Reports ask which Amazon store the violation appears in |
| Using the public form after enrolling | Enrolled brands get Report a Violation, which Amazon calls more robust |
| Leaving a mistaken report open | Retract it from the complaint's details page |
| Losing track of outcomes | Use the Submission History tab |

## Patent disputes: APEX

For utility patents, Amazon Patent Evaluation Express brings in neutral third-party evaluators to decide whether an asserted utility patent is infringed. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-16"/> The terms:

| APEX term | What Amazon publishes |
|---|---|
| Deposit | $5,000 from each party, paid to the neutral evaluator |
| Refund | The winning participant gets its $5,000 back |
| Amazon's role | Follows the evaluator's decision; does not handle the deposit or keep any of the fee |
| Notice | Sellers with listings for the reported products are notified |
| Speed | Decided in around 30 days, versus 2+ years to reach trial |

*Source: Amazon, Brand Registry page on sell.amazon.com and 2025 Trustworthy Shopping Experience Report, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-17"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-18"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-19"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-21"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-20"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-22"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): what APEX can cost each side">
Inputs: a patent owner reports one seller under APEX, and the seller takes part.

- Deposits paid to the evaluator: 2 × $5,000 = $10,000. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-17"/>
- The side the evaluator finds correct gets its $5,000 back, so APEX is free for it. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-18"/> <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-29"/>
- Amazon's page describes a refund only for the winning participant's deposit.

For a patent owner, the stake is the $5,000 deposit, against a decision Amazon says comes in around 30 days. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-22"/>
</Callout>

## Tools that reduce the need to file

Amazon reports that its proactive controls blocked more than 99.9% of suspected infringing listings before brands had to report them, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-23"/> and that valid notices of infringement feed its machine learning systems. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-24"/> Project Zero lets brands search for counterfeit listings and remove them immediately. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-25"/>

For counterfeits, Amazon says it seized and disposed of more than 15 million counterfeit products worldwide in 2025, <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-27"/> and that its Counterfeit Crimes Unit has pursued more than 32,000 bad actors since 2020. <C id="ip-complaints-retractions-26"/>

<Callout type="warning" title="Scope">
An Amazon complaint is a marketplace process, not a court ruling on infringement. Whether a listing actually infringes your mark, copyright or patent is a legal question; for utility patents on Amazon, APEX puts it to a neutral evaluator. Take legal advice before filing.
</Callout>
