Run one hypothetical $25.00 item through each platform's published rates and the per-sale fees come out as: Amazon $3.75 on the Professional plan, Walmart $3.75, eBay $3.80, Etsy about $2.83 (or $6.58 when Etsy attributes the order to Offsite Ads), and Shopify Basic about $1.03 in card fees. Amazon's Professional plan and Shopify's plans add a monthly fee on top; Walmart states it has no monthly fee.

These are arithmetic results from each platform's own fee pages, under the assumptions stated below. They do not include fulfillment, shipping labels, advertising other than Etsy's Offsite Ads, or the cost of bringing buyers to a standalone store. Each platform's full fee reference is linked at the end.

## Assumptions for the worked example

Worked example (hypothetical):

- One unit sold at a $25.00 item price, $0 shipping charged, no gift wrap, no sales tax collected.
- A home goods item: Amazon Home and Kitchen, Walmart Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden, and eBay's most-categories rate.
- Seller-fulfilled everywhere; no FBA or WFS fees.
- eBay listing within the monthly zero insertion fee allowance; Above Standard account.
- Etsy: US seller with a US bank account; one listing fee per sale.
- Shopify: Basic plan, Shopify Payments, standard online card.

*Worked example using rates from Amazon (sell.amazon.com/pricing), Walmart Marketplace (referral fees), eBay Help (selling fees), Etsy (fees and payments policies) and Shopify (pricing), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-15" />

Working, row by row:

- **Amazon Professional:** 15% × $25.00 = $3.75, above the $0.30 minimum <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-15" />.
- **Amazon Individual:** $3.75 + $0.99 = $4.74.
- **Walmart:** 15% × $25.00 = $3.75.
- **eBay:** 13.6% × $25.00 = $3.40, plus $0.40 because the order is over $10.00 = $3.80.
- **Etsy:** $0.20 + (6.5% × $25.00 = $1.625) + (3% × $25.00 + $0.25 = $1.00) = $2.825, shown as $2.83. With Offsite Ads at 15%, add $3.75 for $6.575, shown as $6.58.
- **Shopify Basic:** 2.9% × $25.00 = $0.725, plus $0.30 = $1.025, shown as $1.03.

A dash in the fixed-cost column means no fixed cost was used in this example. Etsy's $0.20 is charged per listing whether or not it sells <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-09" />, and is counted in the per-sale fee here on the assumption of one listing per sale.

| Amazon, Professional plan | $3.75 | $21.25 | 15.0% |
| Amazon, Individual plan | $4.74 | $20.26 | 19.0% |
| Walmart Marketplace | $3.75 | $21.25 | 15.0% |
| eBay | $3.80 | $21.20 | 15.2% |
| Etsy, not attributed | $2.83 | $22.18 | 11.3% |
| Etsy, attributed at 15% | $6.58 | $18.43 | 26.3% |

*Worked example using the rates cited in the comparison table above, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-15" />

Etsy and Shopify amounts are computed from unrounded fees ($2.825, $6.575 and $1.025) and then rounded, so they differ by a cent from subtracting the rounded fee. The Individual-plan percentage is 19.0% because the $0.99 per-item fee alone is about 4% of a $25.00 price.

## What changes the result

### Shipping charged to the buyer

Each marketplace fee base shown here includes shipping. Amazon's total price includes shipping costs <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-16" />; Walmart's total sales price includes shipping and handling <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-17" />; eBay's total amount of the sale includes shipping collected from the buyer <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-18" />; Etsy's transaction fee applies to shipping <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-19" /> and its processing fee to the gross order amount <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-20" />.

Worked example (hypothetical): the same item at $25.00 plus $5.00 shipping, $30.00 total, with the Shopify card charged for the full $30.00.

| Platform | Per-sale fee on $30.00 |
|---|---|
| Amazon Professional (15%) | $4.50 |
| Walmart (15%) | $4.50 |
| eBay (13.6% + $0.40) | $4.48 |
| Etsy, no Offsite Ad | $3.30 |
| Shopify Basic (2.9% + 30¢) | $1.17 |

*Worked example using the same sourced rates, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-18" />

### Sales tax

eBay's total amount of the sale includes sales tax <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-18" />, and Etsy's processing fee is assessed on the gross order including tax <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-20" />. For US sellers, Etsy's transaction fee does not apply to sales tax <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-21" />. With $2.00 of tax on the $25.00 sale, eBay's fee rises to $4.07 and Etsy's to $2.89.

### Category

The 15% Amazon and Walmart figures are for home goods. Other categories differ: Consumer Electronics is 8% on both Amazon <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-22" /> and Walmart <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-23" />, or $2.00 on $25.00. For apparel at $25.00, Amazon's Clothing and Accessories rate is 17% <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-24" />, or $4.25, and Walmart's Apparel & Accessories rate is 15% <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-25" />, or $3.75.

### Volume and the monthly fees

Amazon's $39.99 Professional fee and Shopify's plan fee are fixed per month, so their cost per order depends on how many orders you have. Worked example (hypothetical): at 50 orders a month, $39.99 is $0.80 per order and Shopify Basic billed yearly ($29) is $0.58 per order; at 500 orders, $0.08 and $0.06. Amazon's Individual plan replaces the monthly fee with $0.99 per item <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-03" />; above about 40 items a month, Professional costs less in plan fees.

## What the table does not include

- **Fulfillment.** FBA costs are based on price, weight and dimensions <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-26" />; WFS fulfillment starts at $3.45 for items of 1 lb. or less <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-27" />. Neither is in the comparison.
- **Etsy's Offsite Ads frequency.** Only attributed orders pay it <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-28" />; see [Etsy Offsite Ads economics](/guides/etsy-offsite-ads-economics/).
- **Shopify third-party providers.** Using a provider other than Shopify Payments adds a 2% Shopify fee on Basic <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-29" /> plus that provider's own charges.
- **eBay surcharges.** Below Standard accounts pay an additional 6% on final value fees <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-30" />.
