Sell a $25 kitchen item through each platform's published selling fees and the per-order cost comes out at about $3.75 on Amazon and Walmart (15% referral), $3.80 on eBay (13.6% + $0.40), $2.83 on Etsy without an ad, and $1.03 in card fees on Shopify Basic — before monthly plan fees. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-03"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-07"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-10"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-16"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-22"/> Monthly fees, ad fees and what each platform counts in its fee base change the picture at different sales volumes, so the comparison below keeps every input visible.

## The setup

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): inputs used throughout">
One unit of a $25 kitchen item. The buyer pays $25 total (free shipping; the seller's postage cost is not included). Seller ships the order itself (no FBA or WFS). No sales tax in the base unless stated. Category: Amazon Home and Kitchen; Walmart Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden; eBay "Most categories"; Shopify Basic plan with its online card rate. Results rounded to the cent.
</Callout>

Only published selling fees are counted: referral or final value fees, per-order fees, listing fees, payment processing and plan fees. Postage, returns, optional advertising (other than Etsy's Offsite Ads, shown separately) and fulfillment services are left out. Those costs are real, but they depend on your packaging, carrier and return rate rather than on a published rate card, so including them would hide the fee comparison inside your own assumptions.

## Each platform's fee, one at a time

**Amazon.** The referral fee is a percentage of the total price or a minimum, whichever is greater; Home and Kitchen is 15% with a $0.30 minimum. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-01"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-03"/> Total price includes the list price plus shipping and gift-wrap charges. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-02"/> On $25: **$3.75**. Plan fee on top: $0.99 per item sold (Individual) or $39.99 per month (Professional). <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-04"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-05"/>

**Walmart.** Referral fees vary by category and are deducted only once a sale completes; Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden is 15%. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-06"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-07"/> The total sales price includes item price plus shipping and handling, gift wrap and other charges. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-08"/> On $25: **$3.75**. Walmart states there are "zero setup, monthly, or hidden fees." <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-09"/>

**eBay.** The final value fee is a percentage of the total amount of the sale plus a per-order fee. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-14"/> In most categories it is 13.6% on the total up to $7,500, and the per-order fee is $0.30 for orders of $10.00 or less and $0.40 above. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-10"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-11"/> On $25: 13.6% = $3.40, plus $0.40 = **$3.80**. Listings are free up to 250 a month, then $0.35 each. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-13"/>

**Etsy.** A $0.20 listing fee per item listed, a 6.5% transaction fee on the displayed price plus shipping and gift wrap, and, for US bank accounts, payment processing of 3% + $0.25. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-15"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-16"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-17"/> On $25: $0.20 + $1.63 + $1.00 = **$2.83**. If the order is attributed to an Offsite Ad, add 15% ($3.75), or 12% for shops that crossed $10,000 in sales over the prior 365 days. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-19"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-20"/>

**Shopify.** The Basic plan is $39 a month billed monthly, or $29 a month billed yearly, with an online standard card rate of 2.9% + 30¢. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-21"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-22"/> On $25: $0.73 + $0.30 = **$1.03**. If you use a third-party payment provider instead of Shopify Payments, a third-party transaction fee may apply — 2% on Basic, or $0.50 on this order. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-23"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-24"/>

## Adding monthly fees: 50 orders a month

Per-order fees rank the platforms one way; fixed monthly fees can rank them another. Using the same $25 order, 50 times in a month:

| Platform | Calculation | Fees for 50 orders | Per order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amazon Professional | 50 × $3.75 + $39.99 | $227.49 | $4.55 |
| Amazon Individual | 50 × ($3.75 + $0.99) | $237.00 | $4.74 |
| Walmart | 50 × $3.75 | $187.50 | $3.75 |
| eBay (within 250 free listings) | 50 × $3.80 | $190.00 | $3.80 |
| Etsy, no Offsite Ads orders | 50 × $2.83 (unrounded $2.825) | $141.25 | $2.83 |
| Etsy, every order via Offsite Ads at 15% | 50 × $6.575 | $328.75 | $6.58 |
| Shopify Basic, billed monthly | 50 × $1.025 + $39 | $90.25 | $1.81 |
| Shopify Basic, billed yearly | 50 × $1.025 + $29 | $80.25 | $1.61 |

*Source: fee rates from sell.amazon.com, marketplace.walmart.com, ebay.com/help, etsy.com/legal and shopify.com/pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026; arithmetic is ours.* <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-05"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-13"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-21"/>

Shopify's monthly fee is fixed, so its per-order cost falls as volume rises: at 10 orders a month it is $4.93 per order ($1.025 + $39 ÷ 10), above Amazon Individual ($4.74), Walmart and eBay in this example, though still below Amazon Professional at that volume ($3.75 + $39.99 ÷ 10 = $7.75). Amazon's two plans cost the same at about 40 orders a month ($39.99 ÷ $0.99). <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-04"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-05"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-21"/> These figures cover fees only; each platform also differs in traffic, shipping expectations and services, none of which is priced here.

## Changing the price: $15 and $50

Percentage fees scale with price, but fixed fees do not, so the ranking can shift as the item gets cheaper or more expensive. The same inputs at two other prices:

| Platform | Fee on a $15 order | Fee on a $50 order |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon (15%) | $2.25 | $7.50 |
| Walmart (15%) | $2.25 | $7.50 |
| eBay (13.6% + $0.40) | $2.44 | $7.20 |
| Etsy, no ad ($0.20 + 6.5% + 3% + $0.25) | $1.88 | $5.20 |
| Shopify Basic (2.9% + 30¢) | $0.74 | $1.75 |

*Source: rates from each platform's fee page, checked Nov 8, 2026; arithmetic is ours.* <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-07"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-10"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-11"/>

At $15, eBay costs more than Amazon or Walmart because its $0.40 per-order fee is 2.7% of the sale on top of 13.6%. At $50, the fixed fee shrinks to 0.8% and eBay comes in below the 15% referral platforms. Etsy's fixed $0.45 behaves the same way. On orders of $10.00 or less eBay's per-order fee drops to $0.30, and Amazon's $0.30 minimum only takes over below $2.00 in a 15% category. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-11"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-01"/>

## Common mistakes when comparing

| Mistake | Correct approach |
|---|---|
| Comparing headline percentages only | Add fixed fees: eBay's $0.40 per order and Etsy's $0.25 + $0.20 are 1.6% and 1.8% of a $25 sale. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-11"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-17"/> |
| Ignoring monthly fees at low volume | Spread $39.99 (Amazon Professional) or $39 (Shopify Basic) over your actual monthly orders. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-05"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-21"/> |
| Leaving out Etsy Offsite Ads | An attributed order adds 15% (or 12%) of the order total. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-19"/> |
| Using the Shopify card rate with another processor | A third-party transaction fee (2% on Basic) may apply on top. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-23"/> <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-24"/> |
| Applying one category's rate everywhere | Each platform publishes category-specific rates; recheck for your product's category. <C id="ebay-etsy-for-amazon-sellers-06"/> |
