No. As of November 8, 2026, the $800 de minimis exemption is **suspended across the board**. Executive Order 14388 says that, for articles not covered by 50 U.S.C. 1702(b), it does not apply "regardless of value, country of origin, mode of transportation, or method of entry." <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-02"/> CBP has since put the suspension into its own regulations, one rule for courier and freight shipments and one for mail. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-05"/> <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-11"/> A low-value parcel now needs an informal or formal entry, and the applicable duties, taxes and fees are collected. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-06"/> <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-10"/>

## Current status at a glance

<Stat value="Suspended" label="Duty-free de minimis for imports valued at $800 or less, all origins and modes" claim="de-minimis-suspension-2026-25" />

CBP's postal rule describes the exemption it suspends as covering imports valued at $800 or less. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-25"/> The suspension has three layers, and all three point the same way:

| Layer | Document | Where it stands |
|---|---|---|
| Presidential | Executive Order 14388 (Feb 20, 2026) | Applies to goods entered on or after Feb 24, 2026 |
| CBP, non-postal | Interim final rule 2026-12670 | Effective June 24, 2026 |
| CBP, postal | Interim final rule 2026-12669 | Effective July 24, 2026 |
| Statute | Public Law 119-21 | Ends the exemption effective July 1, 2027 |

The February 2026 Supreme Court decision ended the IEEPA tariffs but did not bring de minimis back. CBP says the decision did not address the suspension of the de minimis exemption. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-04"/> The President continued the suspension the same day, effective for goods entered on or after 12:01 a.m. EST on February 24, 2026. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-03"/>

## Courier, express and freight parcels

CBP's non-postal rule took effect June 24, 2026. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-05"/> Shipments valued at $800 or less that arrive by any mode other than international mail must use formal or informal entry. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-06"/> CBP says Entry Type 11 is the main informal entry method for these shipments, though formal entry is still an option. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-07"/> Informal entry is allowed for shipments valued at $2,500 or less. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-08"/> CBP has also suspended the Entry Type 86 Test. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-09"/>

Duty is owed on these parcels. CBP says E.O. 14388 confirmed that CBP should keep collecting applicable duties, taxes, fees, exactions and charges on them. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-10"/> The processing fee for an automated informal entry not prepared by CBP is $2.77 in fiscal year 2027. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-21"/>

## What the suspension does not touch

Two other low-value exemptions are separate from de minimis. CBP states that the exemption for bona fide gifts valued at $100 or less and the exemption for personal or household articles of $200 or less accompanying travelers remain unchanged. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-20"/> Neither helps a marketplace seller. An order a customer paid for is not a gift, and goods shipped to a buyer do not travel with a person arriving in the country.

## What marketplace sellers should do now

If you ship low-value orders straight from overseas suppliers to US buyers, each parcel is now a dutiable import. Price your listings with duty and the entry fee included. Under the courier route, the fee alone is $2.77 per automated informal entry. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-21"/>

More change is on the way. CBP's October 8, 2026 proposal would create entry type 13, Informal Mail Entry, for mail shipments valued at $2,500 or less. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-23"/> Comments are due December 7, 2026. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-24"/> By statute, CBP says the exemption ends July 1, 2027. <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-22"/>

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What the documents say |
|---|---|
| Assuming the Supreme Court ruling restored de minimis | CBP says the decision did not address the suspension <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-04"/> |
| Treating parcels under $800 as duty-free | The exemption is suspended regardless of value <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-02"/> |
| Filing Entry Type 86 | CBP suspended the Entry Type 86 Test <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-09"/> |
| Using postal informal entry for goods under AD/CVD orders | Those goods must use formal entry <C id="de-minimis-suspension-2026-15"/> |
