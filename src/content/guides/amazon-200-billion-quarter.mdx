Amazon posted its **first $200 billion quarter** — Q2 net sales up 20% to $200.6 billion. For sellers the headline hides the real line: **advertising grew 26% to $19.8 billion**, outpacing the store again. When the ad business grows faster than the marketplace it advertises, the shelf is being repriced — that is the pay-to-play pressure every organic-rank chart quietly records.

## What a seller does with a macro number

Not despair, and not a bigger budget by reflex — arithmetic. Rising auction pressure raises the value of everything that improves conversion (which lowers your [cost per ad order](/guides/amazon-ppc-benchmarks/) at any CPC) and everything that earns unpaid discovery, from [listing structure](/guides/listing-optimisation-a10/) to the AI-era work in our Rufus coverage. And it puts a date on the calendar: **Accelerate, 22-24 September in Seattle.** After a half-year of AI-led platform change, the seller press expects direction-setting announcements for 2027 — we will read them the way this site reads everything: dated, sourced, and priced per unit. If anything announced moves a fee, it lands on the [policy log](/policy-log/) the same week.
