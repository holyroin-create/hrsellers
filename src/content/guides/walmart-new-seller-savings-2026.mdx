Amazon raised fees in January. TikTok raised them in August. Walmart, third in the queue, is doing the opposite for one audience only: sellers it does not have yet. New-Seller Savings 2026 is the most concrete acquisition offer any major US marketplace is running this year — and because the numbers come from Walmart's own program page with terms attached, they can be priced rather than guessed at.

## What the program contains, line by line

| Component | Offer, per Walmart | Mechanics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referral fees | 20% off base fees to $50K GMV · 30% off $50K–$500K · 40% off above $500K | Opt in on the payments page after go-live |
| WFS credits | Up to $2,000 | Taken as 10% off WFS-fulfilled items until the cap is reached |
| Search Engine Marketing | Up to $1,000 in credits | Reimbursement after eligible ad spend, for new advertisers |
| Walmart Connect | Up to $500 in credits | On enrollment as a new advertiser |

The window: sellers must go live **after 1 February 2026**, and the program runs to **31 January 2027**. Walmart's FAQ also opens the door internationally — the US marketplace is listed as open to sellers based in Canada, Chile, China, Germany, India, Japan, Mexico, Singapore, South Korea, Thailand, Turkey, the United States, the United Kingdom and Vietnam.

## The worked math: what the discount is worth at your size

Walmart's referral rates run 6–15% by category. Take a $25 product in a 15% category:

<Figure value="$0.75" kind="recovered" source="Worked example on Walmart's published tiers; checked August 2026">
  Standard referral on a $25 unit is $3.75. With the first-tier 20% discount it is $3.00 — $0.75 recovered per unit, on every unit of your first $50K in GMV. That first tier alone is worth up to $1,500 in a 15% category.
</Figure>

Now the honest ceiling. Reaching the headline "up to $75K" (Walmart itemises "up to $72K off referral fees" plus the credits) requires sustained GMV **well beyond $500K in year one** — a scale almost no genuinely new seller hits. Price the program at your own forecast instead: a seller doing $100K in first-year Walmart GMV in a 15% category captures roughly $1,500 from tier one plus about $2,250 from tier two — around $3,750, before WFS and ad credits. Real money. Not a reason to build a channel; a reason to stop postponing one you already wanted.

One more calibration: the surrounding claims on the program page — 270 million weekly customers, 50% average GMV growth for WFS-tagged items, 7x sales for Walmart Connect advertisers — are Walmart's own first-party marketing data, dated windows and all. Read them as a vendor's best foot forward, not as your forecast.

## Where this meets the decision you were already weighing

This site's position on Walmart has not changed: it is the most credible second channel for an established Amazon seller, and our [Walmart fee page](/marketplaces/walmart/) prices the WFS stack line by line. What the program changes is **timing math**. The [second-marketplace decision](/guides/second-marketplace-decision/) usually turns on whether early, thin volume justifies the setup cost; a first-year discount tier lowers exactly that hurdle. Two cautions stay in force, program or no program:

- **The fee page still wins.** A 20% discount on the referral line does not touch WFS fulfilment, storage, or the under-$10 economics. Run the full stack through the [margin calculator](/tools/margin-calculator/) per channel, discounted referral included.
- **Parity still binds.** Discounted fees make it tempting to price Walmart lower than Amazon. [Cross-channel price parity](/guides/cross-channel-price-parity/) policies mean that decision has consequences on your first channel — make it deliberately.

## The action plan — if Walmart was already on your list

**Sequence the go-live date.** Eligibility keys off going live after 1 February 2026, and the program ends 31 January 2027 — a store that goes live in September gets roughly five months of discounted Q4-and-beyond selling. Onboarding is stated at about 10 days on average; add your own catalog and WFS inbound time.

**Calendar the opt-in.** The discount is claimed on the payments page **after** go-live, per Walmart's FAQ. Put it in the launch checklist as a dated task, not a mental note.

**Model the credits as spend triggers, not free money.** The SEM credit reimburses after eligible spend and the WFS credit arrives as 10% off fulfilled items — both reward activity you would need to justify anyway. If the activity was not in your plan, $500 of credits should not put it there.
