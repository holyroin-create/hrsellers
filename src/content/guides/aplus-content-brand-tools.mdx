A+ Content costs nothing, but only certain accounts can publish it: you need a Professional selling account and either a Brand Representative or Reseller role for a brand enrolled in Brand Registry, or generic products in your catalog. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-08"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-05"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-06"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-07"/> The deeper brand tools are stricter. Brand Analytics and Manage Your Experiments both name the Brand Representative role as a requirement, not the Reseller role. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-20"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-29"/> So the first decision is not which module to design. It is which role your account holds.

If you do not yet have a trademark or a Brand Registry enrollment, start with the [trademark and Brand Registry guide](/guides/brand-registry-trademark/). This page assumes the brand is already enrolled.

## Which tools your account can use

Brand Registry itself is free. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-30"/> What you can do inside it depends on the role attached to your selling account.

"Not listed" means the tool's page names only the Brand Representative role; it does not say a Reseller can use the tool. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-20"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-29"/> If your account is set up only for enforcement work, ask the brand's Administrator which selling role it holds before you plan A+ work. The [authorized reseller guide](/guides/authorised-reseller/) covers how brands assign the Reseller role.

## A+ Content: Basic, Premium and Brand Story

A+ Content adds videos, enhanced images, customized text placements and shoppable comparison charts to your product detail pages. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-01"/> Amazon publishes three types.

| Type | What it adds |
|---|---|
| Basic | Images, customized text placements and shoppable product-comparison charts |
| Premium | Larger images, videos, interactive hotspots, clickable image or video carousels, Q&A modules and shoppable content |
| Brand Story | Your brand's story, values and mission across all your listings, with traffic sent to your Brand Store |

*Source: Amazon, A+ Content page on sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-02"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-03"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-04"/>

Amazon cites its own internal data that Basic A+ can increase sales by up to 8% and well-implemented Premium A+ by up to 20%. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-10"/> Read those as ceilings Amazon reports, not as a forecast for any one listing. The way to find out what A+ does for your product is to test it, which is what Manage Your Experiments is for (see below).

Two rules shape what you can publish. First, A+ Content can only be applied to brand-owned ASINs, <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-13"/> so a listing you merely sell on, for a brand you have no role with, cannot carry your modules. Second, approved content shows up automatically on the chosen detail pages, in the language you selected. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-14"/>

## Brand Stores

A Brand Store is a free storefront that needs no coding experience. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-15"/> Creating and maintaining one is free, <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-17"/> but only brands with a pending or registered trademark can create one. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-16"/> Brand Store Insights reports sales, visits, page views and traffic sources. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-18"/>

Brand Story and the Brand Store are linked: Brand Story runs across your listings and drives traffic to the store. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-04"/>

## Brand Analytics

Brand Analytics is a Seller Central tool for reviewing aggregate customer data, <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-19"/> and it needs the Brand Representative role. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-20"/> Three of the dashboards Amazon describes are relevant to A+ copy:

<Checklist title="Brand Analytics dashboards to check before writing A+ copy" items={[
  "Search Query Performance: the keywords customers use to find your brand",
  "Market Basket Analysis: products customers frequently buy together (candidates for a comparison chart)",
  "Demographics: sales by customer age, income, education, gender and marital status"

*Source: Amazon, Brand Analytics page on sell.amazon.com, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-21"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-22"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-23"/>

The Market Basket dashboard shows which products customers frequently buy together, and Basic A+ supports shoppable product-comparison charts. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-22"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-02"/>

## Manage Your Experiments

Manage Your Experiments tests versions of product images, titles, bullet points, descriptions and A+ Content, including Brand Story. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-24"/> It has three gates:

- the Brand Representative role for an enrolled brand <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-29"/>
- enough recent traffic on the product to produce valid results <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-26"/>
- for A+ or Brand Story tests, content already published <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-25"/>

When you set the duration yourself, Amazon recommends 8 to 10 weeks per experiment; <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-27"/> with the "to significance" setting, results sometimes arrive in as little as four weeks. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-28"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): planning an A+ test">
Inputs: a brand publishes Basic A+ on a product, then starts an A+ experiment on January 4, 2027, sets the duration itself and lets it run the full recommended window.

- 8 weeks = 56 days, so the earliest recommended end date is March 1, 2027. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-27"/>
- 10 weeks = 70 days, so the latest recommended end date is March 15, 2027.
- Had the brand chosen the "to significance" setting instead, the four-week mark would fall on February 1, 2027, the earliest point Amazon mentions for results. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-28"/>

In this example, a result from the full recommended window is not available until March 2027.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What Amazon's pages say instead |
|---|---|
| Building A+ for a listing you only resell on, with no brand role | A+ applies only to brand-owned ASINs, and needs a Brand Representative or Reseller role |
| Planning an A+ experiment before any A+ is live | A+ and Brand Story experiments need content already published |
| Testing a slow-selling product | The product needs enough recent traffic for valid results |
| Stopping a test at two or three weeks | Amazon recommends 8 to 10 weeks for a self-set duration; four weeks is the early case under "to significance" |
| Treating "up to 20%" as a forecast | It is a ceiling from Amazon's internal data for well-implemented Premium A+ |
| Writing a Brand Story with no Brand Store | Brand Story is designed to send traffic to the store, which is free with a pending or registered trademark |

*Source: Amazon, A+ Content and Manage Your Experiments pages on sell.amazon.com, and Amazon Ads Stores page, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-13"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-25"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-26"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-28"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-10"/> <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-17"/>

Selling in more than one language? Approved content appears on the chosen detail pages in the language you chose for it. <C id="aplus-content-brand-tools-14"/>
