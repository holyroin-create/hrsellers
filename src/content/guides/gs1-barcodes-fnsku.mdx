If you are a brand selling your own products, you need one GTIN for every product variation, and GS1 US is where those numbers are licensed: $30 each for a single GTIN with no renewal fee, or a Company Prefix starting at $250 plus $50 a year for up to 10 items. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-20"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-07"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-08"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-13"/> The right choice comes down to how many products you will barcode over the next few years.

## GTIN, UPC, EAN: the vocabulary

A GTIN is a GS1 identification key used to identify a trade item. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-01"/> "UPC" is the name most US sellers use, but it is one format of GTIN:

| GTIN type | What GS1 US says it is |
|---|---|
| GTIN-12 | Synonymous with UPC; the only type that can be encoded in a UPC-A barcode |
| GTIN-13 | Synonymous with EAN; the only type that can be encoded in an EAN-13 barcode |
| GTIN-14 | Used to identify trade item groupings |
| GTIN-8 | Most often used outside North America, for small items |

*Source: GS1 US, "What is a GTIN?", checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-02"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-03"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-04"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-05"/>

The number is the identity; the barcode is just how a scanner reads it. Barcodes issued by GS1 US uniquely identify a single retail product online and in retail stores around the world. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-06"/>

## Why the source of the number matters

GS1-issued barcodes identify your company as the brand owner of the product. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-21"/> That link between number and company is the reason to license numbers in the name of the company that owns the brand. With a Company Prefix, product data stays linked to your company information as long as you renew the membership every year. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-18"/>

The GTIN also turns up outside marketplaces. Under the CPSC certificates rule, a finished product certificate must carry at least one unique identifier, and a GTIN or UPC is among the accepted options. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-24"/> Using the same GTIN across your listing, your packaging and your compliance paperwork keeps the records consistent.

## Option 1: a single GS1 US GTIN

GS1 US licenses a single GTIN for $30, <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-07"/> with no annual renewal fee. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-08"/> The license includes a free lifetime subscription to GS1 US Data Hub. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-25"/> GS1 US itself says that if you have only a few products that need barcodes, this might be the most cost-effective option. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-19"/>

There are limits. A single GTIN is not available for pharmaceutical products, variable measure products, mixed cases, or coupons. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-09"/>

## Option 2: a GS1 Company Prefix

A Company Prefix lets you get multiple barcodes at a single time. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-10"/> It can also identify locations, mixed cases, coupons and higher packaging levels such as a case or pallet. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-11"/> Prefixes come in capacities, each representing the maximum number of unique products you can identify: <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-12"/>

| Capacity (items) | Initial fee | Annual renewal |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | $250 | $50 |
| 100 | $750 | $150 |
| 1,000 | $2,500 | $500 |
| 10,000 | $6,500 | $1,300 |
| 100,000 | $10,500 | $2,100 |

*Source: GS1 US, "Get a barcode," checked Nov 8, 2026. GS1 US reserves the right to change pricing.* <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-13"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-14"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-15"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-16"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-17"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-23"/>

## Count your variations before you buy

Each variation of each product you sell requires a unique barcode. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-20"/> A T-shirt offered in three sizes and three colors is nine sellable items, not one. A kitchen set sold as a 3-piece and a 5-piece is two.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): five-year cost for 8 vs 40 items">
Inputs: published GS1 US fees; one seller has 8 variations, another has 40; both keep them for five years.

- **8 items, single GTINs:** 8 × $30 = $240, with no renewals. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-07"/> <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-08"/>
- **8 items, 10-item prefix:** $250 + 4 renewals × $50 = $450 over five years. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-13"/>
- **40 items, single GTINs:** 40 × $30 = $1,200. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-07"/>
- **40 items, 100-item prefix:** $750 + 4 × $150 = $1,350 over five years, with 60 numbers left for new products. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-14"/>

The renewal count assumes the first year is covered by the initial fee and renewals are billed in years two through five. On these inputs, single GTINs cost less in both cases; the prefix earns its place when you need cases, pallets, locations, or room to grow quickly. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-11"/>
</Callout>

### Capacity is a ceiling, not a target

Prefix capacity is the maximum number of unique products you can identify, so the question is where your catalog will be, not where it is today. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-12"/> A seller with 12 variations has already outgrown a 10-item prefix and would need the 100-item tier at $750 plus $150 a year. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-14"/> The same 12 variations as single GTINs cost 12 × $30 = $360 once (a hypothetical calculation from the published fees). <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-07"/> Single GTINs stay cheaper until the catalog is large enough, or the need for case and pallet codes is real enough, to justify the yearly renewal.

### Cartons and shipping units

If you ship master cartons to a warehouse or a retail buyer, the item-level UPC on each unit is not the only identifier in play. GS1 US describes GTIN-14 as the format used to identify trade item groupings, <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-04"/> and a Company Prefix — not a single GTIN — is what lets you create identifiers for higher packaging levels such as a case or pallet. <C id="gs1-barcodes-fnsku-11"/>

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| One barcode for all sizes or colors | Each variation requires its own unique barcode |
| Buying a prefix far larger than needed | A 1,000-item prefix renews at $500 a year versus $50 for 10 items |
| Letting a prefix membership lapse | Product data stays linked to your company only while you renew yearly |
| Planning case-pack barcodes on single GTINs | Single GTINs are not available for mixed cases |
