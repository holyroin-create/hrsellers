On Amazon, the fees on a low-priced item do not fall in proportion to its price. FBA fulfillment is charged per unit and based on the product's weights and dimensions, and referral fees are a percentage of the price or a minimum amount, whichever is greater, so an $8 item can lose a far larger share of its price to fees than a $24 item that is the same size. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-01"/> <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-04"/> Before you launch or reprice a cheap item, work out the fee share at your target price, not just the fee amount.

## The per-unit charges in Amazon's fee structure

Amazon says FBA costs depend on the products you sell and the services you use. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-03"/> Three charges behave like fixed amounts per sale:

| Charge | How Amazon describes it |
|---|---|
| FBA fulfillment | Picking and packing, shipping and handling, customer service and product returns; based on weights and dimensions |
| Referral fee | A percentage of the total price or a minimum amount, whichever is greater |
| Individual selling plan | $0.99 per item sold (the Professional plan is $39.99 a month instead) |

*Source: Amazon, sell.amazon.com "Fulfillment by Amazon" and "Pricing," checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-02"/> <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-01"/> <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-04"/> <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-07"/> <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-08"/>

Storage is separate: it is charged monthly based on daily average volume in cubic feet, <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-15"/> so it scales with how long and how bulky the stock is, not with the price. The full referral fee schedule is in the [referral fee guide](/guides/amazon-referral-fees/).

### How the referral fee scales

In Home and Kitchen, for example, the rate is 15% with a $0.30 minimum, <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-05"/> so the minimum applies only below $2.00 (15% × $2.00 = $0.30) and the percentage sets the fee above that. Some categories step the rate down for cheap items instead: Beauty, Health and Personal Care charges 8% on products with a total sales price of $10.00 or less and 15% above that, with a $0.30 minimum. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-06"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): the $10.00 threshold">
Inputs: a Beauty, Health and Personal Care item priced at $10.00, then repriced to $10.50; published rates of 8% and 15%.

A beauty item at $10.00 pays 8% × $10.00 = $0.80 in referral fees; raise it to $10.50 and the rate becomes 15%, so the fee is $1.58 (rounded). A 50-cent price rise costs $0.78 more in referral fees and nets the seller less than before, before the fulfillment fee is even counted. For low-priced items in tiered categories, check which side of the threshold your price sits on before moving it. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-06"/>
</Callout>

## What changed for 2026

Amazon announced on October 15, 2025 that FBA fees would increase by an average of $0.08 per unit sold, which it put at less than 0.5% of an average item's selling price. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-09"/> Unless otherwise noted, the changes took effect January 15, 2026. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-10"/> Amazon said it added more granularity to the fee structure to offer lower fees where its costs are lower. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-11"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): the same $0.08 at two prices">
Inputs: the published $0.08 average per-unit increase applied to a $5.00 item and a $25.00 item. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-09"/>

$0.08 ÷ $5.00 = 1.6% of the price; $0.08 ÷ $25.00 = 0.32%. The same dollar amount weighs five times as heavily on the cheaper item.
</Callout>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): the same product at $8 and at $24">
Inputs: a small Home and Kitchen item; referral fee 15% with a $0.30 minimum <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-05"/>; an assumed FBA fulfillment fee of $3.50 per unit, used only for illustration (get the real figure for your product from Amazon's Revenue Calculator <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-13"/>); Professional plan, so no per-item plan fee.

| | Sold at $8.00 | Sold as a 3-pack at $24.00 |
|---|---|---|
| Referral fee (15%) | $1.20 | $3.60 |
| FBA fulfillment (assumed) | $3.50 | $4.25 (assumed for the larger, heavier pack) |
| Total Amazon fees | $4.70 | $7.85 |
| Fees as share of price | 58.8% | 32.7% |
| Fees per item inside | $4.70 | $2.62 |

The referral fee scales with price, but the fulfillment fee does not, so it dominates the cheap sale. Selling three units as one pack spreads one fulfillment fee over three items.
</Callout>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): Individual vs Professional plan">
Inputs: $0.99 per item on the Individual plan versus $39.99 a month on the Professional plan. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-07"/> <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-08"/>

$39.99 ÷ $0.99 ≈ 40.4, so below about 40 items a month the per-item plan fee costs less in plan fees alone.

Multipacks bring their own labeling and barcode rules, covered in [bundles and multipacks](/guides/bundles-and-multipacks/); smaller packaging is covered in [packaging design for FBA](/guides/packaging-design-fba/); and the SIPP discount table is in the [SIPP guide](/guides/sipp-packaging-program/). Note how small SIPP's discount is at the light end: $0.04 per unit for small standard items of 4 oz or less in MCF and Buy with Prime orders. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-14"/> For a cheap item it helps, but it does not change the structure.

## How to confirm the fees for your product

Re-check the referral rates and plan prices on sell.amazon.com/pricing, <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-04"/> the FBA cost description on sell.amazon.com/fulfillment-by-amazon, <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-01"/> and Amazon's "Update to U.S. Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon fees for 2026" on sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com, <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-09"/> then run your product through the Revenue Calculator. <C id="fbt-fixed-dollar-problem-13"/>
