A $3.57 fulfillment fee is 9% of a $40 sale and 24% of a $15 one. Percentages are polite: they take more only when you earn more. Flat fees are not. Fulfilled by TikTok charges in dollars per unit, and that single design choice quietly decides which products can live on the channel at all — a decision that got stricter in August when the [referral fee moved to 8%](/guides/tiktok-shop-fee-increase-2026/).

Add the percentage rail on top — 8% referral plus a creator commission you set at 10-30% — and a $15 single-unit order can surrender half its value before cost of goods. The same stack on a $45 bundle is survivable. Nothing about the product changed; the denominator did.

## The one lever, and the rule

<MiniCalc variant="margin" />

Because FBT's per-unit rate will fall as basket size grows, **bundling is margin engineering** on this channel, not merchandising garnish: multi-packs, kit-plus-refill offers, tiered quantity pricing in the creator brief. The full fee context lives on our [TikTok Shop fee page](/marketplaces/tiktok-shop/), and the SKU rule is blunt: run every candidate through the [margin calculator](/tools/margin-calculator/) at the full stack and your realistic basket profile. A product that only clears as a sub-$20 single is not a TikTok product — better to learn that on paper than in a warehouse.
