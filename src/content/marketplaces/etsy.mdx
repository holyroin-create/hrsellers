A US seller on Etsy pays three fees on a typical sale: a $0.20 listing fee <C id="marketplace-etsy-02" />, a 6.5% transaction fee on the displayed price <C id="marketplace-etsy-01" /> plus shipping and gift wrapping <C id="marketplace-etsy-03" />, and a payment processing fee of 3% + $0.25 for a US bank account <C id="marketplace-etsy-04" />. A fourth fee, Offsite Ads, applies only to orders Etsy attributes to its external advertising, at 15% or 12% <C id="marketplace-etsy-05" />.

The base for each fee is slightly different, which is the main thing to get right when pricing.

## Fee table for US sellers

| Fee | Rate | Charged on | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 per item listed | Each listing; listings expire after four months | <C id="marketplace-etsy-02" /> <C id="marketplace-etsy-06" /> |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% | Displayed price plus shipping and gift wrapping; not sales tax for US sellers | <C id="marketplace-etsy-01" /> <C id="marketplace-etsy-03" /> <C id="marketplace-etsy-07" /> |
| Payment processing (US bank account) | 3% + $0.25 | Gross order amount, including shipping and tax | <C id="marketplace-etsy-04" /> <C id="marketplace-etsy-08" /> |
| Offsite Ads, standard | 15% | Attributed orders only | <C id="marketplace-etsy-05" /> |
| Offsite Ads, shops at $10,000+ trailing sales | 12% | Attributed orders only | <C id="marketplace-etsy-09" /> |
| Offsite Ads cap | $100 per attributed order | Each attributed order | <C id="marketplace-etsy-10" /> |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% | Sale amount, when listing currency differs from payment account currency | <C id="marketplace-etsy-11" /> |

*Source: Etsy, Fees & Payments Policy and Etsy Payments Policy, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-etsy-12" />

Payment processing rates differ by country because they vary with the location of your bank account <C id="marketplace-etsy-12" />; the 3% + $0.25 figure is the US rate.

## The three fee bases

- **Transaction fee:** the displayed listing price plus what you charge for shipping and gift wrapping <C id="marketplace-etsy-03" />. For US sellers, sales tax is excluded <C id="marketplace-etsy-07" />.
- **Payment processing:** the gross order amount, including shipping and tax <C id="marketplace-etsy-08" />. Unlike the transaction fee, this one is charged on the sales tax the buyer pays.
- **Offsite Ads:** the total order amount, meaning the displayed price plus shipping and gift wrapping <C id="marketplace-etsy-13" />; for US sellers it does not apply to sales tax <C id="marketplace-etsy-14" />.

## Offsite Ads in brief

Etsy promotes listings on external sites and charges a fee only when that advertising directly results in a sale <C id="marketplace-etsy-15" />. An order counts when it is placed within 30 days of a click on an Offsite Ad <C id="marketplace-etsy-16" />. Shops under $10,000 in sales over the prior 365 days pay 15% and may opt out <C id="marketplace-etsy-17" />; shops at or above that level must participate at 12% <C id="marketplace-etsy-09" />. The fee per order never exceeds $100 <C id="marketplace-etsy-10" />.

The cost per order at different price points, and how the opt-out works, are covered in [Etsy Offsite Ads economics](/guides/etsy-offsite-ads-economics/).

## Other fees

- **Regulatory Operating fee:** sellers in certain countries pay this on each transaction <C id="marketplace-etsy-18" />. Etsy's fee policy does not list the countries or rates on that page; it links to a separate help article.
- **Currency conversion:** 2.5% when you list in a currency other than your payment account's currency <C id="marketplace-etsy-11" />.

## Worked example: a hypothetical $25 item

Assumptions (hypothetical): US seller with a US bank account, one item listed once at $25.00, no gift wrap, no sales tax collected unless stated.

| Scenario | Listing | Transaction (6.5%) | Processing (3% + $0.25) | Offsite Ads | Total | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $25.00, free shipping, no ad | $0.20 | $1.63 | $1.00 | none | $2.83 | <C id="marketplace-etsy-01" /> |
| $25.00 + $5.00 shipping, no ad | $0.20 | $1.95 | $1.15 | none | $3.30 | <C id="marketplace-etsy-03" /> |
| $25.00, free shipping, $2.00 tax collected, no ad | $0.20 | $1.63 | $1.06 | none | $2.89 | <C id="marketplace-etsy-08" /> |
| $25.00, free shipping, Offsite Ad at 15% | $0.20 | $1.63 | $1.00 | $3.75 | $6.58 | <C id="marketplace-etsy-05" /> |
| $25.00, free shipping, Offsite Ad at 12% | $0.20 | $1.63 | $1.00 | $3.00 | $5.83 | <C id="marketplace-etsy-09" /> |

*Worked example using rates from Etsy, Fees & Payments Policy and Etsy Payments Policy, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-etsy-04" />

Working, first row: 6.5% × $25.00 = $1.625; 3% × $25.00 + $0.25 = $1.00; $0.20 + $1.625 + $1.00 = $2.825, shown rounded as $2.83. In the tax row, processing is 3% × $27.00 + $0.25 = $1.06, while the transaction fee stays at $1.63 because sales tax is excluded from it for US sellers.

If the item sits unsold past four months and renews, another $0.20 listing fee applies <C id="marketplace-etsy-06" />.

## How the effective rate changes with price

The $0.20 listing fee and the $0.25 fixed part of processing do not scale with price, so they weigh most on cheap items. Worked example (hypothetical): US seller, free shipping, no tax, no Offsite Ad, one listing fee per sale.

| Item price | Listing | Transaction (6.5%) | Processing (3% + $0.25) | Total | Fee as % of price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $10.00 | $0.20 | $0.65 | $0.55 | $1.40 | 14.0% |
| $25.00 | $0.20 | $1.63 | $1.00 | $2.83 | 11.3% |
| $50.00 | $0.20 | $3.25 | $1.75 | $5.20 | 10.4% |
| $100.00 | $0.20 | $6.50 | $3.25 | $9.95 | 9.95% |

*Worked example using rates from Etsy, Fees & Payments Policy and Etsy Payments Policy, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-etsy-02" />

The percentages converge toward 9.5% (6.5% transaction plus 3% processing) as price rises, because the fixed $0.45 matters less. Shipping charged to the buyer raises the transaction and processing amounts in dollars <C id="marketplace-etsy-03" /> <C id="marketplace-etsy-08" />, but not as a share of the order total.

For a listing with several units in stock, the $0.20 applies per item listed <C id="marketplace-etsy-02" />; the table assumes one listing fee per sale.
