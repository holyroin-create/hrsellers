For a US seller, Etsy's standard costs on a sale are a **$0.20 listing fee** per item listed <C id="marketplace-etsy-01"/>, a **6.5% transaction fee** on the price you display <C id="marketplace-etsy-04"/>, and a **payment processing fee of 3% + $0.25** <C id="marketplace-etsy-07"/>. If the buyer arrived through an Offsite Ad, a further 15% applies, or 12% once your shop has passed $10,000 in sales over the prior 365 days <C id="marketplace-etsy-09"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-10"/>.

## Etsy's fee schedule for US sellers

| Fee | Rate | What it is charged on |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 USD per item listed <C id="marketplace-etsy-01"/> | Each listing; listings expire after four months <C id="marketplace-etsy-02"/> |
| Multi-quantity renewal | $0.20 after each item sells <C id="marketplace-etsy-03"/> | Listings with more than one unit |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% <C id="marketplace-etsy-04"/> | Displayed price plus shipping and gift wrapping <C id="marketplace-etsy-05"/>; not sales tax for US sellers <C id="marketplace-etsy-06"/> |
| Payment processing (US bank account) | 3% + 0.25 USD <C id="marketplace-etsy-07"/> | Gross order amount, including shipping and tax if applicable <C id="marketplace-etsy-08"/> |
| Offsite Ads | 15%, or 12% at $10,000+ in prior-365-day sales <C id="marketplace-etsy-09"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-10"/> | Total order amount: price plus shipping and gift wrapping, and taxes in some jurisdictions <C id="marketplace-etsy-15"/>; capped at $100 per order <C id="marketplace-etsy-13"/> |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% <C id="marketplace-etsy-16"/> | Sale amount, when you list in a currency other than your payment account's |
| Etsy Plus | $10 USD per month <C id="marketplace-etsy-17"/> | Subscription |
| Pattern | $15.00 USD per month after a 30-day free trial <C id="marketplace-etsy-18"/> | Subscription |

*Source: Etsy Fees & Payments Policy (last updated Oct 5, 2026) and Etsy Payments Policy, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-etsy-19"/>

One detail that trips up new shops: a private listing is charged its listing fee only when it sells <C id="marketplace-etsy-20"/>.

## Two fee bases, not one

The transaction fee and the processing fee look like they apply to the same number, but they don't. The transaction fee covers the displayed price plus shipping and gift wrapping, and for US sellers it excludes sales tax <C id="marketplace-etsy-05"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-06"/>. The processing fee is assessed on the gross order amount, including shipping and tax if applicable <C id="marketplace-etsy-08"/>. So the tax a buyer pays raises your processing fee but not your transaction fee.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): sales tax on a $100 order">
A $100 item with free shipping and $8.00 of sales tax. Transaction fee: 6.5% × $100 = $6.50 (tax excluded). Processing: 3% × $108 + $0.25 = $3.49. With the $0.20 listing fee, total Etsy fees are $10.19, versus $9.95 on the same order without tax. <C id="marketplace-etsy-06"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-08"/>
</Callout>

## Worked example (hypothetical): a $25 sale and a $100 sale

Inputs: US seller with a US bank account, one unit, free shipping, no sales tax, listed in US dollars, listing created for this sale.

| Fee | $25 sale | $100 sale |
|---|---|---|
| Listing fee | $0.20 | $0.20 |
| Transaction fee (6.5%) | $1.63 | $6.50 |
| Payment processing (3% + $0.25) | $1.00 | $3.25 |
| **Base fees total** | **$2.83** | **$9.95** |
| Add if Offsite Ads at 15% | +$3.75 → $6.58 | +$15.00 → $24.95 |
| Add if Offsite Ads at 12% | +$3.00 → $5.83 | +$12.00 → $21.95 |

*Source: rates from Etsy's Fees & Payments Policy and Etsy Payments Policy, checked Nov 8, 2026; arithmetic is ours (the $25 transaction fee is $1.625, shown rounded).* <C id="marketplace-etsy-01"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-04"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-07"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-09"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-10"/>

Note what is missing from the table: no category column. The same 6.5% transaction fee and the same US processing rate apply whether the order is a print, a mug or a piece of furniture, so the only levers on your Etsy fee rate are price, shipping charges, listing count and Offsite Ads <C id="marketplace-etsy-04"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-07"/>. That makes Etsy fees easier to model than Amazon or Walmart referral tables, but it also means there is no lower-rate category to list into.

The base fees are about 11.3% of a $25 sale and 9.95% of a $100 sale, because the fixed $0.20 listing fee and $0.25 processing charge weigh more on cheap items. An Offsite Ads order more than doubles the bill at either price.

The $100 Offsite Ads cap starts to bite on large orders: at 15%, an order total above $666.67 would hit the cap (hypothetical arithmetic) <C id="marketplace-etsy-13"/>.

## Should you stay in Offsite Ads?

Whether you have a choice depends on your trailing sales. Shops under $10,000 over the prior 365 days can opt out for as long as they stay under that threshold <C id="marketplace-etsy-11"/>. Once a shop reaches $10,000, the earlier opt-out no longer applies <C id="marketplace-etsy-12"/>, and the rate drops to 12% <C id="marketplace-etsy-10"/>.

## Common Etsy fee mistakes

| Mistake | What Etsy's policies say | Effect on a hypothetical order |
|---|---|---|
| Charging the transaction fee on price only | It also applies to shipping and gift wrapping <C id="marketplace-etsy-05"/> | A $30 item with $6 shipping pays 6.5% of $36: $2.34, not $1.95 |
| Counting the listing fee once per listing | Multi-quantity listings renew at $0.20 after each item sells <C id="marketplace-etsy-03"/> | Selling 10 units from one listing costs $2.00 in renewals plus the original $0.20 |
| Forgetting listings expire | Listings expire after four months <C id="marketplace-etsy-02"/> | Keeping one slow item live for a year means three $0.20 listing periods |
| Listing in a foreign currency by habit | 2.5% currency conversion fee on the sale amount <C id="marketplace-etsy-16"/> | $2.50 extra on a $100 sale |
| Treating Offsite Ads as optional at any size | The opt-out ends once sales reach $10,000 in 365 days <C id="marketplace-etsy-12"/> | Attributed orders then pay 12% |

Small items are where Etsy's fixed charges matter most. On a hypothetical $5 sale with free shipping, the $0.20 listing fee, $0.33 transaction fee (6.5% of $5, rounded) and $0.40 processing fee (3% of $5 plus $0.25) total about $0.93, nearly 19% of the price <C id="marketplace-etsy-01"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-04"/> <C id="marketplace-etsy-07"/>. At $50 the same three fees total $5.20, or 10.4%. If your catalog leans toward low-priced items, bundling them into one listing at a higher price is the most direct lever on that ratio, since the fixed parts are charged per listing and per order rather than as a share of value.
