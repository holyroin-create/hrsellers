Walmart Marketplace charges no subscription. Walmart states the marketplace operates with zero setup, monthly, or hidden fees <C id="marketplace-walmart-01" />, and referral fees are deducted only once you complete a sale <C id="marketplace-walmart-02" />. The referral fee is a percentage of the total sales price, set by category: 15% in most general merchandise categories, lower for electronics, computers and appliances, and tiered for a handful of categories.

If you use Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS), fulfillment and storage fees come on top, and Walmart publishes those rates by shipping weight.

## How the referral fee is calculated

- **Base:** the total sales price, which includes item price plus shipping and handling, gift wrap and other charges <C id="marketplace-walmart-03" />.
- **Timing:** fees are deducted only once a sale is completed <C id="marketplace-walmart-02" />.
- **Table layout:** the referral fee table has two columns, Product Category and Referral Fee Percentage <C id="marketplace-walmart-49" />.

Two tier structures appear in the table. Apparel, Baby, Beauty, Grocery and Outdoor Power Tools switch rate based on which band **the whole item's** total sales price falls into ("for items with a total sales price of..."). Appliances - Compact, Electronics Accessories, Furniture, Jewelry and Watches apply each rate only to **the portion** of the price inside that band.

## Where tier boundaries bite

Worked example (hypothetical), seller-fulfilled, no shipping charged:

- **Apparel at $20.00 vs $21.00:** $20.00 falls in the 10% band, a $2.00 fee; $21.00 falls in the 15% band, a $3.15 fee <C id="marketplace-walmart-05" />. One dollar more in price costs $1.15 more in fee.
- **Baby Products at $10.00 vs $11.00:** 8% of $10.00 is $0.80; 15% of $11.00 is $1.65 <C id="marketplace-walmart-09" />.
- **Jewelry at $300.00:** 20% of the first $250 ($50.00) plus 5% of the remaining $50 ($2.50), a $52.50 fee <C id="marketplace-walmart-20" />. Because this tier is marginal, there is no jump at the boundary.

Since the total sales price includes shipping and handling <C id="marketplace-walmart-03" />, a shipping charge can move an item across a whole-price band: a $19.00 apparel item with $3.00 shipping is a $22.00 sale at 15%.

## Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS)

Walmart states you won't find setup, monthly or hidden fees with WFS <C id="marketplace-walmart-36" />. WFS accepts products up to 500 lbs. with maximum dimensions of 120 x 105 x 93 inches, including packaging <C id="marketplace-walmart-37" />.

### Standard fulfillment fees (per unit, by shipping weight)

| Shipping weight | Fee | |
|---|---|---|
| 1 lb. or less | $3.45 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-38" /> |
| 2 lb. | $4.95 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-39" /> |
| 3 lb. | $5.45 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-40" /> |
| 4 to 20 lb. | $5.75 plus $0.40 for each lb. over 4 lb. | <C id="marketplace-walmart-41" /> |

*Source: Walmart Marketplace, WFS pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-walmart-42" />

Heavier tiers, oversize surcharges and the big and bulky rate are listed on the same WFS pricing page.

### Surcharges added to the fulfillment fee

| Condition | Add | |
|---|---|---|
| Apparel | $0.50 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-43" /> |
| Hazardous materials | $0.50 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-44" /> |

*Source: Walmart Marketplace, WFS pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-walmart-42" />

### Storage

- January through September: $0.75 per cubic foot per month <C id="marketplace-walmart-46" />.
- October through December: items stored more than 30 days add $1.50 per cubic foot per month <C id="marketplace-walmart-47" />.
- Long-term: $2.25 per cubic foot per month at 366 to 450 days, and $7.50 beyond 450 days <C id="marketplace-walmart-48" />.

Walmart notes these fees are subject to change <C id="marketplace-walmart-42" />.

## Worked example: a hypothetical $25 item

Assumptions (hypothetical): one unit sold at $25.00 total sales price ($0 shipping charged), shipping weight 1 lb. or less, sold in the stated category.

| Scenario | Referral fee | WFS fulfillment | Total | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden, seller-fulfilled | 15% × $25.00 = $3.75 | none | $3.75 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-17" /> |
| Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden, WFS | $3.75 | $3.45 | $7.20 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-38" /> |
| Apparel & Accessories (over $20 band), WFS | 15% × $25.00 = $3.75 | $3.45 + $0.50 = $3.95 | $7.70 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-43" /> |
| Consumer Electronics, seller-fulfilled | 8% × $25.00 = $2.00 | none | $2.00 | <C id="marketplace-walmart-14" /> |

*Worked example using rates from Walmart Marketplace referral fee and WFS pricing pages, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-walmart-05" />

The WFS rows exclude storage. For a unit occupying 0.1 cubic feet for one month between January and September, storage would add $0.075 at the $0.75 rate.

The under-$10 surcharge is worth noting for low-priced items: a $9 item weighing under 1 lb. would pay $3.45 plus $1.00 in WFS fulfillment <C id="marketplace-walmart-45" />, before any referral fee.
