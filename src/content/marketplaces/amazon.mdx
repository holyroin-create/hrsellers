Selling on Amazon in the US costs a plan fee plus a per-sale referral fee. The Professional plan is $39.99 per month plus selling fees <C id="marketplace-amazon-01" />; the Individual plan costs $0.99 per item sold <C id="marketplace-amazon-02" />. On top of either plan, Amazon takes a referral fee on every item: a percentage of the total price or a minimum amount, whichever is greater <C id="marketplace-amazon-03" />.

The referral percentage depends on the product's fee category, and most categories land at 8%, 12% or 15% with a $0.30 minimum. Media adds a closing fee. Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) is a separate set of costs on top of all of this.

## Selling plans

| Plan | Cost | |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | $0.99 per item sold | <C id="marketplace-amazon-02" /> |
| Professional | $39.99 per month plus selling fees | <C id="marketplace-amazon-01" /> |

*Source: Amazon, Sell on Amazon: Pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-amazon-01" />

The break-even between the two is simple arithmetic: $39.99 divided by $0.99 is about 40.4, so above roughly 40 items a month the Professional plan costs less in plan fees. Amazon states you can switch or cancel your selling plan at any time after registration <C id="marketplace-amazon-04" />.

## How the referral fee is calculated

Three rules decide the number:

1. **The base is the total price.** Amazon's total price includes the list price plus shipping costs and any gift-wrapping charges <C id="marketplace-amazon-05" />. A $20 item with $5 shipping is charged on $25.
2. **The greater of percentage or minimum.** If the percentage works out below the category minimum, you pay the minimum <C id="marketplace-amazon-03" />. At 15% with a $0.30 minimum, the minimum only binds on total prices below $2.00.
3. **Fee category, not storefront category.** Amazon notes that a product's fee category may not match the category customers see in the store <C id="marketplace-amazon-06" />. The Everything Else category is meant for products that do not clearly fit an existing category <C id="marketplace-amazon-07" />.

Read the tiered rows carefully, because they use two different structures. Some tiers apply to **the whole price** depending on which band it falls in ("for products with a total sales price of..."), such as Clothing and Accessories, Baby Products and Beauty, Health, and Personal Care. Others apply only to **the portion** of the price inside each band ("for the portion of the total sales price up to..."), such as Jewelry, Furniture, Electronics Accessories and Watches.

## Closing fee

The only closing fee on the pricing page applies to media: books, DVD, music, software and video. In addition to the referral fee, media items incur a closing fee of $1.80 per item <C id="marketplace-amazon-46" />.

## FBA costs: what sell.amazon.com shows

The public FBA page does not publish a per-unit fee schedule; it describes the cost structure and points to Seller Central for rates. What it does state:

- FBA costs depend on the products you sell and the services you use; the fulfillment costs are based on the product's price, weight and dimensions <C id="marketplace-amazon-47" />.
- Storage is charged monthly based on the space your inventory occupies, calculated on daily average volume in cubic feet <C id="marketplace-amazon-48" />.
- An aged inventory cost is charged monthly for items stored in a fulfillment center for more than 181 days <C id="marketplace-amazon-49" />.
- A returns processing cost applies on orders where Amazon gives the customer free return shipping <C id="marketplace-amazon-50" />.
- Removal (return, disposal or liquidation of inventory) is charged per item <C id="marketplace-amazon-51" />.
- Inbound placement, for standard and large bulky products, covers distributing inventory to fulfillment centers close to customers <C id="marketplace-amazon-52" />.

Because the per-unit FBA rates are only published inside Seller Central, this page does not quote them.

## Worked example: a hypothetical $25 item

Assumptions (hypothetical): one unit sold at a $25.00 list price, $0 shipping charged to the buyer, no gift wrap, seller-fulfilled so no FBA costs.

| Scenario | Referral fee | Other per-item fee | Total Amazon fee | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home and Kitchen, Professional plan | 15% × $25.00 = $3.75 | none | $3.75 | <C id="marketplace-amazon-27" /> |
| Home and Kitchen, Individual plan | $3.75 | $0.99 per item | $4.74 | <C id="marketplace-amazon-02" /> |
| Clothing and Accessories (over $20 band) | 17% × $25.00 = $4.25 | none | $4.25 | <C id="marketplace-amazon-17" /> |
| Consumer Electronics | 8% × $25.00 = $2.00 | none | $2.00 | <C id="marketplace-amazon-19" /> |
| Media (a $25 book) | 15% × $25.00 = $3.75 | $1.80 closing fee | $5.55 | <C id="marketplace-amazon-46" /> |

*Worked example using rates from Amazon, Sell on Amazon: Pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-amazon-03" />

In every row the percentage result is above the $0.30 minimum, so the minimum does not apply. On the Professional plan, the $39.99 monthly fee is fixed: spread over 100 units a month it adds about $0.40 per unit; over 1,000 units, about $0.04.

If the same Home and Kitchen item were listed at $20.00 with $5.00 shipping, the referral fee would still be $3.75, because shipping is part of the total price.
