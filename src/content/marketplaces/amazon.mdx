Amazon takes a **referral fee** on every item you sell, a percentage of the total price set by product category, or a per-item minimum if that is larger <C id="marketplace-amazon-03"/>. In Everything Else and many other categories the rate is 15% with a $0.30 minimum <C id="marketplace-amazon-15"/>. On top of that you pay a plan fee: $0.99 per item sold on the Individual plan <C id="marketplace-amazon-01"/>, or $39.99 per month on the Professional plan <C id="marketplace-amazon-02"/>. Media items (books, DVDs, music, software, video) add a $1.80 closing fee per item <C id="marketplace-amazon-04"/>. The full table, two worked examples and the plan break-even are below.

## Which referral fee applies to your item

The table mixes three structures, and reading the wrong one is an easy way to misprice a listing. Amazon's wording separates them: a band phrased "for products with a total sales price of…" sets one rate for the whole item, while a band phrased "for the portion of the total sales price…" splits the price into slices <C id="marketplace-amazon-05"/> <C id="marketplace-amazon-14"/>.

  title="Find the rule that sets your referral fee"

    { if: "Your category is Clothing, Beauty, Baby, Grocery, or Lawn Mowers", then: "Find the band your total sales price falls in and apply that one rate to the whole price", claim: "marketplace-amazon-05" },
    { if: "Your category is Furniture, Jewelry, Watches, Electronics Accessories, Compact Appliances, 3D Printers, or Fine Art", then: "Apply the higher rate to the price up to the threshold and the lower rate only to the amount above it", claim: "marketplace-amazon-14" },
    { if: "You sell books, DVDs, music, software or video", then: "Add the $1.80 closing fee to the 15% referral fee", claim: "marketplace-amazon-04" },
    { if: "You sell Grocery, Gift Cards, Media, Video Games or Consoles", then: "The table lists no minimum, so very cheap items pay the percentage only", claim: "marketplace-amazon-16" }

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): one cent changes the clothing fee">
A shirt with a total sales price of $20.00 falls in the 10% band, so the referral fee is $2.00. At $20.01 the whole price moves to the 17% band and the fee becomes $3.40 (17% × $20.01 = $3.4017). By contrast, a $300 table in Furniture pays 15% on the first $200 ($30.00) plus 10% on the remaining $100 ($10.00), for $40.00, not 15% of $300 ($45.00). <C id="marketplace-amazon-05"/> <C id="marketplace-amazon-14"/>
</Callout>

## Worked example (hypothetical): a $25 item and a $100 item

Inputs: Professional plan, a single unit, total sales price equal to the item price (no shipping or gift wrap charged), no FBA. Add $0.99 per item if you are on the Individual plan <C id="marketplace-amazon-01"/>.

| Category (rule) | Fee on a $25 sale | Fee on a $100 sale |
|---|---|---|
| Home and Kitchen (15%) | $3.75 | $15.00 |
| Clothing and Accessories (whole price above $20: 17%) | $4.25 | $17.00 |
| Beauty, Health, and Personal Care (above $10: 15%) | $3.75 | $15.00 |
| Consumer Electronics (8%) | $2.00 | $8.00 |
| Electronics Accessories (15% on the portion up to $100) | $3.75 | $15.00 |
| Jewelry (20% on the portion up to $250) | $5.00 | $20.00 |
| Media, e.g. a book (15% + $1.80 closing fee) | $5.55 | $16.80 |

*Source: rates from Amazon's Sell on Amazon pricing page, checked Nov 8, 2026; arithmetic is ours.* <C id="marketplace-amazon-06"/> <C id="marketplace-amazon-05"/> <C id="marketplace-amazon-07"/> <C id="marketplace-amazon-11"/> <C id="marketplace-amazon-12"/> <C id="marketplace-amazon-13"/> <C id="marketplace-amazon-34"/>

Two patterns stand out. At $25, the same price can cost anywhere from $2.00 to $5.55 in referral and closing fees depending only on the category you list in. At $100, the Electronics Accessories tier has not yet kicked in, because the 8% rate only applies to the portion above $100 <C id="marketplace-amazon-12"/>; a $150 accessory would pay $15.00 on the first $100 plus $4.00 on the next $50, or $19.00.

The minimum matters only for very cheap items. At 15%, an item has to sell for less than $2.00 before the $0.30 floor takes over: a $1.50 item would owe $0.225 at 15%, so the fee rounds up to the $0.30 minimum (hypothetical arithmetic) <C id="marketplace-amazon-15"/>.

## Individual or Professional: the break-even

The two plans share the same referral fee table; what differs is how you pay for access. The Individual plan charges $0.99 per item sold <C id="marketplace-amazon-01"/>, and the Professional plan charges a flat $39.99 per month plus selling fees <C id="marketplace-amazon-02"/>.

Amazon describes the Professional plan as giving access to "hundreds of tools and programs" for the monthly fee <C id="marketplace-amazon-44"/>, so the break-even above is a floor for the decision, not the whole decision. This page compares only the published fee amounts.
