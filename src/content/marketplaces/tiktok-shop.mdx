TikTok Shop's US seller portal sums up the cost of entry in one line: **no setup fee, and a 6% commission on each sale** <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-01"/>. The harder planning question for most sellers is cash flow, because TikTok pays out after delivery and can hold part of each delivered order in reserve for 30 days <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-08"/> <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-09"/>. This page covers eligibility, signup, payouts, reserves and the contract. The fee arithmetic — fee base, category rates, refund fee — is in the [TikTok Shop fees guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-fees/).

## Who can sell

Businesses, brands and individual sellers can apply, as long as they are in a supported market and complete TikTok's verification <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-02"/>. Registration is per country: TikTok Shop operates locally in each market, so selling in several countries means signing up separately for each <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-03"/>.

## What it costs

The headline is the 6% commission per sale <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-01"/>, which TikTok's policy pages call the referral fee. When an order is refunded, TikTok keeps a Refund Administration Fee of 20% of that referral fee <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-20"/>. The portal also promotes referral fee reductions of up to 4% for sellers using LIVE auctions <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-19"/>; the offer is time-limited <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-21"/>, and the related 2026 LIVE Auction rate policy is covered in [TikTok Shop fee changes in 2026](/guides/tiktok-shop-fees/). Category exceptions and worked examples are in the [fees guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-fees/).

## How and when you get paid

TikTok pays to your linked bank account after orders are completed and any return or refund windows have closed <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-07"/>. The detailed rules sit in the Dynamic Settlement and Reserve Policy, which applies to all sellers on TikTok Shop <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-13"/>.

| Element | What the policy says |
|---|---|
| Trigger for payout | Earnings minus any reserves are paid out based on successful order delivery <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-08"/> |
| Reserve | A portion of earnings for each delivered order is retained for 30 days from the delivery date <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-09"/> |
| Release | Reserve funds not needed are released after 30 days <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-10"/> |
| Review cycle | Settlement tier and reserve levels are set in evaluations on the 1st day of every month <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-11"/> |
| What is evaluated | Shop Performance Score, orders delivered in the previous 15–45 days, and temporary account restrictions <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-12"/> |

*Source: TikTok Shop Academy, Dynamic Settlement and Reserve Policy (dated 07/31/2026), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-13"/>

The evaluation inputs are operational: performance score, recent delivered orders and any temporary account restrictions <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-12"/>.

## How refunds flow through your payouts

Refunds affect your money in three places. First, timing: the portal ties payout to orders being completed and return or refund windows closing <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-07"/>, so an order still inside its return window is not yet money you can spend. Second, fees: when a refund happens, TikTok returns the referral fee minus a Refund Administration Fee of 20% of it <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-20"/>, so a refunded order still costs you something even after the buyer's money goes back. Third, offsets: amounts you owe can be offset against payouts <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-15"/>.

For a new shop, the practical step is to read your first few settlement statements order by order: check that each payout lines up with a delivery date, that reserved amounts reappear about 30 days later <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-10"/>, and that refunded orders show the retained 20% fee rather than the full referral fee.

## The contract you sign

The US Seller Terms of Service (last updated October 15, 2026) <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-18"/> are with BD TikTok USA LLC, which provides its services together with its affiliates <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-14"/>. Three payment clauses bear on cash flow:

- **Offset.** Payments due to you may be offset by amounts you owe <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-15"/>.
- **Reserve.** TikTok may establish and maintain a reserve on your account <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-16"/>.
- **Holds.** Payments to you may be frozen, delayed or withheld in accordance with applicable laws, including if you are in violation of the Seller Terms <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-17"/>.
