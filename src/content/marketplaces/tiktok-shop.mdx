TikTok Shop's US seller portal sums up the cost of entry in one line: **no setup fee, and a 6% commission on each sale** <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-01"/>. Beyond the commission, the published rules are operational and financial: orders must be scanned In Transit within 2 business days <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-24"/>, four fulfillment rates are scored against published targets, and TikTok pays out after delivery while holding part of each delivered order in reserve for 30 days <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-08"/> <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-09"/>. This page covers eligibility, signup, the shipping clock, returns, payouts, reserves and the contract. The fee arithmetic — fee base, category rates, refund fee — is in the [TikTok Shop fees guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-fees/).

## Who can sell

Businesses, brands and individual sellers can apply, as long as they are in a supported market and complete TikTok's verification <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-02"/>. Registration is per country: TikTok Shop operates locally in each market, so selling in several countries means signing up separately for each <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-03"/>.

## What it costs

The headline is the 6% commission per sale <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-01"/>, which TikTok's policy pages call the referral fee. When an order is refunded, TikTok keeps a Refund Administration Fee of 20% of that referral fee <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-20"/>. The portal also promotes referral fee reductions of up to 4% for sellers using LIVE auctions <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-19"/>; the offer is time-limited <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-21"/>, and the related 2026 LIVE Auction rate policy is covered in [TikTok Shop fee changes in 2026](/guides/tiktok-shop-fees/). Category exceptions and worked examples are in the [fees guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-fees/).

## The shipping clock: from Awaiting Shipment to Delivered

TikTok's Fulfillment Policy (dated 09/28/2026) <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-23"/> runs every deadline from the moment an order reaches **Awaiting Shipment**. For a regular order:

| Deadline (business days from Awaiting Shipment) | What must have happened | What if it doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Order marked In Transit, for example by a carrier scan <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-24"/> | Counts as a late dispatch <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-30"/> |
| 5 | Order updated to Awaiting Collection | TikTok Shop auto-cancels it <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-26"/> |
| 6 | Order marked Delivered <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-27"/> | Misses the policy's delivery deadline; on-time delivery is also scored as OTDR, below |

*Source: TikTok Shop Academy, Fulfillment Policy (dated 09/28/2026), checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-23"/>

Made-to-order, backorder and custom-handling orders get the handling time you set plus 1 business day to reach In Transit <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-25"/>.

## The four fulfillment rates and their targets

| Rate | Published target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Valid Tracking Rate (VTR) | 95% or higher <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-28"/> | FBT and TikTok Shipping orders are excluded <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-33"/> |
| Late Dispatch Rate (LDR) | 4% or lower recommended; enforcement may apply above 10% <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-29"/> | Late = not In Transit within 2 business days <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-30"/> |
| On-Time Delivery Rate (OTDR) | 80% or higher, calculated weekly Monday–Sunday <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-31"/> | FBT orders are excluded <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-33"/> |
| Seller-Fault Cancellation Rate (SFCR) | 2.5% or lower <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-32"/> | Cancelling for stock problems can raise it <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-41"/> |

*Source: TikTok Shop Academy, Fulfillment Policy and Customer Order Cancellation, Return, and Refund Policy, checked Nov 9, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-23"/>

Missing the targets has consequences: the policy lists enforcement that ranges from point deductions in Account Health Rating to limiting exposure of products with high negative feedback and capping order volume, up to suspending new orders <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-34"/>.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): reading your own LDR">
Inputs: 400 orders dispatched in the period you are reviewing; 20 of them reached In Transit on the third business day after Awaiting Shipment.

LDR = 20 ÷ 400 = 5%. That is above the 4% TikTok recommends but below the 10% level at which the policy says enforcement may apply <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-29"/>. Cutting late scans to 16 or fewer of the same 400 orders brings LDR to 4% (16 ÷ 400).
</Callout>

The policy makes sellers responsible for return-related costs for buyer-fault and seller-fault reasons. For buyer-fault reasons such as "No longer needed", its return-shipping table puts the cost on the seller when the customer chooses drop-off with platform-preferred logistics, and on the customer when the customer self-arranges return shipping <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-42"/>. For a product with a purchase price up to $10, Refund without Return is switched on automatically <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-39"/>.

## How and when you get paid

TikTok pays to your linked bank account after orders are completed and any return or refund windows have closed <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-07"/>. The detailed rules sit in the Dynamic Settlement and Reserve Policy, which applies to all sellers on TikTok Shop <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-13"/>.

| Element | What the policy says |
|---|---|
| Trigger for payout | Earnings minus any reserves are paid out based on successful order delivery <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-08"/> |
| Reserve | A portion of earnings for each delivered order is retained for 30 days from the delivery date <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-09"/> |
| Release | Reserve funds not needed are released after 30 days <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-10"/> |
| Review cycle | Settlement tier and reserve levels are set in evaluations on the 1st day of every month <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-11"/> |
| What is evaluated | Shop Performance Score, orders delivered in the previous 15–45 days, and temporary account restrictions <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-12"/> |

*Source: TikTok Shop Academy, Dynamic Settlement and Reserve Policy (dated 07/31/2026), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-13"/>

Because payout is based on successful order delivery <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-08"/>, the shipping deadlines above also set when an order's earnings can be paid out.

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): what a reserve does to month-one cash">
Inputs: $20,000 of orders delivered in your first month; an assumed reserve of 10% of each delivered order (an input chosen for illustration, not a TikTok figure).

Paid after delivery: $18,000 (before fees). Held: $2,000, released 30 days after each order's delivery date if not needed. If the next month delivers the same volume at the same reserve, a new $2,000 is held while the first is released, so about $2,000 of working capital stays tied up for as long as the reserve level holds. <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-09"/> <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-10"/>
</Callout>

## How refunds hit your settlement

Refunds affect your money in three places. First, timing: the portal ties payout to orders being completed and return or refund windows closing <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-07"/>, so an order still inside its return window is not yet money you can spend. Second, fees: when a refund happens, TikTok returns the referral fee minus a Refund Administration Fee of 20% of it <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-20"/>, so a refunded order still costs you something even after the buyer's money goes back. Third, offsets: amounts you owe can be offset against payouts <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-15"/>.

For a new shop, the practical step is to read your first few settlement statements order by order: check that each payout lines up with a delivery date, that reserved amounts reappear about 30 days later <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-10"/>, and that refunded orders show the retained 20% fee rather than the full referral fee.

## The contract you sign

The US Seller Terms of Service (last updated October 15, 2026) <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-18"/> are with BD TikTok USA LLC, which provides its services together with its affiliates <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-14"/>. Three payment clauses bear on cash flow:

- **Offset.** Payments due to you may be offset by amounts you owe <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-15"/>.
- **Reserve.** TikTok may establish and maintain a reserve on your account <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-16"/>.
- **Holds.** Payments to you may be frozen, delayed or withheld in accordance with applicable laws, including if you are in violation of the Seller Terms <C id="marketplace-tiktok-shop-17"/>.
