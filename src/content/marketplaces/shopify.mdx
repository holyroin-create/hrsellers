Shopify's pricing page lists a **monthly plan fee** plus **payment processing** on each card sale. Billed monthly, the plans cost $39 (Basic), $105 (Grow) and $399 (Advanced), and Plus starts at $2,300 <C id="marketplace-shopify-01"/>. Through Shopify Payments, a standard online card sale costs 2.9% + 30¢ on Basic, down to 2.25% + 30¢ on Plus <C id="marketplace-shopify-03"/>. If you use a third-party payment provider instead, Shopify adds its own transaction fee of 2% on Basic down to 0.2% on Plus <C id="marketplace-shopify-06"/> <C id="marketplace-shopify-07"/>. All figures are in US dollars as listed on Shopify's pricing page.

## Shopify plan and payment fees

| | Basic | Grow | Advanced | Plus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plan, billed monthly <C id="marketplace-shopify-01"/> | $39/mo | $105/mo | $399/mo | from $2,300/mo |
| Plan, billed yearly <C id="marketplace-shopify-02"/> | $29/mo | $79/mo | $299/mo | not listed |
| Online card, standard <C id="marketplace-shopify-03"/> | 2.9% + 30¢ | 2.7% + 30¢ | 2.5% + 30¢ | 2.25% + 30¢ |
| Online card, premium <C id="marketplace-shopify-04"/> | 3.5% + 30¢ | 3.3% + 30¢ | 3.1% + 30¢ | 2.95% + 30¢ |
| In-person card <C id="marketplace-shopify-05"/> | 2.6% + 10¢ | 2.5% + 10¢ | 2.4% + 10¢ | 2.3% + 10¢ |
| Third-party provider transaction fee <C id="marketplace-shopify-06"/> | 2% | 1% | 0.6% | 0.2% |
| Online international card <C id="marketplace-shopify-08"/> | +1% | +1% | +1% | +1% |

*Source: Shopify, Pricing page, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-shopify-03"/>

Read the table by row, not by column: the plan you choose fixes your monthly cost, but the row that applies to each order depends on how the buyer paid. A Basic store taking a mix of standard, premium and international cards will see three different percentages on the same day, which is why a single blended "Shopify rate" in a margin sheet can understate costs.

Two more charges show up for multichannel sellers and new stores. Synced marketplace orders above 50 a month cost 1%, up to $99 a month, on every plan <C id="marketplace-shopify-09"/>. New stores start with 3 days free, then $1 a month for 3 months <C id="marketplace-shopify-10"/>.

## Worked example (hypothetical): a $25 sale and a $100 sale

Inputs: one online order, no shipping or tax added, paid by card. Plan fees are excluded because they don't change per order.

| Scenario | Shopify fee on $25 | Shopify fee on $100 |
|---|---|---|
| Basic, standard card (2.9% + 30¢) | $1.03 | $3.20 |
| Basic, premium card (3.5% + 30¢) | $1.18 | $3.80 |
| Basic, standard international card (3.9% + 30¢) | $1.28 | $4.20 |
| Grow, standard card (2.7% + 30¢) | $0.98 | $3.00 |
| Advanced, standard card (2.5% + 30¢) | $0.93 | $2.80 |
| Plus, standard card (2.25% + 30¢) | $0.86 | $2.55 |
| Basic, third-party provider (2% Shopify fee only) | $0.50 | $2.00 |

*Source: rates from Shopify's Pricing page, checked Nov 8, 2026; arithmetic is ours, rounded to the cent.* <C id="marketplace-shopify-03"/> <C id="marketplace-shopify-04"/> <C id="marketplace-shopify-08"/> <C id="marketplace-shopify-06"/>

The 30¢ fixed charge is why small orders cost proportionally more: on Basic the total fee is about 4.1% of a $25 standard-card sale ($1.025) but 3.2% of a $100 sale. The third-party row is not a saving; it is only Shopify's share, and the outside provider's own processing fee comes on top <C id="marketplace-shopify-07"/>.

## Which plan: the fee-only break-even

Higher plans cost more per month but lower the card rate by 0.2 percentage points at each step from Basic to Grow and from Grow to Advanced <C id="marketplace-shopify-03"/>. Dividing the price gap by that rate gap gives the monthly online card sales at which the upgrade pays for itself on processing alone.

Plus sits outside this comparison because its price is a starting figure, "starts at $2,300 USD/mo", rather than a fixed list price, and no yearly price is shown <C id="marketplace-shopify-01"/>. Its standard online card rate of 2.25% + 30¢ is 0.25 points below Advanced <C id="marketplace-shopify-03"/>, which on its own would take very high monthly volume to offset a four-figure subscription; treat it as a quoted contract rather than a self-serve upgrade.

With a third-party provider, the Basic-to-Grow saving is 1% of sales, so yearly-billed Grow covers its extra $50 at $5,000 a month in sales ($50 ÷ 0.01), a much lower bar. These thresholds ignore plan features; they compare only the published fees.

## Common Shopify fee mistakes

| Mistake | What Shopify's pricing page says | Effect on a hypothetical order |
|---|---|---|
| Budgeting with the standard rate only | Premium cards cost more, e.g. 3.5% + 30¢ on Basic <C id="marketplace-shopify-04"/> | A $100 premium-card sale costs $3.80, not $3.20 |
| Forgetting international cards | Online international adds 1% <C id="marketplace-shopify-08"/> | A $100 international sale on Basic costs $4.20 |
| Assuming a third-party processor avoids Shopify fees | A Shopify transaction fee may still apply, by plan <C id="marketplace-shopify-07"/> | 2% on Basic: $2.00 per $100 before the provider's own fee |
| Ignoring marketplace sync charges | 1% on synced marketplace orders above 50 a month, up to $99 <C id="marketplace-shopify-09"/> | The monthly charge is capped at $99 no matter how many orders sync |
| Comparing plans by price alone | Card rates fall on higher plans <C id="marketplace-shopify-03"/> | At $40,000 a month, Grow saves $80 in card fees vs Basic |

The trial pricing is easy to underestimate as a cost of testing: 3 days free and then $1 a month for 3 months means a store can run for roughly three months for $3 in plan fees <C id="marketplace-shopify-10"/>, with card processing charged on any sales during that time at the normal rate for the plan. In-person sales are the other blind spot for online sellers who add pop-up or trade-show selling: the in-person rate on Basic is 2.6% + 10¢ <C id="marketplace-shopify-05"/>, so a $25 in-person sale costs $0.75 against $1.03 online.
