The fees on shopify.com/pricing are a monthly plan fee and a payment fee per order. On the Basic plan, that is $29 per month billed yearly or $39 billed monthly <C id="marketplace-shopify-02" />, plus 2.9% + 30¢ per online card payment through Shopify Payments <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" />. Higher plans cost more per month and charge lower card rates.

If you use a payment provider other than Shopify Payments, Shopify adds its own third-party transaction fee of 2% on Basic, falling to 0.2% on Plus <C id="marketplace-shopify-03" />, on top of whatever that provider charges.

## Plans and monthly prices

| Plan | Billed yearly | Billed monthly | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $29/mo | $39/mo | <C id="marketplace-shopify-02" /> |
| Grow | $79/mo | $105/mo | <C id="marketplace-shopify-04" /> |
| Advanced | $299/mo | $399/mo | <C id="marketplace-shopify-05" /> |
| Plus | from $2,300/mo | | <C id="marketplace-shopify-06" /> |

*Source: Shopify, Pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-shopify-02" />

The pricing page shows a trial offer of 3 days free, then $1/month for 3 months <C id="marketplace-shopify-07" />. Staff accounts differ by plan: Basic includes no additional staff accounts, Grow 5 additional, Advanced 15 additional and Plus unlimited <C id="marketplace-shopify-08" />.

## Payment rates per order

| Rate | Basic | Grow | Advanced | Plus | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Online, standard cards | 2.9% + 30¢ | 2.7% + 30¢ | 2.5% + 30¢ | 2.25% + 30¢ | <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" /> |
| Online, premium cards | 3.5% + 30¢ | 3.3% + 30¢ | 3.1% + 30¢ | 2.95% + 30¢ | <C id="marketplace-shopify-09" /> |
| In person, card reader | 2.6% + 10¢ | 2.5% + 10¢ | 2.4% + 10¢ | 2.3% + 10¢ | <C id="marketplace-shopify-10" /> |
| Third-party transaction fee | 2% | 1% | 0.6% | 0.2% | <C id="marketplace-shopify-03" /> |

*Source: Shopify, Pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-shopify-11" />

Shopify states that third-party transaction fees may apply when you use a third-party payment provider instead of Shopify Payments <C id="marketplace-shopify-11" />. That fee is Shopify's; the provider's own processing charges are separate and are not on Shopify's pricing page.

## Worked example: a hypothetical $25 order

Assumptions (hypothetical): one online order of $25.00 paid with a standard card, no shipping charged, no tax.

| Plan | Card rate | Fee on $25.00 | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 2.9% + 30¢ | $0.725 + $0.30 = $1.03 | <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" /> |
| Grow | 2.7% + 30¢ | $0.675 + $0.30 = $0.98 | <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" /> |
| Advanced | 2.5% + 30¢ | $0.625 + $0.30 = $0.93 | <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" /> |
| Plus | 2.25% + 30¢ | $0.5625 + $0.30 = $0.86 | <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" /> |
| Basic, third-party provider | 2% Shopify fee | $0.50, plus the provider's own fees | <C id="marketplace-shopify-03" /> |

*Worked example using rates from Shopify, Pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" />

The plan fee is fixed. Spread across orders, Basic billed yearly ($29) adds $0.29 per order at 100 orders a month and $2.90 per order at 10. That fixed cost is why per-order comparisons with marketplaces depend heavily on volume.

Moving from Basic to Grow lowers the standard online card rate by 0.2 percentage points, or $0.05 on a $25 order. Against a $50 difference in yearly-billed plan price ($79 minus $29), the card saving alone covers the gap at about 1,000 such orders a month. This is arithmetic only; the plans also differ in features listed on the pricing page.

## Card fee as a share of the order

The 30¢ fixed part makes the effective rate higher on small orders. Worked example (hypothetical): Basic plan, standard online card, Shopify Payments <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" />.

| Order total | 2.9% | Fixed | Card fee | Fee as % of order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $10.00 | $0.29 | $0.30 | $0.59 | 5.9% |
| $25.00 | $0.725 | $0.30 | $1.03 | 4.1% |
| $50.00 | $1.45 | $0.30 | $1.75 | 3.5% |
| $100.00 | $2.90 | $0.30 | $3.20 | 3.2% |

*Worked example using rates from Shopify, Pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-shopify-01" />

Premium cards cost 0.6 percentage points more on Basic (3.5% + 30¢) <C id="marketplace-shopify-09" />; on a $25 order that is $1.18 instead of $1.03.

## Third-party provider and in-person arithmetic

Worked example (hypothetical): a $25.00 order on each plan.

| Plan | Third-party transaction fee | Shopify fee on $25.00 | In-person card reader rate | In-person fee on $25.00 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 2% | $0.50 | 2.6% + 10¢ | $0.75 |
| Grow | 1% | $0.25 | 2.5% + 10¢ | $0.73 |
| Advanced | 0.6% | $0.15 | 2.4% + 10¢ | $0.70 |
| Plus | 0.2% | $0.05 | 2.3% + 10¢ | $0.68 |

*Worked example using rates from Shopify, Pricing, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-shopify-10" />

The third-party column is Shopify's fee only <C id="marketplace-shopify-03" />; the external provider's processing charge comes on top and is not shown. In-person amounts are rounded: on Grow, 2.5% of $25.00 is $0.625 plus $0.10, or $0.725, shown as $0.73; on Plus, $0.575 plus $0.10 is $0.675, shown as $0.68.

## Monthly versus yearly billing

The yearly-billed price is lower per month on each plan that shows both <C id="marketplace-shopify-02" /> <C id="marketplace-shopify-04" /> <C id="marketplace-shopify-05" />. Over twelve months, the difference is $120 on Basic ($39 minus $29, times 12), $312 on Grow ($105 minus $79, times 12) and $1,200 on Advanced ($399 minus $299, times 12). Check the pricing page's terms for what yearly billing commits you to before choosing it.
