On eBay.com, a typical sale costs 13.6% of the total amount of the sale (up to $7,500 per item) plus a per-order fee of $0.30 or $0.40 <C id="marketplace-ebay-01" /> <C id="marketplace-ebay-02" />. Listing is free for the first 250 listings each month; beyond that, most categories charge $0.35 per listing <C id="marketplace-ebay-03" /> <C id="marketplace-ebay-04" />. Several categories carry different final value fee rates, from 3% for heavy equipment to 15.3% for books, movies and music.

The detail that changes the arithmetic most: eBay's total amount of the sale includes shipping collected from the buyer and sales tax <C id="marketplace-ebay-05" />, so the 13.6% is applied to more than the item price.

## Insertion fees

| Item | Fee | |
|---|---|---|
| Zero insertion fee listings per month | Up to 250, or more with an eBay Store | <C id="marketplace-ebay-03" /> |
| Insertion fee after the allowance (most categories) | $0.35 per listing | <C id="marketplace-ebay-04" /> |
| Guitars & Basses | Free | <C id="marketplace-ebay-06" /> |
| Heavy Equipment, Commercial Printing Presses, Food Trucks, Trailers & Carts | $20 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-07" /> |

*Source: eBay, Selling fees (eBay Help), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-ebay-04" />

## Final value fees

The final value fee is a percentage of the total amount of the sale, which includes the item price, handling charges, shipping costs collected from the buyer and sales tax <C id="marketplace-ebay-05" />. A per-order fee is added: $0.30 for orders of $10.00 or less and $0.40 for orders over $10.00 <C id="marketplace-ebay-02" />.

| Category | Final value fee | |
|---|---|---|
| Most categories | 13.6% up to $7,500 per item; 2.35% on the portion over $7,500 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-01" /> |
| Books & Magazines, Movies & TV, Music | 15.3% up to $7,500 per item; 2.35% above | <C id="marketplace-ebay-08" /> |
| Coins & Paper Money (except Bullion), Comic Books & Memorabilia, Non-Sport and Sports Trading Cards, Collectible Card Games | 13.25% up to $7,500 per item; 2.35% above | <C id="marketplace-ebay-09" /> |
| Women's Bags & Handbags | 15% if the sale is $2,000 or less; 9% if over $2,000 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-10" /> |
| Jewelry & Watches (excluding Watches, Parts & Accessories) | 15% if the sale is $5,000 or less; 9% if over $5,000 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-11" /> |
| Watches, Parts & Accessories | 15% up to $1,000; 6.5% from $1,000 to $7,500; 3% over $7,500 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-12" /> |
| Guitars & Basses | 6.7% up to $7,500 per item; 2.35% above | <C id="marketplace-ebay-06" /> |
| Athletic Shoes | 8% with no per-order fee if the sale is $150 or more; 13.6% if under $150 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-13" /> |
| Heavy Equipment, Commercial Printing Presses, Food Trucks, Trailers & Carts | 3% up to $15,000; 0.5% above | <C id="marketplace-ebay-07" /> |

*Source: eBay, Selling fees (eBay Help), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-ebay-01" />

Note the two structures. "Up to $7,500... on the portion over" is marginal: only the amount above the threshold gets the lower rate. Women's Bags & Handbags, Jewelry & Watches and Athletic Shoes use an "if the sale is..." structure, where the whole sale takes one rate depending on its size.

## Other fees that can apply

- **International fee:** 1.65% of the total amount of the sale, in the cross-border cases eBay describes <C id="marketplace-ebay-14" />.
- **Below Standard surcharge:** sellers rated Below Standard pay an additional 6% on final value fees for sales in the following calendar month <C id="marketplace-ebay-15" />.

The Selling fees page also lists reserve price and other optional listing upgrades, real estate fees, a dispute fee and a seller currency conversion charge, which are outside the scope of this summary.

## Worked example: a hypothetical $25 item

Assumptions (hypothetical): one unit at a $25.00 item price, fixed-price listing within the free insertion allowance, US buyer and seller, Above Standard account.

| Scenario | Total amount of the sale | Final value fee | Per-order fee | Total eBay fee | |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Most categories, free shipping, no sales tax collected | $25.00 | 13.6% = $3.40 | $0.40 | $3.80 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-01" /> |
| Most categories, $5.00 shipping charged | $30.00 | 13.6% = $4.08 | $0.40 | $4.48 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-05" /> |
| Most categories, free shipping, $2.00 sales tax collected | $27.00 | 13.6% = $3.67 | $0.40 | $4.07 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-05" /> |
| Books & Magazines, free shipping, no tax | $25.00 | 15.3% = $3.83 | $0.40 | $4.23 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-08" /> |
| Guitars & Basses, free shipping, no tax | $25.00 | 6.7% = $1.68 | $0.40 | $2.08 | <C id="marketplace-ebay-06" /> |

*Worked example using rates from eBay, Selling fees (eBay Help), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-ebay-02" />

Amounts are rounded to the cent. If the listing were the 251st of the month in a standard category, add the $0.35 insertion fee <C id="marketplace-ebay-04" />, which is charged whether or not the item sells.

The sales-tax row matters for comparisons: on a $25 item, $2.00 of collected tax adds $0.27 of final value fee. Some other marketplaces exclude tax from their fee base, so equal headline percentages do not mean equal fees.

## How the fee scales with price

Worked example (hypothetical): items in a most-categories listing, free shipping, no sales tax collected, Above Standard account. The per-order fee is $0.30 at $10.00 or less and $0.40 above <C id="marketplace-ebay-02" />; the 13.6% rate applies up to $7,500 and 2.35% to the portion above <C id="marketplace-ebay-01" />.

| Total amount of the sale | Final value fee | Per-order fee | Total fee | Fee as % of sale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $10.00 | $1.36 | $0.30 | $1.66 | 16.6% |
| $25.00 | $3.40 | $0.40 | $3.80 | 15.2% |
| $100.00 | $13.60 | $0.40 | $14.00 | 14.0% |
| $1,000.00 | $136.00 | $0.40 | $136.40 | 13.6% |
| $10,000.00 | $1,020.00 + $58.75 = $1,078.75 | $0.40 | $1,079.15 | 10.8% |

*Worked example using rates from eBay, Selling fees (eBay Help), checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-ebay-01" />

The per-order fee is what pushes the effective rate above 13.6% at low prices: $0.30 is 3% of a $10 sale, while $0.40 is 0.04% of a $1,000 sale. At $10,000, only the first $7,500 is charged 13.6% ($1,020.00) and the remaining $2,500 is charged 2.35% ($58.75).

### The Athletic Shoes threshold

Athletic Shoes use a whole-sale threshold rather than a marginal one: 13.6% below $150, and 8% with no per-order fee at $150 or more <C id="marketplace-ebay-13" />. Worked example (hypothetical, no shipping or tax): a $149.00 pair pays 13.6% ($20.26) plus $0.40, or $20.66; a $150.00 pair pays 8%, or $12.00. The fee falls by $8.66 while the sale rises by $1.00.
