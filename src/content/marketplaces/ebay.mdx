eBay charges most sellers two things per sale: a **final value fee**, which is a percentage of the total amount of the sale, plus a **per-order fee** <C id="marketplace-ebay-01"/>. In most categories without a Store the percentage is 13.6% on the total up to $7,500 per item <C id="marketplace-ebay-04"/>, and the per-order fee is $0.30 for orders of $10.00 or less or $0.40 above that <C id="marketplace-ebay-03"/>. The "total amount of the sale" includes the item price, handling, shipping paid by the buyer (with some exceptions eBay lists), sales tax and other applicable fees <C id="marketplace-ebay-02"/>. Without a Store you get up to 250 zero insertion fee listings a month, then pay $0.35 per listing in most categories <C id="marketplace-ebay-15"/>.

## Final value fees by category (no Store)

Rates below apply to sellers without an eBay Store. "Per item" thresholds are applied to each item's total amount of the sale.

| Category | Final value fee |
|---|---|
| Most categories | 13.6% up to $7,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-04"/>; 2.35% on the portion over $7,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-05"/> |
| Books & Magazines, Movies & TV, Music (listed exceptions apply) | 15.3% up to $7,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-06"/> |
| Women's Bags & Handbags | 15% if the sale is $2,000 or less; 9% above $2,000 <C id="marketplace-ebay-07"/> |
| Jewelry & Watches (except Watches, Parts & Accessories) | 15% if the sale is $5,000 or less; 9% above $5,000 <C id="marketplace-ebay-08"/> |
| Watches, Parts & Accessories | 15% up to $1,000; 6.5% from $1,000 to $7,500; 3% above $7,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-09"/> |
| Coins & Paper Money (except Bullion) | 13.25% up to $7,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-10"/> |
| Trading cards, Collectible Card Games, Comic Books & Memorabilia | 13.25% up to $7,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-11"/> |
| Guitars & Basses | 6.7% up to $7,500; no insertion fee <C id="marketplace-ebay-12"/> |
| Men's and Women's Athletic Shoes | 8% at $150 or more, no per-order fee; 13.6% below $150 <C id="marketplace-ebay-13"/> |
| Heavy Equipment, Commercial Printing Presses, Food Trucks | 3% up to $15,000; 0.5% above; $20 insertion fee <C id="marketplace-ebay-14"/> |
| NFT categories | 5% <C id="marketplace-ebay-19"/> |

*Source: eBay Customer Service, "Selling fees" help page, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-ebay-01"/>

## Other fees that can apply to a sale

- **Insertion fees.** Beyond the free allowance, each listing costs $0.35 <C id="marketplace-ebay-15"/>. They are charged per listing and per category and are non-refundable if the item doesn't sell <C id="marketplace-ebay-16"/>.
- **International fee.** When it applies, it is 1.65% of the total amount of the sale <C id="marketplace-ebay-18"/>.
- **Below Standard surcharge.** An account rated Below Standard pays an additional 6% on final value fees for sales in the following calendar month <C id="marketplace-ebay-17"/>.

## eBay Store subscriptions

A Store changes both your listing allowance and, from Basic upward, your final value rate.

| Store | Monthly renewal | Yearly renewal (per month) |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $7.95 | $4.95 <C id="marketplace-ebay-20"/> |
| Basic | $27.95 | $21.95 <C id="marketplace-ebay-21"/> |
| Premium | $74.95 | $59.95 <C id="marketplace-ebay-22"/> |
| Anchor | $349.95 | $299.95 <C id="marketplace-ebay-23"/> |
| Enterprise | Not currently available | $2,999.95 <C id="marketplace-ebay-24"/> |

*Source: eBay Customer Service, "Store selling fees" help page, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-ebay-21"/>

With a Basic or higher Store, most categories pay 12.7% on the total up to $2,500 per item <C id="marketplace-ebay-25"/> and 2.35% on the portion over $2,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-26"/>. A Starter Store keeps the 13.6% rate up to $7,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-27"/>. A Basic Store also includes 1,000 zero insertion fee fixed-price listings a month in all categories, then $0.25 per listing <C id="marketplace-ebay-28"/>.

## Worked example (hypothetical): a $25 sale and a $100 sale

Inputs: one item per order, total amount of the sale of exactly $25 or $100 (no extra shipping or tax), no international fee, account at or above standard. Each fee is the category rate times the total, plus the per-order fee of $0.40 because both orders are over $10.00 <C id="marketplace-ebay-03"/>.

| Seller and category | Fees on a $25 sale | Fees on a $100 sale |
|---|---|---|
| No Store, most categories (13.6%) | $3.80 | $14.00 |
| Basic Store, most categories (12.7%) | $3.58 | $13.10 |
| No Store, Books & Magazines (15.3%) | $4.23 | $15.70 |
| No Store, trading cards (13.25%) | $3.71 | $13.65 |
| No Store, Guitars & Basses (6.7%) | $2.08 | $7.10 |

*Source: rates from eBay's Selling fees and Store selling fees help pages, checked Nov 8, 2026; arithmetic is ours, rounded to the cent.* <C id="marketplace-ebay-04"/> <C id="marketplace-ebay-25"/> <C id="marketplace-ebay-06"/> <C id="marketplace-ebay-11"/> <C id="marketplace-ebay-12"/>

<Callout type="example" title="Worked example (hypothetical): tax and shipping raise the fee base">
A $100 item shipped with $8.00 of sales tax collected has a total amount of the sale of $108.00. In most categories without a Store the fee is 13.6% × $108.00 = $14.69, plus $0.40, or $15.09, which is $1.09 more than the same sale with no tax. Buyer-paid shipping is added to the base the same way. <C id="marketplace-ebay-02"/> <C id="marketplace-ebay-04"/>

## Do you need a Store? The fee-only break-even

For items under $2,500 in most categories, a Basic Store saves 0.9 percentage points (13.6% minus 12.7%) of every sale's total. That is the number to weigh against the subscription.

## Common eBay fee mistakes

| Mistake | What eBay's fee page says | Effect on a hypothetical order |
|---|---|---|
| Ignoring the per-order fee on cheap items | $0.30 per order at $10.00 or less <C id="marketplace-ebay-03"/> | On a $5 sale, $0.30 is 6% of the price on top of 13.6% |
| Leaving tax and shipping out of the base | Total amount includes shipping, handling and sales tax <C id="marketplace-ebay-02"/> | A $40 item with $8 shipping is charged on $48 at least |
| Assuming any Store lowers the rate | Starter stays at 13.6% up to $7,500 <C id="marketplace-ebay-27"/> | No final value saving from a Starter Store |
| Expecting insertion fees back on unsold items | Non-refundable if the item doesn't sell <C id="marketplace-ebay-16"/> | 100 extra listings at $0.35 cost $35 whether or not they sell |
| Missing seller-level surcharges | Below Standard adds 6% on final value fees the next month <C id="marketplace-ebay-17"/> | A full month of sales carries the surcharge, so one bad evaluation affects every order the following month |

The per-order fee is the one most often left out of quick margin math. At $25 it adds 1.6% of the sale; at $100 it adds 0.4%; at $8 it adds $0.30, or 3.75%, because orders of $10.00 or less pay the lower $0.30 fee rather than $0.40 <C id="marketplace-ebay-03"/>. Low-priced items carry a heavier total fee rate than the headline category percentage suggests, so it is worth pricing small items against the combined figure rather than the rate alone.

This is a reading of eBay's US help pages for fees, not tax advice. Your seller account's fee invoice shows what was actually charged on each order; if a charge differs from the published rate, that invoice and eBay's own dispute route are where it gets resolved.
</Callout>
