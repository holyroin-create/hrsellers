A US-based business can sell on AliExpress under a dedicated US contract: your agreement is with **AliExpress International (United States) Corporation** <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-01"/>, which also collects the commission <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-05"/>. What that contract does not do is print a commission rate. It says the rate is whatever AliExpress announces from time to time <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-04"/>, so check the announced rate for your category before you price. This page sets out what the public US documents do say.

## The documents that make up the US seller contract

AliExpress's service agreement for sellers from the United States (marked "Updated on 22th July, 2024") <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-02"/> sits on top of a set of documents that it calls, together, the Platform User Agreements <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-06"/>.

| Document | What it governs for a US seller |
|---|---|
| AliExpress Service Agreement for Sellers from the United States | Commission, deposit deductions, rule changes, termination, New York governing law <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-03"/> <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-18"/> |
| AliExpress Rules for U.S. Platform Sellers | Stated by the agreement to form part of it <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-06"/> |
| AliExpress.com Free Membership Agreement (updated Aug 26, 2026; effective Sep 26, 2026) | Contracting entity, registration information, listing limits, Proposition 65, recalls <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-26"/> |
| AliExpress.com Terms of Use | AliExpress's role in transactions and seller responsibility for product compliance <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-27"/> |

*Source: AliExpress US seller service agreement, Free Membership Agreement and Terms of Use, checked Nov 8, 2026.* <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-06"/>

## Money: commission, deposit and refunds on exit

**Commission.** You agree to pay commission in accordance with the Platform User Agreements <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-03"/>, at the rate AliExpress announces from time to time <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-04"/>. AliExpress International (United States) Corporation collects it in its own name <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-05"/>.

**Membership.** The Free Membership Agreement offers the service free of charge for an unspecified period unless terminated <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-20"/>, and AliExpress reserves the right to charge for it <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-21"/>. "Free" here describes membership, not selling: commission still applies <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-03"/>.

**Deposit.** If a seller's breach causes AliExpress losses, AliExpress is entitled to deduct first from the deposit the seller has made, and the seller pays any difference <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-07"/>.

**Exit.** After deregistration or termination, any payment that has to be reimbursed is paid within 180 days from order shipment <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-13"/>.

## Control: rule changes, pricing conditions and termination

AliExpress reserves the right to establish or modify the platform's rules at any time <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-08"/>, with notice at least 15 days in advance unless exceptional circumstances apply <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-09"/>. If you disagree with a change, the agreement's answer is to stop using the services <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-10"/>. You also authorize AliExpress to adjust the commercialization conditions of your products as needed to match promotions you have joined <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-19"/>, and AliExpress may limit how many product listings a member can post <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-24"/>.

## Responsibility: delivery, transactions and compliance

AliExpress positions itself outside the sale. It is not involved in the actual transaction between sellers and buyers <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-14"/>, does not represent either side <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-27"/>, and is not responsible for obligations arising from the commercial transactions; delivery, product condition and suitability sit with the seller <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-15"/>. The two governing-law clauses are worth reading side by side: the US seller service agreement is governed by New York law <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-18"/>, while the general Terms of Use choose the law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region for users contracting with the entities named there <C id="marketplace-aliexpress-29"/>. Which one controls a given dispute is a question for the documents themselves and, if it matters, for counsel.
